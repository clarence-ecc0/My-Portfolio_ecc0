Topic&#58; *Identifying False Prophets in Our Midst* (Part 7, Matthew, 7&#58;16)

🗣️Let us talk about two more things introduced in the Church by Church leaders whose main exhibition is materialism, the mark of falsehood.

1️⃣ *Offering Time, Blessing Time.* This expression which is always echoed when the Church calls for offerings, seems to connote that *"Everything that goes on in the Church has no blessings following it, except when one gives offerings in the Church.* 

🗣️This is another coinage that Prosperity preachers introduced into the Church to make Church look like *Business Centres.* A Muslim cleric in Kaduna says, *"80 to 90% of Churches in Nigeria are Business Centres."* With the activities going on in some modern Churches, and their unprecedented  focus on currency and riches, this Muslim Cleric is justified.

🗣️ *Churches whose pastors use working hours* for programmes should be called to order  by both CAN and PFN. *Working hours are very vital for the blessings members ask for.* Unfortunately, this vital periods are highjacked by greedy Church leaders who want to make more money. 

🗣️I equally blame the members who are so deceived, that God would send money from above to them. *These are lazy oeople, looking for money without work.* The Bible says _"Anyone who does not work should not eat."_ *Stop looking for unlaboured money, it is unrealistic.* It is a deceit.

🗣️A Church beside where I stay, has a programme every Thursday, from 7 AM to 7 PM. Within this period of 12 hours, the pastor calls for more that 10 offerings. *This is a Business Centre in action.* This is not the Church the Lord bequeathed to us.

2️⃣ *Acts 19&#58;1-6.* Many Church leaders have been using this passage *to justify their action of calling people out to receive the Holy Spirit.* This passage does not call for that.

🗣️ *First, it is preponderant to mention here that the Book of Acts is not a doctrinal book.* The occasion here is *a phenomenon of being baptized without accepting Christ in one's life.* The men in question were baptized into the baptism of John, they never knew Jesus and the Holy Spirit, they only knew about John.

🗣️Apollos himself, the leader of these men, was an expert in the Old Testament, that is why, Theologians believe, it is either he or Barnabas who wrote the Book of Hebrews. It was Priscilla and Aquila who let him to Christ.

🗣️What happened in Acts 19&#58;1-6 was one of the phenomena that took place during the *Confirmation Period* of the Church. Remember, Jesus Himself says that *His disciples would be His witness in Jerusalem, Samaria, Judea and the uttermost part of the it.* The experience of Acts 2 (Jerusalem), happened in Samaria, Caesarea (Cornelius), and now in Ephesus, making Jesus' statement come to reality.

🗣️ *So, the Holy Spirit is received at conversion,* He does not come into people's life through laying on of hands, at a period after conversion, (Ephesians 1,2). God  seals one with the Holy Spirit when one repents. *The HOLY SPIRIT is the mark of ownership placed on believers.*
