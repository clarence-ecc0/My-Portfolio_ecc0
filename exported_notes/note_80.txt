TOPIC&#58; CHRIST COMPASSION AND CONCERN FOR THE FAINTING AND THE FORGETFUL 

MARK 8&#58;1-9;
Our compassion for the spiritual needs of the people should extend to the needs of the flesh -food, clothing, visitation, etc. This is the way of Christ. Pray to God to give you so that you can give others. Even the little that you have (the disciples had only 7 loaves and there were about 4000 people to feed), you can pray on it and the miracle of multiplication can still happen today for you and your gift in Jesus name.  


Point-1&#58; His compassion for fainting souls.
MARK 8&#58;1-2; Mathew 9&#58;36-38; Mark 6&#58;34
For three full days, Christ was holding a retreat, teaching the people and no food was given to them. Compassion must move us to teach the people many things. Compassion should also move us to see their basic needs of the flesh. Christ spent three days teaching people, messages after messages. This is required of us and it requires much preparation.

Mathew 14&#58;14; Mark 1&#58;40-42; Micah 7&#58;18,19; Deuteronomy 30&#58;2-3,6; Luke 15&#58;18-19,20
Compassion also made Jesus to heal the people. It caused Him to feed the people, teach the people, and show mercy to people. 
It is the Lord's miracle of mercy and grace that enables us to be cleansed and acceptable to God. 

Point-2&#58; The condemnation of faithless sign-seekers.
John 9&#58;23, 30-35; 11&#58;47-52; 12&#58;10-11; 15&#58;22-25; 20&#58;30-31
The Pharisees wanted to destroy the evidence of the power of Christ over death (power to resurrect  the dead) by killing Lazarus. When people learnt that Lazarus that died four days ago and was buried was brought back to life, they came, saw him and believed that Jesus was indeed the Christ, the Son of God and Savior of the whole world. The Pharisees whom many thought were learning of God to show the people when Christ eventually came saw Him, recognized Him but rejected the rich evidence of His presence. Satan did a good job of preparing the Pharisees for the coming of Christ. They could not stand the presence of Christ. 
Malachi said it all&#58; "But who may abide the day of his coming? and who shall stand when he appeareth? for he is like a refiner's fire, and like fullers' soap&#58;".  


Point-3&#58; The caution against false shepherds.
Mark 8&#58;14-21; Mathew 16&#58;7-12
We are to avoid the corrupting influence of false messages from unsanctified preachers. It is a leaven that will rise and corrupt the whole person - body soul and spirit. The demonic preachers are demonizing people that go to them as they initiate and consecrate them unto Satan. 
Some of the things that Satan demands from his servants (slaves) is immorality, defilement, etc. Some cults require immoral practice to powerfully possess people for Satan. Avoid all the pornographic things and escape Satan. 

Mathew 24&#58;24-25; 2 Thessalonians 2&#58;9-12; 1 Timothy 4&#58;1,2; 2 Peter 2&#58;1-3, 18-22; Jude 1&#58;4-7
Many of the servants of Satan will combine wonders with lies to deceive people and bring them over to their master. We need to be watchful.
