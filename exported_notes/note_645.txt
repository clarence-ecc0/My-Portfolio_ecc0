            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _Our Confidence in Troublous Time_

"_For the Egyptians shall help in vain, and to no purpose&#58; therefore have I cried concerning this, Their strength is to *sit still.*_"
 *Isaiah 30&#58;7* 

[15&#93;For thus saith the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel; In *returning* and *rest* shall ye be saved; in *quietness* and in *confidence* shall be your strength&#58; and ye would not.
 *Isaiah 30&#58;15*

The two verses above from the book of Isaiah made mention of some words or used some expressions that are quite significant in our relationship with the Lord- 

'sit still'
'returning'
'rest'
'confidence'

We also see expressions such as&#58;
'their strength '
'shall ye be saved'
'shall be your strength '

When you join the two verses together and you reflect on all these words/expressions, you will come to this conclusion&#58;

In the face of all the commotion around us -the political instability, the threat of war and unrestrained  economic meltdown, our strength, our salvation is tied to our&#58;

Returning to the Lord from all forms of idols and then, 
*sitting still* (a state of *quietness*) and be untroubled by the sights we see and the noise we hear, but 
reposing *confidence* in the Lord. This will be our strength in the sight of God.

How do we return to the Lord?

We take down every confidence we have reposed in our coverings, umbrella, shade from adverse weather, (idols) 

You see, in Isaiah 30&#58;1, the Lord complained of the false covering of Israel but in 30&#58;22-..., He gave an admonition on what they ought to do

Isaiah 28&#58;20 has also told us the foolishness of trusting in idols

What is an idol?
An idol is beyond what we have always known them to be.
Anything that becomes your confidence, your deliverer - your knowledge, your good look, your connection (your Egypt in Isaiah 30&#58;2) your education, skills, learnings, etc. These good things become idols to you the day you declare in your heart that, "my own is finished if I lose this asset that has made me great, made me to be desired".

These good things cannot make us great by themselves; they require God to bless them for them to make us truly great. (the Lord said, I will bless the work of your hands Deuteronomy is full of this promise). We know many who are our equal in the assets we have who are not faring well, and we know many who do have less than what we have and are doing better than us. God's blessings are paramount.

Please, have your education, skills, etc but let your confidence not be in them, do not make them your coverings, your idols, your hope.

May your confidence remain in God, not in your assets; in your Benefactor, not in the benefits. The benefits are good but they are not our deliverer, our God.

May the peace of the Lord reign in your heart in this Troublous time as you understand and accept this in Jesus name.

Shalom.
