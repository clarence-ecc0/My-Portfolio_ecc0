{{text:*Theme&#58;* The prayer of a new testament believer over his enemies.

_"Mine eye also shall see my desire on mine enemies, and mine ears shall hear my desire of the wicked that rise up against me."_  *Psalms 92&#58;11*

What will the prayer of a new testament believer be over his enemies as Christ commanded us? If Christ should be me, what will His prayer be?

[A&#93;. Let what they plan for me happen to them.

[B&#93;. Let God's judgement for the wicked happens now, that they and others may know that they are touching the apple of God's eye.

[C&#93;. Oh Lord, give them the type of heart that you gave to me, that they may become my brethren and no longer my enemies.

Only a gracious, sanctified believer can pray option *'C'* prayer. The flesh will definitely ask for *'A'* or,  *'B'* or, *'BOTH'*

_May the good Lord help us in Jesus name._
_May He take us FROM THE POINT where our flesh is in charge and His Spirit in us is striving to take over, TO THE POINT where His Spirit in us is in charge and the flesh is the one striving to take over. _"The wrath of man [no matter its cause&#93; DOES NOT work the righteousness of God"_  *[James 1&#58;20&#93;*

May our flesh consistently be the loser up to the moment that our job here on earth is done and we are being switched over to the other side of eternity, in a moment of time in Jesus name. 

*Shalom*
