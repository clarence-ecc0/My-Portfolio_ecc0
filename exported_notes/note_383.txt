TOPIC&#58; GRACEFUL GODLINESS AND CONTENTMENT......
Godliness with contentment is a great gain but contentment without godliness is a great loss

Point-1&#58; Godly contentment with essential raiments and food

1 Timothy 6&#58;6-8; Mathew 6&#58;31-32; Deuteronomy 8&#58;3-4, 18
Yes, we will not carry any material thing from earth to heaven but it is certain that we will carry our sanctification, righteousness and purity back to heaven,we are not leaving them here on earth. 
When we major in the minor things (things that satisfy the body such as clothes, travel abroad, education, etc) and minors on the major things (spiritual nourishment) are not wise. We must seek first the kingdom of God (major thing) and the minor things will be added unto us. Take note that the Bible did not say God will add unto us in the measure the people of the world have but He will add unto us what is appropriate and in the right measure. Food and raiments are good if in the appropriate measure.
God can permit us to hunger for training (science has proven that fasting is good for the body). Everything the Lord did for Israel in the wilderness was appropriate for their needs (pillar of clouds against the heat in the day and pillar of fire to drive reptiles and deadly animals away at night).

Luke 12&#58;15-21.
It is good for us to possess things but it is not good for us for the things to possess us. When we keep accumulating wealth and people around us are suffering, we are already possessed by the wealth. 
Unlike the rich fool, we have enough room to keep our stuff. The rooms of our penniless neighbors, brethren and relations are rooms for us to discharge the abundance we have. The rich fool had three compartments (body, soul and spirit) and he provided only for the body and ignored the Spirit and the soul. That is a fool. So rich physically but spiritually, he is penniless 

Point-2&#58; Grievous consequences of evil roots in focus

1 Timothy 6&#58;9-10; Genesis 13&#58;10-13; 2 Peter 2&#58;15-17 
We can have money without loving it excessively. Money is profitable to take care of ourselves but when a man loves money so much that he can ignore his health, soul, study of God's word, it destroys the soul. When we have material things in focus so much, we become blinded to spiritual things. Balam was so engrossed with the thought of Balak's wages of unrighteousness that he could not see the angel that his Ass could see. He became blind.

Point-3&#58; Growing consecration for experiential.... of faith

1 Timothy 6&#58;11-12
We must always ask, is this abundance of money from the Lord, a blessing that maketh rich and added no sorrow or, it is from Balak that brings sorrow and death?  We must flee every appearance of evil. 
The greatest fight we have to fight is to fight 'SELF." After hearing God's word over and over, there is still something inside us that is so strong and draws us away from God. This is what it means to fight the good fight of faith. We fight anything that wants to relegate our faith to the background. Take note that God will supply all our needs but not all our wants. 
All we have professed before many witnesses (I am saved, Sanctified, I am on my way to heaven, etc.), we must fight for them, against every pull in us that is drawing us away from our eternal goal. Elisha fought the pull within that wanted Naman's wealth but unfortunately, Gehazi could not. 

Mathew 5&#58;6-9
Righteousness, heart purity are among things that we should fight for.

I = Integrity despite bodily weakness
