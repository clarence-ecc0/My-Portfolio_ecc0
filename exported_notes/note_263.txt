{{text:TOPIC&#58; PROFITABLE REHEARSAL OF LIFE EXPERIENCES FOR THE BENEFIT OF OUR SUCCESSES IN LIFE AND MINISTRY 

Deuteronomy 1&#58;1,9,10,13-18; 3&#58;23-28
We can't build a church (even our family) alone all by ourselves. We need God. 

Point-1&#58; The prayer of Moses a mentor and servant par excellence 

Deuteronomy 3&#58;

a. The servanthood of Moses
Hebrews 3&#58; 2,5
The testimonies of Moses' faithfulness was for us to learn to that it is possible for us to be faithful as well.


b. Saints as the Savior's model
Hebrews 4&#58;14
Christ said, "as the Father sent Him (with all enabling grace, power, etc), so sent I you." He has sent us therefore,  we too can succeed.


c. Supplications before selection of ministers 
Numbers 27&#58; 15-19
What kind of prayer will a man pray after God has told him his ministry is about to end. Here, we see God telling Moses in Numbers 27&#58;12-14 that he will not go to where he desires so much, Canaan. There is a better place than the physical Canaan that God wanted Moses to reach as He was not permitted to reach the physical Canaan. Do not allow that unfulfilled dream to cause you to act, speak to God, or pray a prayer that will disallow you from entering the better land. Moses did just that. He was neither offended at Israel nor charging them for making him to miss Canaan. This is an example for us. May God help me to raise a man that can takeover from me when my time is fully spent in Jesus name. 

Luke 6&#58;12-16
Even Christ had to pray to His Father before He could appoint His apostles. In the ministry, nobody is permitted to appoint people into ministry without prayer.
Christ ordained twelve to be with Him (intimate relationship for mentoring) to understudy Him.


Point-2&#58;  Preparation and mentoring through sharing of experiences 

Mark 3&#58;13-15
a. Personal persuasion for service with the Master
Psalms 116&#58;16-17; Romans 4&#58;21; 8&#58;38-39; 2 Timothy 1&#58;12
Abraham, the friend of God was fully persuaded. Even old age could not cause to begin to doubt that God was faithful. Paul knew whom he believed and thus despite all that he suffered, he was fully persuaded that all he committed to God would be kept secured.


b. Purposeful preparation for stewardship by a Mentor
The Bible expects us to have mentors. 

Numbers 27&#58;18-19
There must be a man whom God has appointed for us to guide us in the way that we should go. This was what was done for Joshua. 
God always put honor upon individuals He chose for His service. It was on Moses and it was to be transferred to Joshua as well. 

Joshua 1&#58;1-3; Genesis 41&#58;38
The word minister means servant. There is nothing degrading concerning that. Some members will give money and goods to their pastor to please him but will not sit down to take counsel or accept instructions. There is no wisdom and thus no gain in that.
Joseph, in prison and his brothers was under the mentorship of God Himself, allowing him to go through challenges in life that prepared him for later service. 


c. Preaching and praying while shepherding a ministry
Any mentoring that doesn't include preaching and praying is defective and not acceptable. 

Mathew 9&#58;35-38; 1 Thessalonians 5&#58;17-18
It matters to God what we see with our eyes, and what we do with what we see. When we see people in their multitudes coming out of their temples and churches, we should ask, where are they going, to heaven or hell? God expects us to do something. It requires that we pray to God and then to preach unto the people. 
Whatever I am going through, as long as I am a Christian, that is the will of God (not of Satan) for me; there is a lesson God what's me to learn. The next thing I need to do in that situation, is to give thanks to the Lord so that His will be done in Jesus name. 


Point-3&#58;  The preaching and ministration of successors without self-exaltation

Preachers and pastors are not lords over God's heritage. They are servants. Their lives will be exemplary. Paul said, be ye followers of me as I am a follower of Christ.

Numbers 27&#58;21-23
Leaders should take counsel from their priests (pastor) no matter their levels. They should not despise him. That's what God commanded Joshua should do, taking counsel from Eleazar.
The charge from Moses to Joshua must be as "the Lord commanded Moses".

a. The dominion and ability of Christ
The dominion of Christ is what He transferred unto us. Christ in us is the hope of glory. Acts 19&#58;38


b. Delegated authority of Christians through Christ 

Luke 10&#58;19; John 15&#58;16
The authority of Christ has been delegated unto me as a Christian. We are his and like Him,men and women of authority. Those who go out at Christ command, whatever they ask of God according to His will, He will grant unto them. We must minister without exalting 'Self' and this will be our portion.


c. Demonstration of dominion by ambassadors of Christ.
Dominion is not by mouth but is manifested for people to see.
Mark 16&#58;19-20
When I go out, the Lord will be working with me, confirming His word that I speak in Jesus name.
