TOPIC&#58; What next?

1. After establishing your dream (my job, building, degrees, etc.), what next?

2 After a deliberate disobedience to abstain yourself from the fellowdhip activities, what next?

3. After giving your best to God's projects, what next; after you tell people not to give, what next?

4. After the glorious celebration (e.g., wedding, naming ceremony, birthday celebration, etc.), what next? 

5. After all that you have done that brought you to the level that you are at, what next?

6. As you look forward to the future coming of Christ, what next?

7. After death, what next? Will you be justified or condemned?
