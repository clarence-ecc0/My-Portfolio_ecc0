{{text:TOPIC&#58; FULL SALVATION BY GRACE THROUGH FAITH FOR GLORY
Out of the abundance of salvation in the heart, the mouth speaks, the legs walk, the eyes look, the ears listen, etc. 

When the heart has full salvation, all the members of the body will be affected - the hand can't commit its sins, the mouth can't speak its evil words, the legs can't go to the shrine again, etc. 


Point-1&#58; Foretold salvation through the pardoning Savior by grace
Our salvation was foretold by the Lord. All the water that I will drink from birth to death, all the air that I will breath, all the vegetables that I will need, etc., God provided all before I was born. All that Adam and Eve would need in the garden were created before they were created. My salvation was created for me before I was born.

Titus 2&#58;11-14; Luke 19&#58;5-10; Romans 10&#58;8-10
When there is no salvation in the heart, there is nothing to transfer good sense to the head. Salvation in the heart helps us to live righteously and godly.
It is the salvation in the heart that permits us to look for that "blessed hope". We have hope because we are saved.

Our salvation makes us magnetizable by Christ when He appears in the sky for rapture. This is why He gave Himself for us, He is in us. 
The Lord has made us peculiar people. Because we are peculiar, failure cannot visit us as he visits all the people around us. The Lord will make us peculiar in Jesus name 


Point-2&#58; Full salvation by the perfect Savior for godliness 

Luke 1&#58;74-77; Acts 4&#58;12
Part of our package of salvation is to be able to "serve Him without fear'. Why Should I fear when the Lord knows my upstanding and downseating.

Anywhere we live, we will share the knowledge of salvation with others in Jesus name.


Point-3&#58; Final salvation in the powerful Savior before glory
That is the final salvation that transforms and translates us to heaven.

The Lord Jesus is with the Father and He has asked the Father to write my name in the book of life. I will get to glory

2 Timothy 2&#58;10
