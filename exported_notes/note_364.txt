Message by GS

TOPIC&#58; JESUS, THE ALMIGHTY ADVOCATE WITH ALL AUTHORITY 

1 John 2&#58;1-3
Advocates plead on behalf of people, to be set free. Christ came as a heavenly advocate and because He has transforming power, He worked in us so that we do not fall into the same error again.

Revelations 1&#58;7-8
He was, is and is to come.

Point-1&#58; The prevailing authority of Almighty Sovereign 

Mathew 28&#58;18-20
We are not to go into the world to teach people about Satan, about what the evil spirits (devils) can do. The more they do that, the more the people are made afraid. We are to go and teach people to observe all that Christ has commanded us to do. We are to teach, not to entertain people. When the message has ended, there must be something that the audience must observe/obey (restitutions, forgiveness, reconciliation, etc.) as they leave the assembly. 

Like 4&#58;18-19,20-21,32,36
Christ came with the authority to deliver people that are captive to the spirit of womanizing, telling lies, cheating in deals, etc.
Christ never pleaded with evil spirits to come out; He always commanded them to come out. 

Mathew 8&#58;8-9; 1 Corinthians 15&#58;24
Jesus gets to us as His message is being preached unto us. The preacher is only Christ's microphone, he is not the doer of the miracles. 
When people have insanity, depression, misfortunes, bad character, etc. there is another power reigning in their body. When Christ takes over a life, He suspends all the reigning powers and takes over.


Point-2&#58; The perpetual ability of the always-able Savior

Men can only help at certain times and in certain situations. For Christ, there is no limit in time or in situations that Christ can address. 

Hebrews 6&#58;25-27; 4&#58;15-
Christ ever liveth to make intercessions for us; that's what He lives for. Whatever temptations we have or challenges we faced, somebody else has had that same trial, challenges before. 

Acts 20&#58;32
Many people coming to church and hearing the messages constantly are not being built up. The way they react to issues today is the same way they reacted to similar issues 20 years ago. 

Ephesians 3&#58;20-21
Anytime we come before Christ, we are coming before Him that is able to do far greater than we can ever think or expect of Him. He has a power that worketh in us, not in those that frighten us. Through that power, what we desire in our heart can be achieved. 

Jude 1&#58;24-25; Philippians 3&#58;10-17,21-22
Christ is able to keep us from falling into the hands of the wicked, into sickness, by the wayside, before Satan, into sin, etc. 

When Paul said that he had not already attained, he did not mean attaining salvation, sanctification or Holy Ghost baptism, no; he had already attained those ones. But he knew that there is still more than what he has already attained, and that he was pressing towards the higher callings of God. He also said as many of us as are already perfect meaning there are areas where he is already perfect salvation, sanctification, and Holy Ghost baptism.

Point-3&#58; The permanent authorization of the acknowledged sons
Christ came to show us how the perfect man should live. He came to make the sons and daughters of men live as the Son of God. He authorizes us to live and work like Him.

Luke 9&#58;1-2,6; 10&#58;19
Christ authorized His disciples (sons of God) to have the power of devils and diseases. We should live our lives using that power, not wait to call the SO, RO or group pastor. I have to use my own power that Christ gave me in Luke 9&#58;1. I have the name of Jesus and His power and will live my life using them. Sicknesses and demons will bow before me in Jesus name. I am sent forth to preach the kingdom of God and to heal the sick. I am going to use that authority and power in Igogoro and Ofante this year in Jesus name. The enemies of our lives are nothing. We are the ones magnifying them ignorantly. If we read Luke 10&#58;19 every morning before we go out, and before we go to bed every night, the enemy cannot hurt us by any means. You are traveling to the village, you open Luke 10&#58;19 and read it before setting off, nothing of the enemy can by any means hurt you.
