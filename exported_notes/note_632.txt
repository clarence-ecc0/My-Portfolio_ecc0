The Law of exigency says, the end justifies the means.

The law of excellency says&#58;
✓ Stand alone if you must
✓ Do not accommodate failure. Joshua failed against Ai and went before God (the promise keeper) to find out why.
✓ Serve others passionately and leave your rewards alone. It will come later as we saw in Joseph
