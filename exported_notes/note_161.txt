{{text:TOPIC&#58; GRACIOUS TRANSFORMATION OF GODLY TRAVELLERS FOR A GLORIOUS TRANSLATION.

Roman 12&#58;1-2; Mathew 25&#58;13-14; Hebrews 11&#58; 5-
Are you a member of a Bible believing-church, be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind.
Show me a man who wants to reach heaven, a church that wants its members to reach heaven&#58; they single themselves out. Whatever others believe or preach, still, they single themselves out. Others may but I cannot. A heaven bound believer must separate himself from the multitude that is going to hell.
Some people (preachers) want to be found, accepted everywhere. There are some places (inglorious , despicable places) that heaven-bound believers cannot be found. Enoch was of that sort and by God's grace, I am of that sort in Jesus name. I will live my life to please God as Enoch did. 


Point-1&#58; The glorious transformation of guilty transgressors
I owed a debt I could not pay and Christ paid a debt He did not owe. By this, He took the guilt away from me. 

a. The reconciliation and gracious transformation of sinners
2 Corinthians 5&#58;18-20; 1 Corinthians 7&#58;9
God does not change. I turned my back on God by my sins and He could not turn around to cause me to face Him. Worse still, I couldn't change myself to face Him. Jesus Christ came and turned me around such that my face now is towards God. He reconciled me unto God.


b. The restoration and gracious transformation of backslidders
Psalms 51&#58; 1-2, 12
Backsliders are people that slide back from God.
They are not many Nathans in the pulpit today. They respect many people including fathers and mothers in the Lord. 
Backsliders pray the prayer of David in Psalms 51&#58;1-2
What is your joy when your name is out of the book of life? A backslider that is in joyful mood and spirit is unconscious of his state and eternal doom.

c. The repentance and gracious transformation of a city
A family and a whole city can repent and turn into the Lord
Jonah 3&#58;1-7
Jonah had a definite command to go and preach to the people, the preaching that God bidded him. He did not come to excite the people, entertain them, etc. A preacher that preaches smooth things to sinners is not preaching the preaching that God bidded him. I will always preach that message that God has bidded me to preach. 
In heaven, God sees our works and acts on them. Christ also mentioned this in the book of Revelations in the letter to the churches. 

Point-2&#58; The godly travellers with growing thirst

a. The good thirst of pilgrims for God and godliness 
Psalms 63&#58;1-2; Mathew 5&#58; 6-8; Psalms 

b. The growing thoughtfulness and passion for glory 
They will not take anything that will take heaven away from them. They are thoughtful of all their deeds, thoughts and utterances. They are not people that go to places without considering how it will impact their reaching heaven. For a real child of God, everyday sees his separation from the world system lengthening.

Phillipians 4&#58;5-7,8
There must be moderation in our celebrations, our dressing, utterances, etc. 

c. The great thoughts and plans of God for the godly 
Jeremiah 29&#58;11
 God's thoughts concerning me is not to reach the greatest heights here on earth and then descend to the lowest pit in hell. God's thoughts concerning my expected end is to reach heaven and partake of the glory. I must match my thoughts and plans with the thoughts and plans of God.


Point-3&#58; The glorious translation before the great tribulations 
Hebrews 11&#58;5
God took Enoch away (Genesis 5) before the flood came in Genesis 6. Noah and his family entered the ark before the flood came and took the sinners away into sorrowful eternity.
In the same token, we are going to be translated before the great tribulations. It is to ensure we do not see the death of the great tribulations as Enoch did not see it. 

a. The glorious translation for the transformed 

Hebrews 11&#58;5-6; Luke 18&#58;8b; 
Everywhere Enoch went - in the market, with the family, etc- unrighteousness was there. He was lonely in the community and yet was not lonely because God was walking with God. 

b. The great tribulations for the transgressors
All church people who are not really not saved, all deeper life by name who are really shallow life, all publicly holy people but who are privately unholy people, will be in that great tribulations.

Mathew 24&#58;21-22
c. The glowing trophy for the Triumphants.
1 Corinthians 9&#58;24-27; 2 Timothy 4&#58;7-8; James 1&#58;
Paul fought a good fight - against that Pharisees that would want to bring him back into Pharisees method by force or by subtle methods. Even older Christians before him who were going contrary to the will of Christ, he fought against wrong doctrines and practices (e.g., Peter).
Have we kept our Christian Life deeper? Have we done this in our place of work, family, community, in our transactions? May the Lord help me. 

A believer at the point of death should not have doubt of where he will end up. Paul was not in doubt over where he would end up. We should live our lives  like Paul
