{{text:*WHO NEED THE LIGHT MOST?*
Maurice Freehill posed the question, "who is more foolish, the child afraid of the dark or the man afraid of the light?"
Certainly, there is something wrong with the man who is afraid of light. Sadly, many men are. They are afraid of the light that comes from God's word, principally because it exposes their cherished evil deeds and rouses their conscience. But heaven-bound people delight in having God's word. 

*_"It is a pity that those who need the light most shun it most"_*
