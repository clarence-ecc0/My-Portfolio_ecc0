{{text:_An unshakeable assurance from the Holy Ghost_

_" Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence to make your calling and election sure&#58; for if ye do these things, *ye shall never fall*"_. 
*2Peter 1&#58;10*

Please, give a focused attention to the phrase, *"ye shall never fall"*
As Bible students, we know that the Holy Ghost is the author of whatever that is written in the Holy Scriptures. 

The Bible reference above makes an audacious statement that *we can never fail in our pilgrimage journey to heaven,* if we allow certain characters traits to be part of our lives. 

The characters are mentioned in *2Peter1&#58;5-7*
Let me list them here for you.

1. Faith
2. Virtue (doing good)
3. Knowledge (of God's word, will and ways)
4. Temperance (self-control)
5. Patience
6. Godliness
7. Brotherly kindness
8. Charity

The Holy Ghost is saying that, if we have these eight characters in us,
i. We will never fall (before Satan or his demons)
ii. Our calling by Christ is sure
iii. Our election into the kingdom of God is sure.

These three promises are the dreams of every serious Christians. We labor night and day and, deny ourselves many things to have this assurance. The Holy Ghost is giving us His unshakeable assurance that we will be admitted to heaven if we have the characters abiding and controlling us.

My brethren,  measure your Christian life daily by checking if any of them is missing and strive, pray and ask for grace to possess them. 

By His grace, we will see each other in heaven after our work here on earth is done in Jesus name.

Have a blissful week.
Shalom.
