*CHECK THE SCRIPTURES YOURSELF* 

            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _Are you practicing your profession?_

The doctor went to the University to learn medical practice. When he finished, he came back to the community to practice. He is profiting from that profession and today, it is meeting his needs. It is the same with the engineers, pharmacists, nurses, etc. They all practice their profession in the community and are taken care of by their profession. 

The Bible teaches us that Christianity is also a profession and we are taught by the greatest professor of all time, Jesus Christ. The church is our university where we are taught to live Christ-like life. If, when we enter the community we take time to practice our profession like the doctors, nurses, etc., our profession will also take care of us. 
Check the following Bible references and you will see that a Christian is a professional in godliness. The Bible says Christ is faithful to give us all He has promised us in His teachings.


_"Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast *professed a good profession* before many witnesses."_ *1 Timothy 6&#58;12*

_"Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the *Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus;"*_ *Hebrews 3&#58;1*


_"Seeing then that we have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast *our profession."*_ *Hebrews 4&#58;14*

_"Let us hold fast the *profession of our faith* without wavering; *(for he is faithful that promised;)"*_ *Hebrews 10&#58;23*
