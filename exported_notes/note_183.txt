TOPIC&#58; The fall and the rise of the prodigal son

God has two kind of sons, the one is going in the direction that is in line with His Father's will, the other is moving contrary to the will of the Father.

Luke 15&#58;12-17
Be very careful of how you help people in want. Some are prodigal sons that the hunger is what will cause them to reason within themselves. The hunger drove the prodigal son unto his father, confessing his sin. Sometimes, it is the problem that we enter into that will preach the greatest message ever to us. Providing support to such people shield them from the great teacher - the hunger. Action must match decision for fruitfulness to manifest. The prodigal son did that.


Point-1&#58; The regrettable freedom and independence of the prodigal son 

a. The fatal freedom of the prodigal son
Psalms 10&#58;4; 12&#58;4; Jeremiah 2&#58;11-13, 31
The freedom people need sometimes may become broken cisterns that cannot hold any water. 
It is important to know that freedom can sometimes be fatal.

b. The fierce famine on the persistent scoffer
Ezekiel 14&#58;13-14; Amos 8&#58;11; 2 Chronicles 7&#58;13-14
It is not all negative experiences that are from the devil. Sometimes, God sends famine to His prodigal sons so that they can come back and be saved from perishing. We (the Noahs, Daniels & Jobs) are not to be stabilizing such a one whom God is destabilizing (disciplining, correcting ) to turn back to him. Our good deeds to such a one will be working contrary to God's purpose and it will not please Him. Jeremiah 6&#58;14; 8&#58;11.  

As the Lord said, even if Daniel, Job and Noah pray for someone under punishment He would not terminate the punishment, we are wasting our time praying for a man under punishment from the Lord. God will not answer. All the one under punishment should do is to behave according to 2 Chronicles 7&#58;14 and to behave like the prodigal son (Luke 15&#58;17-20). All the prayers of an anointed servant of God for mercy for a man unwilling to abandon his sin is an exercise in futility. The first mercy of God for any sinner is the mercy of forgiveness (for a repentant sinner).

c. The forbidden food with the polluted swine.

Luke 15&#58;15-17; Mark 5&#58;11-14; Mathew 7&#58;6; 
Swine represents a favourable home for demons. Pigs are unclean and the evil spirits that entered into them are also unclean. When you move away from your father's house, you may end up eating polluted meals, forbidden meals because you have no options.
When you go and preach holiness to swines and dogs, they will hate you and rend you with their insults and rejection. Your labor is lost.
