Numbers 16&#58;1-4, 19-24, 32, 35, 41, 45, 49.

Point-1&#58; The mischievous tongues of position-seeking superiors in their corruption

Numbers 16&#58;1-
Dathan and his gangs were not sanctified people. They were unfortunately famous and could and did influence the people to their hurt. Such people might be even in the church today. I pray that I will not be part of such a group nor be influenced by them in Jesus name.

a. The corruption and mischievousness of famous position-seekers
Numbers 16&#58;10; Jeremiah 46&#58;5
God doesn't want us to seek positions, great things for ourselves. Hidden inside position-seeking is pride which the Lord hates.

b. The condemnation and misery of filthy popularity-seekers
Numbers 16&#58;9,12-14
Rebellious people against you will put a shield between you and them and will not come to you to be helped. The closer people are to you, the more rebellious they may become towards you. The Egypt where Israel was in bondage, the rebels said was for them, a land flowing with milk and honey. 


c. The character and the mindset of fallen prize-seekers
Philippians 2&#58;21; 1 Corinthians 13&#58;1-5; Proverbs 24&#58;8-9; Ecclesiastes 10&#58;13-15


Point-2&#58; The murmuring tongues of place-seekers' supporters in their contention.
Numbers 16&#58;19, 41-42

The conviction was so much that even after their destruction, the people still gathered in their defence referring to them as "the people of the LORD." The gifts that work against God's purpose will lead to damnation. 

a. The contention by thoughtless sympathizers with the godless.
 Numbers 16&#58;41-46, 49; Isaiah 45&#58;9; Hebrews 4&#58;4-5; 
The rebellious died in rebellion to God's will. Where are they now? In hell!
The position of being called the GS does not take anyone to heaven. Why can't people concentrate on what brought them to the church which is, to reach heaven?


b. The contentiousness of treacherous supporters for gain
Those that were supporting Dathan and his gang actually knew that Moses was guiltless and that the gang was wrong and yet, they supported them. Very unfortunate and they paid for it. Where are they right now for their support? In hell!

1 Corinthians 11&#58;16; 
Contention is not a custom in the church of God. It is not in its character. People should be bold to contend with the contentious and kill their project. 

c. Our consecration with thoughtful submission to God
If Dathan and his gang had submitted unto Moses, heaven would have seen them as submitting to Him because, He appointed them.

Leviticus 10&#58;1-3
God expects those that He permits to come close to Him for service to be pure and completely submissive to Him. Aaron's children who were permitted to come nigh to God perished because they did not sanctify the Lord.

a. The guilty silence of the appointed burden-bearing seventy

We must be ready to correct errors and not allow only those above us to have that responsibility. That is true burden-bearing.

b.The gagged speechless seventy abandoning their burden-bearing support
