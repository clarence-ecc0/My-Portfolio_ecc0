TOPIC&#58; FIGHTING FOR PRESERVATION WITHOUT ACCOMMODATING FEARS

Deuteronomy 20&#58;2-48-9; 1 Samuel 25&#58;8; 

As God gave Israel the commission to take their land as a possession, we also have been commissioned by God to take the people of all nations unto Him, our God. 
God's plan for the Israelites gave consideration to people that newly planted a vineyard, newly built their houses, newly married to a wife (any of these can distract the soldiers at the battlefield). God allowed them to stay back at home so that they do not die from lack of concentration at the battle front. This consideration which applied to the Jews does not apply to contemporary Christians because we love Christ more than those new things (wife, house, vineyard). Know that the word of God tells us Christians that those that are married are to behave as if they are not married, or possess something new.


Point-1&#58; The purposeful prioritized fight for their promised possession 

Deuteronomy 20&#58;1-4; Joshua 1&#58;4; 13&#58;1
The people were not to be afraid of the enemies. Theord was with them and the Lord was against the enemy; the land was waiting to speak out the enemies while the land was waiting to welcome the Israelites. We cannot be afraid of those who are fighting against God. 

Joshua 13&#58;1; 19&#58;47; Psalms 2&#58;8
Even God said Joshua was very old and well stricken in years and yet, God did not pity him nor ask him to retire. He gave him assignment to do still. God does not pity us, He strengthens us 
The reason for fighting is to possess. Therefore we will fight for our God in Judea, Samaria, Ofante Igogoro, and the uttermost part of the earth and possess the people for Christ. 


Point-2&#58; The punishable perilous fight against the priesthood 

Korah, Dathan and Abiram were mobilizing to fight against God's program entrusted to Moses. It will never be in their favor.
1 Kings 12&#58;24; Isaiah 45&#58;9; Numbers 16&#58;8-11; Acts 5&#58;35,38-39;


Point-3&#58; The prescribed perpetual fight for present day priests

We are the present day priests. There is a prescribed fight for us, the present day priests. 

John 18&#58;36-37
The kingdom of our king (Christ) is not of this world. We are therefore not enlisted to fight for the kingdom of this world. If it were this world then would His servants (i.e., us) fight for it. 

Luke 9&#58;51-56; Mathew 26&#58;49-51,2 Corinthians 10&#58;3-6
As Christ was not allowed to have habitation in the region of Samaria, He moved to another place without strive. This is an example to us. 
Those that prevail with the sword shall perish by the same sword. Christ said this to one who was fighting in ignorance to defend Christ.

1 Timothy 6&#58;12; 2 Timothy 4&#58;7-8; Galatians 2&#58;4,5-6; Jude 1&#58;4
We should use our strength to help people to have the faith that can help them overcome their diabetes, hypertension, etc. That is a good fight of faith. We can fight that. We are prevented to fight, we were only fighting (contend) for the faith once delivered to us.
