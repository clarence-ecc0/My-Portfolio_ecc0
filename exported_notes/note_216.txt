Topic&#58; Our perfect offering to God

A. A biblical example
Perfection of heart precedes perfect offering before the Lord
Proverbs 23&#58;26 Genesis 17
The first & most important thing God wants from us is our heart, not our material things. Abraham was instructed to walk before God with a perfect heart. It was after this that God required Abraham to give him what he loved so much, his only son.

1 Samuel 16&#58;7; Hebrews 13&#58; 15
We should give unto God our sacrifice of praise. 

B. The basis for offering 

Romans 11&#58; 35-36; 12&#58;1
All that we have, our eyes, ears, fame, bank accounts, intelligence, innovations, including all that we cherish so much shall be left behind. All that we have are of Christ, through Christ and to Christ. We were given of Him, and through Him, to enjoy them and leave them behind. God delights in our offering ourselves and material things to him.
When you have offered your heart to God, you can't use that heart to think evil, the body you offered to Him can't be used to commit sin, our offered eyes cannot look at sinful things with pleasure and desire. 


C. The blessedness of our offering
Abraham was to offer one child unto God but God blessed him and gave him Jacob and Esau in addition to Isaac.

2 Chronicles 31;20-21



TOPIC&#58; UNDERSTANDING THE UNIVERSAL LAW OF SOWING AND REAPING

Galatians 6&#58;7-10; 2 Timothy 2&#58;7
It is what we put that we will harvest; what we sow, that we shall reap. If we consider what God says, we will not get into trouble. Whatever you want to do or say, consider what God has said.
When we hear the word of God, we should match it with our ways of life and make necessary adjustments.

Point-1&#58; The endless reaping after sowing to the flesh
Galatians 6&#58;7-8
Whatever we sow is not to another person's flesh but to our own flesh. We therefore should think of the consequences whatever we are doing.

a. The consequences of sowing defilement to the flesh
Galatians 6&#58;7-8; 1 Corinthians 15&#58;33; Proverbs 22&#58;8
We can predict what we will reap later on by looking at the thing that we are sowing right now.

2 Peter 2&#58;12, 19

b. The cost of sowing discord in the fellowship 
This is about those that sow discord in the family of God. If we sow discord in God's family, someone will sow discord in our own families. If we scatter God's family, our family will also be scattered.

Proverbs 6&#58;14-19; 16&#58;28; James 3&#58;14-16; 2 Samuel 
She that commits abortion is shedding innocent blood. The baby in the womb is innocent. Ahitophel sowed abomination and reaped the fruit thereof.

c. The condemnation of seared sower destined to fury forever 
The single seed men sow bring much fruits. One evil that a man sow brings much evil fruits. 


1 Timothy 4&#58;1-2; Mathew 13&#58;24-27; Jude 1&#58;4,12-13; Job 4&#58;8-9
When men of God depart from the faith, they become seared sowers and it will not be long they will speak lies in hypocrisy. Their conscience now seared will assist them to continue in their evil. 
When you sow holiness in the fellowship and somebody comes to sow hypocrisy, he is an enemy of God.

Point-2&#58; The eternal reward of sowing to the Spirit
The sanctified born again brethren are sowing to the Holy Spirit of God. 

Galatians 6&#58;7-8b

a. The conversion of fleshly sinners by the Spirit
Jeremiah 4&#58;3-4; Acts 3&#58;19, 26
A sinner must repent of his sin and be converted and then, his sins will be blotted out. 

b. The cultivation and faithful sowing by the saint.
Hosea 10&#58;12; Proverbs 11&#58;18, 30-31; James 3&#58;17-18
Anywhere I go, I will be sowing the seed of righteousness by God's grace. As


c. The commendation of fruitful sowers to the Spirit
Philippians 1&#58;9-11; John 15&#58;2,8,16; Hebrews 13&#58;12-16
Christ suffered without the gate so that we can be sanctified. We must be ready to hear the reproach from those who do not appreciate what we are doing in response to what Christ suffered for us. We endure the reproach because we are looking forward to being admitted to heaven. 


Point-3&#58; The effectual response of saint sowing in due season.
Galatians 6&#58;9-10
Anytime we have an opportunity, we are committed to well-doing 

a. The covenant of well-doing in the family
Proverbs 31&#58;10-12; Ephesians 5&#58;28-33
When we have a covenant of well-doing in our family, great will be the favor of the Lord upon our family. With such covenant, we will be able to love our partners like our own body. The universal law of sowing and reaping will bring great blessings to our family.

b. The commitment to well-doing in the fellowship
