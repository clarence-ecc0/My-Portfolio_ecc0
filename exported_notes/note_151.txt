Point-2&#58; The possible perseverance of the believers while enduring temptations.

a. Resisting the tempter with the sword of the word
We deceive ourselves if we think that Satan will not tempt us. If he tempted the first man and woman that had no sin, if he tempted even Christ, who are we to assume he will not tempt us?

Mathew 4&#58;3-11
Satan has a thousand and one ways to tempt us. He will tempt us in the direction of our hunger (fame, food, knowledge, flesh of the opposite gender, etc.). 
The devil doesn't give up so quickly. If he fails in  one temptation, he will come up again, from another angle, at other time, in another day. 

If God has promised you something and it has not come, if Satan comes with the same thing (money, good health, fame, children, life partner, etc), do not accept. Be like Christ who shunned Satan and waited  for the kingdom the Father had promised Him. He got it at the appointed time. 

b. Refusing appealing temptation in supplication with willingness 
Mathew 26&#58;41
If you are praying for something from God (e.g., consecration unto God) but you do not have the willingness to do what it takes, you are wasting Gods. We must bring our flesh to the same level of desire as our spirit so that we can overcome.

c. Rejecting attractive temptations with the steadfastness of the warrior.

Luke 8&#58;13-14; 1 Timothy 6&#58;9; 
We must be ready to reject all the attractive temptations of Satan. On my way to heaven, the Lord will help me not to be sidetracked
