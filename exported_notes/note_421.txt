Mark 3&#58;13
To be a minister of Christ, you need to be with Him so that you can
1. Be for Him (for Him alone and not for another thing)
2. Be in Him (we are in Him and He is in us) 
3. Be like Him (the works He did, so that we can do likewise)
4. Be by Him (pray in His name, power. Nothing from Satan kingdom to do His work)
5. With Him (we stay with Him to watch and learn how to do His work acceptably)
6. Through Him. 

Point-1&#58; The sinfulness and the self-centeredness of fruitless men
As long as any man is sinful, he will be fruitless in the kingdom even though he may be fruitful in the secular world. The fruitlessness also applies to those that are always self-centered in their work in the ministry. 

Luke 5&#58;4-5,8; 
Peter a natural man could not catch fish.
Christ's instructions to Peter was let down the nets but he let down only one net. And the multitude of fish that entered the single net was breaking. If the other nets around were let down, the fish would have entered the other nets and there wouldn't have been breakage. It is a lesson for us. All the converts getting into our net alone will cause the net to break. Let's allow converts to go to the other ministries. 

Peter saw the miracle and had contrition (the miracle by Christ smote his heart); conviction that Christ was the Messiah (and he yielded to Him)! Confession (he confessed his sinfulness), conversion (changed from a sinful way of life), consecration (in obedience to Christ command to follow Him, they did that), continuation (until the end of his life, Peter continued with the Lord). Note that in consecration, it is not only sin that we leave behind but things that may hinder Christ's assignment (Peter left the fishes even though they were not sinful). 
These 6 (contrition, conviction, confession, conversion, consecration and continuation) must be there in the life of minister to be fruitful.

Romans 7&#58;14-17
Remember the Lord's day and keep it Holy. The question is, is there any day that is the devil's day? No. Therefore everyday is the Lord's day and we must keep all days holy. 

We are going to unseat that sin that controls us. 


Point-2&#58; The salvation and the Sanctification of His favored members
We are favored members of Christ's Church and He grants us salvation and Sanctification.

Luke 5&#58;10
Before we met Christ, all of God's ministers were partners together in sinful living but after meeting Christ, we are now partners together in contrition, in conviction, confession, in conversion, in consecration, and in continuation. They forsook the good thing they were doing (various professions) for a better thing (ministry). 

We are not following any leader (like pastor Kumuyi) but Christ. We are following Christ and we are with Him, for Him, in Him, like Him.

Titus 2&#58;11-12; John 17&#58;8
Grace of God cancelled out "cannot" from my personal dictionary. I will never say again, I cannot live Holy, act selflessly live right. Grace has addressed all these. I am now a peculiar person to God. The world will say that I am a queer person but I am an uncompromising, peculiar person.
