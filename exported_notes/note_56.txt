{{text:TOPIC&#58; THE ALMIGHTY ADVOCATE WITH ALL AUTHORITY

1 John 2&#58;1-2; Revelation 1&#58;7-8

Christ is the Advocate for the whole world and He has all authority, meaning, He can address all challenges.

Point-1&#58; The prevailing authority of the Almighty Sovereign 
Mathew 28&#58;18-20; Mark 1&#58;22
When you go into all nations, do not teach them about Satan, and all the evil spirits and what they can do to them. We are commanded rather, to teach them simply the commandments of Christ, and the powers of Christ in heaven and on Earth. This is enough for all their problems. We are to teach and not to entertain and make people happy. There are things to observe and obey in the word of God. This is what we are commanded to teach- e.g., repentance, restitution, forgiveness, etc. After our teaching, there must be something for the people to observe in their old lives. THERE MUST BE SOMETHING TO OBSERVE.

Luke 4&#58;18-21, 32, 36; 
Christ has authority to heal all broken-hearts, deliver all the captives/slaves of Satan, captives to Palm wine, marijuana, immoral thoughts, etc. Everywhere Jesus went, there was authority. That is what He has transferred unto us that diligently obey Him and are unafraid for ourselves. 

Mathew 8&#58;8-9, 10-13; 1 Corinthians 15&#58;24
There are some negative people and spirits that still manifest power at this time, but the time is coming when Christ shall have put down all authorities and all powers and He alone will have power to do anything. Even the power of death to take life shall be taken away by Him, and the power of sickness to disrupt our lives at will be taken away. There will be no sickness in the kingdom of Christ.

Whosoever/whatsoever that controls you (anger, lust, gluttony, etc.) becomes your lord and master. 

Point-2&#58; The perpetual ability of the always-able Savior 
Hebrews 7&#58;25-26; 2&#58;17-18; 1 Corinthians 10&#58;13

Christ is ever able to save those that come to God for salvation, for deliverance, for healing, THROUGH Him
Every temptation that we face today, challenges that we face today, somebody else, in the kingdom of God has faced them before and the faithful God helped them, therefore, He will help you also. 

Acts 20&#58;32; Ephesians 3&#58;20-21
As you look at some Christians, you will observe that they are not being built up. The simple reason is that, although they have been hearing the word, and also reading the word all these times, they have not been practicing them as Christ commanded in Mathew 7&#58; 24-27. There must be transformation in our lives from the word that we read or hear constantly and this comes through constant practice of the word.

If you truly believe that there is a power that is working in you and yet, you are so afraid to carry out Godly instructions, the way out is to ignore your fears and move ahead. The feared tyrant will be subdued for you through your act of courage and faith. 

Do we bring glory to the church we attend, or are we destroying the glory of the church by the bribes we give, our evil utterances and adjudication, etc? We need to remember that wherever we are and whatever we do, we are ambassadors of Christ and our words and deeds rub-off on the glory of Christ. 

Philippians 3&#58;10-21; Jude 1&#58;24
There is still more in heaven to be given unto us. None has gotten all that heaven has to offer; none has attained the complete things that the heaven can give and apostle Paul attested to this. He was not saying that he was not yet saved, sanctified or baptized in the Holy Ghost. He had attained all these even from the day that Ananias prayed for him. There is a perfection, completeness that is in the heavens that all of us are looking up to. We will all receive this perfection, completeness from Christ if we remain steadfast till the end. 

Point-3&#58; The permanent authorization of the acknowledged Son.
Christ came to show us how the perfect man should live. The Son of God came in the form of the son of man and in that lower form, taught us how the sons of men could live as the Son of God with the life of holiness and to exercise power over sickness and demons. Let maintain the practice of using the power that Christ has given unto us. There is no gain accumulating power and not using it.
