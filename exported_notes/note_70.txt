TOPIC&#58; Purposeful and proper concerns for the harvest of souls. 

There are two types of harvest of the fields- what we do through evangelism and what the angels will do at the end of the age. 

Jeremiah 8&#58;11,20

Point-1&#58; Concern for sinners without entanglement with their sins

Although we have concerns for sinners we will not be entangled with their sins. In all our struggles to bring in sinners, we must be careful not to compromise and miss the kingdom ourselves. Polygamy is sin and we must tell the people if not we become compromisers. 

In all things I must live unto God. As I do all I can to add sinners to the record of those that are saved.

Judges 8&#58;22-24; 2 Chronicles 25&#58;1-2, 14-16
