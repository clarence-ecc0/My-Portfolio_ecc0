TOPIC&#58; THE GREAT POWER OF OUR TONGUE
Proverbs 18&#58;21; 21&#58;23; 25&#58;15.

We will eat the fruit of the power of the tongue (death and life) depending on how we use the tongue; Our tongues can break bones (difficult matters) depending on how we speak to the Prince.  Learn how to control your tongue. 

Point-1&#58; The Great peril of a transgressing tongue
Psalm 17&#58;3; 39&#58;1; Numbers 21&#58;4-5; Psalm 52&#58;2-7; Isaiah 51&#58;1-3,12-15; James 3&#58;5-7; 1&#58;26-

To avoid sinning with our tongue requires our personal determination. Israel said God brought them into the wilderness for their death, very wrong because God had said He was taking them to a land of milk and honey where they will worship Him in Holiness. 
The tongue is a little matter, behold how great a matter the little fire (tongue) kindleth. Nobody has been able to tame the tongue, only grace will help us to overcome it. 

In all that Israel (including his brethren Aaron & Miriam) spoke against Moses, he always came to intercede for them before God. That is a great example of sanctification and humility. 


Point-2&#58; The gracious privilege of a transformed tongue
John 7&#58;46; Psalm 45&#58;2; Isaiah 50&#58;4; Colossians 4&#58;6; Proverbs 22&#58;11; Ephesians 4&#58;29

The tongue of a sinner cannot be tamed or, trained until the heart is transformed through grace. Because the mouth must speak out of the abundance of the heart therefore, transform the heart and the tongue will speak gracious, healing words. 
Do not allow yourself or anyone to put graceless words in your mouth. 

Point-3&#58; The glorious power of a tender tongue
Proverbs 15&#58;1; 25&#58;15; Judges 8&#58;1-3; 1 Samuel 25&#58;10-13, 21-24, 32-33,38; Colossians 3&#58;13-16

Great wrath like that of the Ephraimites was abated by the soft-spoken word of Gideon. There would have been bloodshed and Satan would have rejoiced seeing Israel fight itself. Same thing happened when Abigail intervened and turned away the wrath of David. Let us be careful how we speak, to offend powerful people. 
Let the peace of God always rule in your heart, do not  let Satan take over your heart because of provocative word. Anger can cause heart attack. Case of a man who slumped and died in a fit of great anger. 
Great anger tells you what is in the heart of the person. The Bible says that "anger lies in the blossom of fools." The antidote to great anger in the heart is the presence of the peace of God on the throne in the heart. This is similar to the indwelling of the Word of Christ. Sing a song, share some joy as the singer says, and you will gain control of the anger.

If you always listen to dirty words from evil songs or movies or from social media, you will store the abundance of hurting words in your heart, and when there is provocation, they will come out in torrent from the abundance stored in the heart.
