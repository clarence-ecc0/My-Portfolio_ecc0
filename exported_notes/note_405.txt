{{text:Sharing the property bought for you with whom it was bought from&#58; 
✓ When a car is bought for you, you have full ownership of the car. The old owner who sold it to you cannot come any day and demand to use it for some time, for any venture before returning it back to you. Christ bought us completely from our former owner (Satan) and must have full use of our services all the time. 
✓ There can be no time that Satan can come to use our body or mind for his own service. We cannot be for Christ's use on Sunday only and, from Monday to Saturday, we make ourselves available for Satan's use. We cannot be serving God only on Sunday and, from Monday to Saturday, we are in the service of Satan, our former owner. Christ complained about this (Mathew 6&#58;24). We can never get any reward from Christ your supposed new owner. 

Sharing our properties with our neighbors&#58; 
✓ But, can we share our material properties with our neighbors? Yes (cast your bread upon many waters in Eccl 11&#58;1; make unto yourself Mammon of unrighteousness [Luke 16&#58;9&#93;). 
✓ When a man is evil to his neighbor, he isl evil to himself. There was a corn farmer who consistently produced the best corn in the land and his secret is that he shares his great corn species with all around him. Cast your bread upon many waters (sow in many businesses and sow in many people). Do not be evil to the aged and to the toddlers. Retribution of evil done to the aged will come back to you (Isaiah 33&#58;1) and the toddler you maltreated today will be at his full strength when you will be in your weakness (remember Joseph & his brothers).  The reward of the wicked (Ecclesiastes 8&#58;12-13).

✓ Both wisdom and money are defences. But the excellency of knowledge is that it is only wisdom that gives life. (Ecclesiastes 7&#58;12). Wisdom is better than gold.

✓ Believers and suffering. Believers can, and indeed suffer many things in this world. There is another world we are going to where suffering shall be no more. Isaiah 43&#58;2 states clearly God's mind concerning believers suffering&#58; when we pass through the waters, when we walk through the fire, God will be with us and we will not be destroyed. 

The story was told of a missionary that hazarded his life on the mission field and as he was returning home, he happened to be in the ship with a general of the empire who was returning home as well.  The general received a hero's welcome at the harbor and was celebrated. The missionary enjoyed no such heroic welcome and he went home bitter and disappointed and cried unto God. The answer God gave him in response to his bitterness is enlightening. As the story goes, he complained that the general who went to wage war, killed people, came home and was celebrated but for him who went to save soul, nothing was done for him. God simply told him, "but, you have not reached your home yet".  Believers should wait until they reach their home for their celebration. We will be celebrated. 

✓ God sometimes chooses to 'PROVE' His people. Have you trusted the Lord for an outcome and have not seen it yet, and are you losing faith in God because of this? Remember that God sometimes chooses to prove His people. God met Abraham at 75 years of age and the promise of Isaac was not fulfilled until age 100 years. When at old age (about 99 years), Abraham still had no Isaac and yet believed God to come true with His promise, God proved Abraham to be righteous (Romans 4&#58;19-22). And yet again after Isaac was born, God proved Abraham again to sacrifice him to Him and when Abraham came true once again, God made an everlasting covenant with Abraham saying, through his Seed, the whole earth will be saved. Do you know that Abraham lived up to 175 years and saw His great grand son (Jacob)? Genesis 25&#58;7-8; Hebrews 11&#58;8-9. 
✓ Unfortunately, God tried to 'PROVE' the children of Israel (Abraham's seeds) in the wilderness with lack of food and water and they failed woefully. 

Forgiving the people that wrong us
✓ The Lord asked us to forgive our enemies (Mathew 6&#58;15). It has great benefits for even our health when we forgive those that offended us. A man that does not forgive, that keeps offences against his enemy in his heart is like someone that is drinking poison and expecting another man to die. 
✓ Science has taught us that great sicknesses (hypertension, depression, stress, ulcer, anxiety and many more deadly sicknesses) have their root in unforgiveness.
