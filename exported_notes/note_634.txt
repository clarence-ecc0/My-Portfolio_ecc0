TOPIC&#58; JESUS IS LORD

Philippians 2&#58;11
[11&#93;And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

What does this mean?
1. Plainly, when we declared Jesus is Lord, God is glorified. 

What does it mean to declare Christ as Lord?
Answer&#58; (1) Total obedience and submission to His will.
When you say someone is the Lord of your life, it means you are completely obedient to Him.

(2) It is not merely verbal declaration. This is from deep within the soul with evidence of submission. There is an involvement of the Holy Spirit.

1 Corinthians 12&#58;3
[3&#93;Wherefore I give you to understand, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed&#58; and that no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost.

When we declare Christ as the Lord but we live our lives contrary to His will, is He truly our Lord?

Luke 6&#58;46
[46&#93;And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?

Malachi 1&#58;6
[6&#93;A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master&#58; if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear? saith the LORD of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?

Admonition
Judging by your present life, do you think heaven's record will show that Christ is the Lord of your life? 

Reflect on this and make necessary amendment before the light goes out.

Be blessed.
