Ecclesiastes 11&#58;3
"If the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth&#58; and if the tree fall toward the south, or toward the north, in the place where the tree falleth, there it shall be."

No change in position after death. 
✓ A man that died living his/her life among the congregation of sinners will forever remain in the congregation of the damned, in the lake of fire. 

✓ The opposite is also true. He that dies among the congregation of the saints (obedient children of God) shall remain in the congregation of the saints, in paradise eternally.

God keeps a record.

Every tree will fall one day
✓ No tree will stand forever. Everyone of them will fall one day. No man will live forever. Everyone will depart from this earth one day.

✓ The tree many people carry about (and steal, kill, cheat or poison others to nourish it; give it pleasure through adultery or fornication, etc.), one day it will fall.

✓ We need to ask ourselves this question. Where will I be when my tree falls? In the evil forest of sin where is documenting more of my disobedience against Him, or in the place of good work where I am living by the commandments of God? 

✓ We need to ask ourselves this also? What will be the last thing that will be documented in God's register concerning me before death takes me away?  He died as a disobedient man who rejected God until the final moment or, he died a repentant man who strive and lived by God's commandments? 

✓ If you have a clock close to you, listen to it carefully. It is ticking- for you and for me. 
What will our final moment be like? Child of God or enemy of God? The choice is ours

I pray that you will have many reflective moments as you as you go about your business for the day.
