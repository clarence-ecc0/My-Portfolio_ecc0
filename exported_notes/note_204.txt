*Theme&#58;* _The twelve sons of Jacob and how they were named_

In the Song of Solomon, Jealousy was likened to the cruelty of the grave. Jealousy appeared to be the order of the day in the house of Jacob, whom God named Israel, because of the great jealousy of two blood sisters. It is better to imagine atmosphere of that house where the Spirit of jealousy rieigned high.
The bitterness and the strife can be picked out of the lifelong names that they gave to their children. Except for Judah (and to him was given 'The Sceptre of Authority), the names of the remaining eleven sons of Jacob bore the Mark of their jealousy. Rachel went to her grave with her jealousy and one wonders how Leah must have mourned her with whom she strove throughout their shared lives with Jacob.
