*Theme&#58;* _God's love is for all men_

G. Rios told a story of a young homeless boy who approached a police officer during a blizzard in Chicago at the train station and asked if he knew where he could rest safe for the night.

The police officer asked him to go down the road to a white house, knock on the gate and if anyone opens the door, he should say *John 3&#58;16.* He did and an older woman let him in. She gave him food, hot water to bath and a comfortable bed to sleep in till morning. 

In the morning, the woman took him to a big fire place, brought out a big Bible and asked him if he knew what *John 3&#58;16* meant. He replied that he never heard of that till the police officer told him the day before. Beginning from this verse, she led to faith in Jesus Christ.

"For God so loved the world" is a message that conveys a new idea of love. *_The Jews before now believed that God only loved the religious (the Jews) and hated the non-religious (the Gentiles)._* The fact is that His love is boundless. God loves everyone, not just the religious and good.

The evidence of this love is that He gave His only Son to the world to save humanity from self-destruction and consequences of transgression.

_An excerpt from *Daily Manna, January - December, 2024*_
