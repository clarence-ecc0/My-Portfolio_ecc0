TOPIC&#58; THE TONGUE THAT DETERMINES DESTINY

Point-1&#58; The tongue that retires or renews the soul

Point-2&#58; The thoughts that rule or reign in the seekers

Point-3&#58; The turning that resets or refines His sons



Point-1&#58;

Acts 27&#58;23
The miracle that I need is already in the making. It will manifest today. Because God is with me, the people with me shall be preserved and blessed.

a. The reformed tongue and the reserved miracle
    Luke 1&#58;64
Zechariah got his miracle reserved for him at the naming of his son

b. The ruinous tongue and the rejected miracle
     2 Kings 7&#58;1
Actually, all the challenges we experience in life are due to our ruinous tongues. Just simply agree with what God has said and it will be well with you. We have no reason to doubt God. A doubting tongue rejects God's miracles and it is a ruinous tongue.

c. The renewed tongue and the refreshing miracles 
     Luke 1&#58;37-38
Nothing shall be impossible in my life this year. There is a promise in every book of the Bible. We need to claim them for they are ours. All we need to say is, "be it unto me according to thy word"

Point-2&#58; 
2 Kings 5&#58;9 - 14; 2 Corinthians 10&#58;4- 5
When we come to the Lord, the thoughts in our mind are important. They will determine what we get from the Lord. Naaman came to the Lord and had already thought of what God would do. When it didn't happen according to his thoughts, he lost interest in what God could do. We need to cast down all the imaginations in our hearts and let the will of God be done. 

Job 42&#58;2 - 3, 6, 10.
Job could have gone to God the first day his afflictions started and He would have recovered him but he just kept up with his thoughts such as, bad things happen to good people and vice versa, my children may be destroyed because of sins I am not even aware of, etc. 

a. The thoughts of foolishness or faithlessness is sin
Proverbs 24&#58;9; 
When the Israelites thought about going back to Egypt, that was foolishness it was sin before God.

b. The thought of faith in His faithfulness heals sickness
Hebrews 11&#58;11
It appears that after Sarah laughed her cynical laughter when God said she was going to have her baby Isaac and after she lied and God corrected her, she immediately got enough faith that God was going to do what He said. She believed and had enough strength to conceive Isaac. Faith strengthens us. 

c. The thoughts of the Father and freshness bring salvation
Isaiah 55&#58;7-8,11
Negative thoughts make us unrighteous before God. The thoughts of God are revealed in His word. The word of God is His messenger and will accomplish the task it was sent to do. He sent His word and it healed them. 

Point-3
The Lord will reset my life tonight and I will be on my way to be at the exact place He expects to be. 

2 Kings 20&#58;1-6; 
We can talk to God directly over anything unpleasant we want to be reversed in our lives.

a. The turning to God with His word in your mouth
Hosea 14&#58;2
Speak God's word back to Him

b. Turning to His grace with His wonders on our mind
Psalm 105&#58;5
When you come to God, do not concentrate on your pains or sorrows but rather on His miracle that He will do in your life.

c. Turning to His goodness in His way for our miracles.
Isaiah 30&#58;1,26
My righteousness, victory, etc multiplied in Jesus name.
