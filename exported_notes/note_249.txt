{{text:TOPIC&#58;  COMPLETE HEALING AND HEALTH  BY HIS STRIPES



Point-1&#58; The proclamation of your healing and health

Exodus 15&#58;26; Psalms 7&#58;20
God does not have any reason to put sickness in the body of a child of God. He said, "I will not put any of the diseases which I put upon the Egyptians" Therefore we remove them. 

The word of God is His servant (an angel or Christ )and it goes out to accomplish His purpose. He has sent His word of power into my life this night and it accomplishes His purpose in Jesus name. 

Isaiah 55&#58; 8-11; Mark 16&#58;15-17
My thoughts of weakness of the body, limited power or wisdom are not God's thoughts concerning me. His thoughts about me are higher than my thoughts. 
If anyone,or power is able to chase back the rains God has commanded to come down upon me with blessings, then I will believe that there is someone or power that can chase back God's rain of blessings commanded to come and bless me. There is none.
Among the signs that will follow me are the power to cast out devil's, no poison that I mistakenly drink ( in daytime or in dreams) can kill me; in my dreams, my spirit is awake; my hands that I lay on the sick will bring recovery into them. 


Point-2&#58;  The prescription for your healing and health 

God's prescription is in Exodus 15&#58;35; 23&#58;25
I am to hearken to His commandments. God created me,not Satan therefore, I will not serve Satan or idols. 
James 5&#58;15-16
Another prescription is that we must pray a prayer of faith and it will heal the sick. It is not the prayer of self-condemnation that, I am a sinner and this is upon me because of my past (forgiven) sin, etc. We must pray the prayer of faith. 
Another part of the prescription is that we must confess our offence to one another. Whosoever I have offended, I will confess to him. 

Point-3&#58;  The preservation of your healing and health 

I am healed but they will take it away from me. What kind of confession is that? Who is greater than Jesus that he can take what Christ gave to you, from you? There is none. 

Exodus 15&#58;16-; Job 17&#58;9; Proverbs 4&#58;20-23; Isaiah 33&#58;24
After our healing, we will keep on reading God's promises and keep believing them. We got our healing via faith and we will preserve them by the same faith. My constant reference verse and confession will be "I am the Lord that healeth thee."
The inhabitants in Deeper Life shall not say "I am sick". 

John 5&#58;14; 3 John 1&#58;2; Mathew 8&#58;7-8
We preserve the dominion we have received by refraining from sinning any further.
I prosper, I am in good health, and my soul has also prospered. 
My servants (my legs, eyes, etc. serving me are my servants) are healed in Jesus name.
