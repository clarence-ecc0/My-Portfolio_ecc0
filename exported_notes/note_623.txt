{{text:There is no eternal security
The whole humanity came to the bus station of life. They are travelling to one of two pre-determined destinations. We joined when we reached the age of accountability, to choose what is right and to pursue it, or reject it. 

There are two buses with Captain- Christ and Captain-Satan in charge of the buses.

Each  bus is predestined to a final end - the paradise of God, though lost by Adam and Eve has been made available again by Christ or, the lake of fire for all who ever rebelled against God's command, Satan, his angels and the human beings they successfully deceived, all included. 

From the beginning, God gave man the permission to choose which bus he will travel with- Captain- Christ or captain - Satan.

Bus-Christ has rules of life that can be summarized as holy and peaceful living.

Bus-Satan's only agenda is that no one should obey the rules of bus-Christ because all such automatically belong to his bus.

As we journey daily to the predestined eternal habitation of man, Satan and his servants are daily headhunting the travellers on Bus-Christ with greater fervency.
