{{text:TOPIC&#58; No Christianity without obedience 
The matter of eternity is so important that we need to have assurance that our names are truly in the book of life, particularly when Christ Himself has warned that many shall be disappointed in that day.

✓ Do you have an assurance of eternal life with Christ?

What is your confidence? Grace? 
Maybe, if your grace is not in vain!
Let's check the scriptures to see the state of the grace we received.

_"That as sin hath reigned unto death, even so might grace reign through righteousness unto eternal life by Jesus Christ our Lord."_
Romans 5&#58;21

Titus 2&#58;11-12,14
[11&#93;For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men,

[12&#93;Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;

[14&#93;Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works.

✓ Grace reigns in our lives unto eternal life through righteousness. Has God abandoned righteousness as a necessity for seeing Him? No!

Habakkuk 1&#58;13
"Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil, and canst not look on iniquity&#58;......"

✓ But, whose righteousness gives us eternal life? Mine or Christ's?
The righteousness imputed to us (not earned by us) when we believe in Jesus Christ, the same way Abraham believed in God and He imputed righteousness to him. 

✓ How do I have assurance that the righteousness of Christ has been imputed to me? What is the evidence that I believe in Christ the way the Holy Scriptures meant it/expect it, so that righteousness can be imputed to me?

Is heart or head belief (affirmation) that Jesus Christ is the Son of God and He died for the sins of the whole world enough? Is that all that the Scripture is asking for? No! If it is only this, then demons also qualify for salvation because they too know this for sure.

Matthew 28&#58;19-20 tells us what we must do to show we truly believe in Christ. 

Matthew 28&#58;19-20
[19&#93;Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost&#58;
[20&#93;Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you&#58; and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Complete obedience to His instructions (observance of His teachings), that is the work that evidences our faith. 
✓ This is what the Holy Ghost meant when He said, the faith that is without work (obedience to God) is a dead faith that cannot save. 

James 2&#58;20
[20&#93;But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?

If faith is merely head knowledge (belief) without evidencing work, know that this is possessed by devils also, and we know that they are not going to see God. 
✓ They believe there is God, to the point of trembling but they live constantly to oppose all His instructions (.... thou child of the devil, thou enemy of all righteousness, with thou not cease to pervert the right ways of the Lord? Acts 13&#58;10). 

James 2&#58;19
[19&#93;Thou believest that there is one God; thou doest well&#58; the devils also believe, and tremble.

✓ What scriptural backing do we have to support the assertion that grace (which incidentally comes by faith) cannot do us any good without work (without obedience to God's instructions)?

[A&#93; We simply look at the life of Abraham (God told Abraham, 
# move to another place and he obeyed; 
# sacrifice Isaac to me and he went ahead to do that; # send Ishmael away, and he went ahead to do that even though it was grievous in his eyes, etc.). 

James 2&#58;21
[21&#93;Was not Abraham our father justified by works, when he had offered Isaac his son upon the altar?

[B&#93; We also look at what Christ said in Mathew 28&#58;19-20

✓ Let's look again at Romans 5&#58;21
"That as sin hath reigned unto death, even so might grace reign through righteousness unto eternal life by Jesus Christ our Lord."_

Grace cannot reign through unrighteousness (disobedience to God). Any grace we believe we have while we are thriving in sin, in clear disobedience to God's instructions cannot save us. Romans 5&#58;21 clearly does not apply to such Christians. 

But this can change. Why not pray to receive the grace that makes you live a life that is obedient to the Bible first. With that, you will be working towards becoming a Romans 5&#58;21 Christian.
May this project be achieved in your life in 2025 in Jesus name.
