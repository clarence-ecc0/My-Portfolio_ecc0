TOPIC&#58; Rest And Restoration After Warfare 

Joshua 21&#58;43-44

Point- 1&#58; The restlessness and reason for warfare and crises.

A. The reason for the war against Canaan
Deuteronomy 18&#58;10-12; 9&#58;4; Leviticus 18&#58;24-28

The Canaanites were throwing their children into the fire. It was because of this wickedness, not the righteousness of Israel that God gave away their land. God could have used flood or water to destroy them as happened at other times but this time, He chose Israel to do it. There is no partiality with God. He warned Israel that if they do what the Canaanites did, they too would be dispossessed of the land.

B. The rebel in warfare against their Creator
Isaiah 45&#58;9; Acts 5&#58;38

The Canaanites (and Israel eventually) rebelled against their Maker and it profited them not.

C. The restlessness of the warriors in their corruption 
Genesis 6&#58;3,5,12; Isaiah 57&#58;20-21


Point- 2&#58; Our redemption and recovery from the world of corruption 

A. Repentance from the warfare of carnality
James 4&#58;1-3,4
The wars, strives in humans come from their lusts. Nebuchadnezzar , Herod, empires fought wars to possess what belonged to others. Since the Prince of peace has come for us, we need not be at war against anyone but should live in peace with all men if we are truly born again. Whatever we get through warfare (e.g., trampling upon others) is not worth it. Our lust is because of fraternity with the world.

Romans 8&#58;7

B. Redemption from the corruption of the world 
1 Peter 1&#58;18-19,14-16
The corruption of people comes from the corrupt ways they observed and learned under their fathers. It is transmitted through all generations. Satan is also the father of the sinful world. Born again persons are supposed to be free from corruption.

GS supports individual evangelism. It was the reason for which he was ostracized in his time. The Evangelism for Enugu and Kogi state will continue in Jesus name. 

C. Recovery from the consequences of all wars

Whatever we have lost in our wars, the Lord will restore them to us in Jesus name. As we repent and return to the Lord, He will restore to us the inheritance that the palmer worms, the caterpillars, the canker worms have eaten up in Jesus name (Joel 2&#58;25)

2 Timothy 2&#58;25-26


Point-3&#58; The restoration and reformation by the word of Christ

A. Rest and restfulness with security in Christ 
Mathew 11&#58;28-30

Coming to Christ in sincerity takes away our restlessness and replaces it with restfulness. Our yoke (to Satan) drove us furiously around for the vanity of the world. This was before we came to Christ. His anointing has broken the yoke and we are now free from the wicked driver (Satan). Many are running and striving because they are yoked to Satan who is driving them furiously from one misdemeanor (immorality, list for money, power, women, self -aggrandisment, etc.) to another.

B. Recreation and restoration to sanctification by Christ
1 Thessalonians 5&#58;23
For the pastor or leader to be able to help the congregation, he must not be afraid of anything or anyone because, he has the authority of heaven (the love of the Father, the peace of Christ and the power of the Holy Ghost) assisting him in his work. Sanctification brings peace to the heart. Two people that are Sanctified cannot be without peace towards one another.


C. Realization of redemption through submission to Christ
Philippians 2&#58;3-5
Our redemption is only truly realized when we submit fully to Christ therefore, it is profitable for us to submit to Him.
