Holding unto God's promises without wavering&#58;
As husbands, when we wait to hear from God before we speak to our family, they will treat our words with utmost respect. They will learn to be patient with us when we say we are waiting for the Lord to speak to us and will wait until we hear from God. This is very important for husbands. 
While waiting to hear from God, we are patient and we are doing something. E.g., we know that God has promised us something in a storehouse but we have not seen the key to the door. We will be searching for the key while we are waiting for God. For instance, God has already told Joshua that all his enemies will be defeated. Yet Jericho was shut against them. They did not just stay still but were doing something, moving around the city as commanded by God. 

To blast stones, engineers move about the rock, drill a hole and drop explosives in the holes; not complaining but moving about and dropping explosives. We need to drop the explosives of prayers and good deeds as we move about eliminating our obstacles. All our prayers are explosives we are dropping in the holes we have drilled around our obstacles, blast the obstacles away to give us access to our promises. 

Hebrews 6&#58;12-15
We will inherit promises made unto us as we patiently endure (doing things) all circumstances through faith. 
Some people labor and pray but are not patient enough. When the angel that bears the promise finally arrives, they have already abandoned the project or have left the position. This is not for me. I will wait at the place of the promise until my change comes. 

The things we see as challenges are there to make us wiser and stronger. Let's endeavor to learn from the challenges of our lives.
