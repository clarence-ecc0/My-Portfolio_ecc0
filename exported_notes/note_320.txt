Grace means&#58;
(a) God's Redemption(Riches) At Christ Expense
The redemption that I got was at the expense of Christ.

(b) God Response Abundantly, Completely, Exceedingly 

Titus 2&#58;11-15; 
As soon as grace comes to us, he teaches us that now, we have the power to abandon ungodliness and worldly lust, and to live righteously, godly and soberly in our communities, our place of business and in all places.



Point-1&#58;The privileged partakers of God's grace

Titus 2&#58;11; 2 Corinthians 6&#58;1-2,17-18; Hebrews 2&#58;9,10,14-15
In the day of salvation (today), the Lord succoured (helped, relieved) me. 

Point-2&#58; The present products of God's grace
(I am a product of heaven and heaven is proud of me. My salvation is not different from the salvation that Peter, Paul and all the other saints had.)

Titus 2&#58;12
Upon salvation, I entered a new world. In this present world, I have denied ungodliness and worldly lust and have accepted godliness, righteousness and sobriety. I now live like God in His holiness and have the ability to live right. Before grace entered my life, when ungodliness knocked at the door or unrighteousness, I did not have the power to say 'NO'. Now grace has come and I can say to both, a loud 'NO'
Sobriety entered me and I no longer enjoyed dirty jokes or laughing at such jokes. 
There is something higher than deliverance and that is dominion. I have dominion in Jesus name. 

Acts 11&#58;23
It is possible to see grace. How? The same way we see the air and that is by its action

GRACE
When I see GENTLENESS; when I see RESTFULNESS; when I see ATTITUDE TO PROBLEMS; when I see COMPORTMENT; and when I see ENABLEMENT in a person/people, I  have seen the grave of God in the person or people. 


Point-3&#58; The peculiar people of God's grace 

In peculiar people or places, when there is darkness all around, there is light around the people. It happened to the children of Israel in Egypt. 

Titus 2&#58;14
Immediately I became a peculiar person (by my salvation), I became zealous of good work. Preaching is a good work.
