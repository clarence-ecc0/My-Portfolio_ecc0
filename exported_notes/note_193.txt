*CHECK THE SCRIPTURES YOURSELF* 

*Theme&#58;* _Believe to see and not wait to see before believing_


Jesus saith unto her, Said I not unto thee, that, if thou *Wouldest Believe,* thou *Shouldest See* the glory of God?
John 11&#58;40

This Bible reference is the direct word of Christ. The summary of the verse is, those that will believe first are the ones that should see what they believe in God for. 

The degenerate mind doesn't work this way. It's conviction is, seeing (first) is believing (later); when I see, I will believe. The whole of science is built on evidence, proofing; "seeing is believing".

Many will sincerely say, but I believed and yet did not see anything. 

#1. Believing is active, not passive. The story was told of a community that went to the Town square to pray to God to send them rain. Only a small boy went to that prayer with an umbrella expecting God to give the rain. 

#2. God has His calendar, His appointed time to fulfill His promises. Abraham lived for 25 years expecting Isaac throughout those years, not knowing God's calendar concert the promise. This troubled him and Sarah. In response to his Sarah's laughter when God said she would have a son *according to the time of life [God's calendar&#93;,* God told him,

_"Is any thing too hard for the LORD? *At the time appointed* I will return unto thee, *according to the time of life,* and Sarah shall have a son."_
*Genesis 18&#58;14*

*_When was Abraham's promise fulfilled?_*
At the appointed time, for the Holy Scripture has this to say,

For Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, *at the set time of which God had spoken to him.*
*Genesis 21&#58;2*

Beloved, God has His appointed time to fulfill the promises He has in the Holy Scriptures for those that actively believe Him and are expecting the fulfillment.

*_Trust in His faithfulness, Expect the fulfillment of His promises, Wait for His appointed time_*

*Shalom*
