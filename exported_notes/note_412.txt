TOPIC&#58; THE PLACE OF WISDOM IN MAN'S LIFE AND DESTINY

James 3&#58;13-16
The world around us must see our wisdom though our meekness. There is no pride in true wisdom
The earthly (devilish) wisdom cannot take us to heaven
Point-1&#58; The essence and discernment of excellent, spiritual , divine wisdom

Ecclesiastes 8&#58;5; Deuteronomy 4&#58;6-8
Without knowledge there is no wisdom because in reality, wisdom is applied knowledge. If we have knowledge and do not apply it to our living, we have no wisdom. Therefore our lives will be applied knowledge and people will see it.
Wisdom demands discernment and this is helped by the knowledge we have of the word of God.

What differentiated the children of Israel from all the gentile nations? It is the word of God, even their wisdom as they applied it. The wise Christian and the unwise Christian is determined by their applications of the word of God. If I apply the commandments, the precepts of God's word in my daily activities, I am a man of wisdom. Are you born again but are not applying God's word, you are not wise.

Mathew 7&#58;24-25; Ecclesiastes 7&#58;11-13
Whosoever does what he has heard from Christ, Christ will liken him to a wise man. 

Whatever we have from our parents (genetics, materials, etc.) or from the promise of God in the scriptures, we need wisdom so that the inheritance can be profitable. 

Jeremiah 9&#58;12-15; 1 Corinthians 1&#58;20-24,30
The wise man is one to whom the mouth of the Lord has spoken to. There is no other wisdom outside the word of God. Some people look at the imagination of their own heart (those that construct gods of wood and metals use the imagination of their own hearts). 
The world by its earthly (worldly) wisdom cannot know God through it. The Jews and the Greeks that pursue wisdom but turn their back on the Bible may have gotten a type of wisdom that is from beneath and sensual. 

Colossians 3&#58;15-17
If Christ is in us, we will have the peace of God in our hearts. anti-Christ comes to take peace away from the world. There is no trial, situations on earth that can come to a believer that he cannot overcome because the word of God will give him wisdom from the Psalms he reads, the spiritual songs he sings.


Point-2&#58;  The exemplary deeds of enlightened supportive distinguished wisdom 

James 3&#58;13
Who is a wise man?  Am I wise (in my interactions, conversation, what I share with people of the opposite sex, in my dressing, etc.)? My wisdom should show in my conversation, deeds, conduct amongst people with the meekness of wisdom. 

Ecclesiastes 10&#58;10,12; 1 Kings 3&#58;9; 11&#58;1-8, 9
To make decisions (career, family life, business investment, etc.), we need the wisdom of Christ (His word, will, way) to direct us profitably. The things we hear in the church or we get from studying the Bible should guide us in the time of decision making. 

Solomon asked for wisdom to judge God's people aright. He received the wisdom he asked for but, because he loves many strange women, they caused him to misapply the knowledge he had contrary to God's word, will and way. Beauty is vain, be not unequally yoked with strange women, etc. King Solomon disregarded all these. Solomon used his wisdom to direct other people but did not to direct himself. 

God had declared that anyone that goes into the strange women, will be SURELY turned away in their hearts. Solomon knew this but could not direct his own life. 
The key question is, am I applying the wisdom of God to others (e.g., my children) and not to myself? 

In Ecclesiastes chapter 2 Solomon said after drinking various types of wine, and sampling various types of wine, 

Romans 16&#58;19
You can't conquer Satan by using his wisdom. If Eve could not conquer the serpent after taking the counsel of the serpent, how do we think we can use the wisdom of satan to overcome him?  

Point-3&#58;  The evidence and deadlines of earthly, sensual, and devilish wisdom

James 3&#58;13-15
The wisdom of Joab and the wise woman was what drove David from the throne through Absalom. How did Joab feel at the end when he realized his worldly wisdom drove David from the throne. The same Joab killed Absalom contrary to king David's command. The point here is that it is not all wisdom that comes from God. 

Disqualification by bitterness
Ahitophel was a close relative of Bathsheba. He was bitter because of what David did to Uriah. It was so easy for him to join himself to Absalom. Any wisdom coming from bitterness, retaliation, is not good for our church. May the Lord turn such counsel to foolishness. Ahitophel committed suicide. With his wisdom, he could not direct himself to repair his relationship with God. He went to hell straight.

Dishonesty in envy- Philippians 1&#58;15-16
Any wisdom coming from envy or strife is not from God. It is earthly, sensual and devilish 

Depravity in strife- Philippians 2&#58;3
Let preaching not be done with strife and vain glory; HCF coordination, etc be done with vain glory. God doesn't want us to get into competition.
