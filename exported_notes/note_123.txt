{{text:TOPIC&#58; PASSIONATE IMPORTUNATE PRAYER FOR PURE PRACTICAL WISDOM
James 1&#58;5-6
There is human wisdom, satanic wisdom and spiritual wisdom. The Bible is talking of spiritual wisdom, the wisdom that is from above. It is pure, peaceable, gentle and easy to be entreated. This wisdom is full of mercy. We do not have the spiritual wisdom naturally. It is the Spirit of God that brings it into our hearts


Point-1&#58; Perceiving the wisdom we all lack
James 1&#58;5
a. The wisdom for workmanship beyond natural wisdom
The work we are called into as God's children is beyond what our natural wisdom can accomplish 
Exodus 31&#58;3-5 ; 1 Corinthians 2&#58;7
The princes of this world do not know (understand , appreciate) the spiritual wisdom from God- the wisdom of maintaining peace & of being 
easily entreated when they are hurt, of purity no matter what, of showing mercy when the goings get tough. It is strange to them.

b. The weapon of wisdom above native wisdom
1 Kings 2&#58;6,9; 3&#58;5-9; Ecclesiastes 2&#58;1-3,9
Solomon had native wisdom which David exploited to deal with Shimei and Joab. It destroys people, it wasn't for forgiveness or promoting peace and not ready to be entreated. It was not from God.

Our native wisdom is what we use in our profession. When Solomon married all those women, his spiritual wisdom (he asked God and received it) departed from him and he reverted back to his native wisdom that enables him to destroy Joab and Shimei. He lost self-control and gave in to whatever that he desired- his native, natural wisdom gave them to him. We are mostly like this and we need to pray to grow beyond this fleshly wisdom that satisfies only the flesh.


c. The wealth of wisdom higher than the neighbors wisdom
The spiritual wisdom we have (if we already have it)  is higher than the wisdom of our neighbors. The exhortation for peace, mercy, etc that we have from the word of God,  our neighbors do not have it. 
Deuteronomy 4&#58;6; Proverbs 14&#58;1-2; 31&#58;26
We build our lives, church, homes with the spiritual wisdom from the Lord. 


Point-2&#58; Praying for wisdom from the Lord
James 1&#58;5
We may not lack the wisdom for our enterprise but when it comes to living peaceably, we need the wisdom from God.

a. Search for wisdom in the world 
Job 28&#58;11-20,28
The wisdom described in Job 28 that was being sought after is not native wisdom but spiritual wisdom. It can't be gotten from the land of the living. This is the wisdom Christ came from heaven to give us, not the native, natural wisdom that does not propel us towards godliness 
1 Corinthians 1&#58;22-24,30

If someone does not have Christ living in him, he may have native wisdom but cannot not have spiritual wisdom


b. The supplication for wisdom without wavering 
James 1&#58;5-6; Luke 21&#58;15
If we have wisdom that enables us to succeed in our enterprises but we are still violent physically or in our mind,we are not easy to be entreated, are not peaceable, we still lack spiritual wisdom and should pray for it. We can't see God without spiritual wisdom
The wisdom that Christ promised to give us in Luke 21&#58;15 is not the wisdom of winning argument through native intelligence but of winning souls through peace, mercy, purity, love, easy entreatability. 

c.  The supremacy of His wisdom above the world
Colossians 2&#58;3,8; 2 Timothy 3&#58;14-15
What God considers as true wisdom is a personality that is peaceable, easy to be entreated, gentle and pure. This is the wisdom that Christ has. A wisdom that does not make us wise unto salvation is not from God and it is not good enough.

Point-3&#58; Possession of His wisdom in our lives
James 1&#58;5! Colossians 4&#58;5-6
If as a minister, your wisdom does not lead you to admonish for peace among those that are warring in your congregation, you do not have the wisdom of God. Knowing the verses of the Bible and not praying that those verses will be acted out in your life is not wisdom. Knowing many verses of the Bible is not wisdom. It is the transformation that those verses cause in you (peacefulness , easy entreatability, mercy, etc ) that is your wisdom.

a. Begin with the wisdom of the Lord for a lifestyle
Job 28&#58;28; Micah 6&#58;8-
Begin with the wisdom that makes you fear God and eschew evil. That is the greatest wisdom. Pursue this and get it. 

b. Beware of the wisdom of the lost with looseness
James 3&#58;14-16
Any wisdom that brings envy into your heart and bitterness is devilish and is not from God. It is the wisdom of those who are lost. You need to allow yourself to be found in Christ.


c. Behave in the wisdom of love with liberality 
James 3&#58;17
The wisdom that makes us love, merciful, makes those that interact with us to be purer than before is from above, from God. Whatever situation we are in, God wants us to be peaceable. The wisdom that makes you not to love some people  (e.g., they did evil things in the past, etc.) but tells you why you should hate them is devilish, sensual. The wisdom that makes people fear you, to  manipulate people is not of God.
