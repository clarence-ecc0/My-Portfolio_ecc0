{{text:TOPIC&#58; THE FAITH JOURNEY INTO REVIVAL
Habakkuk 3&#58;2; Number 10&#58;29
There is personal revival, people's revival, pastor/prophet's revival; practical revival.
Moses realized they were on a journey. Every Christian needs to realize that he is on a journey, 'The Pilgrim's Progress', journeying to the Celestial city.

REVIVAL
R_Run the race. 
Hebrews 12&#58;1-2
Many of the Israelites did not reach the end of the race because of the sin that did easily beset them.
The sin of Achan beset Israel, the sin of murmuring, immorality, etc. hindered Israel in its journey
As we run the race, we should lay aside the sins of Achan, Absalom and Ahab; the sins of Solomon, Saul and Samson.

E_Expel the enemy
As we run towards the place (God's celestial city), we must expel the enemy. 

Deuteronomy 18&#58;9
There are abominations in many nations, many colleagues , many neighbors; in their lifestyles. We must expel them (the abominations) from our homes and lives.

V_ Vigilance against the vagabond 
Satan told God, I have been up and down, to and from upon the face of the earth. He is a vagabond, looking for people to destroy. The Lord wants us to be vigilant against Satan and his gang. 

1 Peter 5&#58;7-8
To reach the end of our journey, we must be vigilant against the vagabond (Satan). Resist him. You tell a dog to go back and it wags the tail and follows you still hoping you will change your mind. You need to be stern against it, shout at it and it will heed the command and go back. That's how to get Satan away from you. You do not need to handle him with kid's glove. 

Satan does not walk in the open, does not announce, "Here I am, I am Satan". He comes to people through instruments. He tries to make us believe in superstition, turn us away from Christ. 

I_ Interceding without iniquity
If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me. Hatred, animosity, unforgiveness, works of the flesh, etc are iniquity. The Lord cannot hear us with these even if we pray and pray.

Job 22&#58;21-28
As we are journeying for revival, we must intercede for the revival without iniquity

V_ Vivify the vision 
Make your vision of revival vivid.
Habakkuk 2&#58;2
A vision that can be fulfilled must be vivid, written down and made plain. Anyone that reads your plain vivid vision will be compelled to run with it, to accomplish it.
Revival is coming to my family. I make it plain and vivid to them so that they can run with it. 

A_ Abiding under the Almighty 
Psalms 91&#58;1-3,4-16
Psalms 91 was written when Israel was in the wilderness and there were dangerous people that could and wanted to destroy them. It is the same for us today as we proceed on our revival journey. There are satanic people in the wilderness of this world whom Satan has persuaded to destroy us. It is only safe when we are abiding under the Almighty.
He is my God on Sunday and even from Monday to Friday when I go to work, He remains my God.

L_ Live always in the light
Live your life as if you are under the sunlight, you are in the open and every person passing by can see you, and there is nothing to be ashamed of.

John 8&#58;12, 39-32, 36
All the Israelites knew there was a promised land they were going to but many of them did not reach there. They didn't run the race, they did not expel the enemy, they were not vigilant against the vagabond, they were Interceding with iniquity, they did not vivify the vision, they did not abide under the Almighty and did not live always in the light (Achan had what he had to hide).
