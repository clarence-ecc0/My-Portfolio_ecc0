{{text:Stakeholder management
Problem statement&#58; Project funders have an understandable desire to see the sustainability of their long term objectives where they expended their funds. Effective stakeholder management is crucial to not only successful project implementation but also the sustainability of the project gains at the exit of the implementer/funder. 

What was done&#58; In all the projects Howard University has worked on in Nigeria, it's effective engagement with key stakeholders has been instrumental to its successes. Howard engages with regulators on pharmacy services notably, the pharmacy council of Nigeria, the National Agency for Food and Drug, Adminiyand Control (NAFDAC) and, Food and Drugs department of the Federal Ministry of Health on projects involving pharmacy practice and drug products. It equally deliberates with the government of Nigeria supervisors (directors of pharmaceutical services) that exercise oversight on rational medication use at the tertiary, secondary and primary care facilities. 

Howard has had an enviable working relationship with the pharmaceutical society of Nigeria, the umbrella pharmacy professional association in the country, along with the various technical associations (the ACPN and AHAPN) as well as key technical interest groups such as the Association of Lady Pharmacists (ALPS). 

Achievements&#58; Howard has overall, trained ... health care workers in Nigeria across the three tiers of health care. This includes ... Hospital pharmacists, .... Community pharmacists, and pharmacy support staff. Many of the multi-disciplinary health care workers training it conducted includes doctors, nurses,medical records staff, and laboratory staff.

Howard has worked with the GON regulators from inception of its projects in Nigeria in the development of concepts and materials for improved medication access and use in Nigeria (e.g., the COPA initiaitve for improved community pharmacy practice; MCPD training modules for continuous pharmacists' education; tiered training modules for PMVs for increased access to medications in the communities, etc.)
