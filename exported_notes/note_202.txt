*CHECK THE SCRIPTURES YOURSELF* 

            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _Why some believers are afraid_

Imagine a Hen sitting over its chicks, preserving them from harm. They are supposed to be there resting or sleeping, unafraid of any flying Hawk above. It will be unthinkable for the chicks under the cover of the mother Hen to be worried about a Hawk flying above.

But our God is greater than the Hen. He has covered us, His children under His feathers. Why then are God's children under His feathers still afraid of demons, and witches, and witchcraft, flying about in the sky? It is senseless. 

This, here is the promise of our Faithful Father&#58;
_"He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust&#58; his truth shall be thy shield and buckler."_  *Psalms 91&#58;4*

Why are God's children still afraid?
I will tell you the possible reasons. 
i. They may have abandoned His shield and buckler - the truth. They may have been speaking lies to their customers, employers, neighbors, wives or children. It's no wonder then because the guilty are always afraid.

ii. They may have abandoned the admonition that counselled, "under His wing shalt thou trust [have faith&#93;." They have lost faith in the Everliving One because they threw away his shield and buckler and ca no longer dwell under His wings, in His presence.

But as many as are holding onto the truth (the shield and the buckler), are constantly searching for it; as many as are obeying the injunction, _"Buy the truth and sell it not,"_ they shall remain under the wings of they Lord and trust it. *They shall not be afraid.*
