{{text:TOPIC&#58; THE IMMEASURABLE AND THE MEASURELESS, INFINITE GRACE THAT BROUGHT SALVATION TO MAN
There was salvation in old times for people like Enoch, David, Noah; Christ came and the people at His time had salvation as well like the apostles, etc. After Christ rose from the dead, we have salvation as many of us as believed upon His name.
Some people want whitewashed salvation like the Pharisees.  The salvation of the gospel time is the type that Christ referred to, "go and sin no more". 
1 Peter 1&#58;9-11

Point-1&#58; Calvary, fathomless grace that grants the penitent forgiveness 
A child who took a piece of meat from the pot and the mum says, what's that in your mouth and all he can say is, I am sorry" and kept on chewing the meat was not really sorry. This is similar to the salvation of some people who continue to live in their sin and keep saying, "I am sorry". They are not really sorry. What kind of salvation do you have? 

Acts 5&#58;30-
Any sacrifice that is done today for salvation is not of the Lord because it attempts to nullify the salvation that God Himself provided through the sacrifice of His Son. 
It is the grace of God that taught us to fear God.
A man has fallen into the well and asks for anyone that can save him. It cannot be someone in the same well like him, someone at his level; it must be someone above the well. All of humanity was in the well of sin. Only Christ was up above the well and able to save souls in the well. The man saved out the well (of sin) can no longer be living in the well of sin. A saved soul cannot continue to be a sinner.  But when someone in the well has been saved, he is up above and can be sent by Christ with the tool to draw people out of the well (God's word) to pull others out of their well of sin and as many as hold onto that rope, they can be pulled out of the well. 
Acts 4&#58;12; Isaiah 55&#58;6


Point-2&#58; The costly free grace that gives the punishable freedom 
Those that go back to their sin after they were forgiven and come back again and again, committing the same sin and asking for forgiveness do not know the cost of their salvation. Christ valued our soul so much that He paid the price to procure us. 

It is appointed onto man that once after death, there is judgment waiting. When we come before the Lord, daddy and mummy cannot save us, if we rejected Christ from saving us while we were alive on earth. 

Romans 3&#58;23-24; 10&#58;9
All men are short of the glory of God. No man's hand can touch the heavens if he raises his hand. Only Jesus Christ can lift us up, and bring us (men sons) to the glory. 
In primary and secondary school, they teach religious knowledge. If you just know it in the head but not in the heart, you are not saved. The knowledge that Christ was God's Son, sinless and sent to die for my son is what saves a soul. 
My salvation has come


Point-3&#58; Crucifying full grace that guarantees a peace in fullness

The salvation the Lord provided for us is full. There is nothing that can be added to it to make it full. 
There is the peace of God that comes to us (when we give our lives to Christ) from the God of peace. 
Roman 16&#58;20; 1 Thessalonians 5&#58;23,24. 

When you want to go somewhere (or do something) but discover that you are going somewhere (or doing something) else, it is the arrows of the enemy. The Lord inactivates all the arrows of the enemy directed against me this day in Jesus name. The faithful God will do it for me in Jesus name today, giving me authority over demonic arrows in Jesus name. 
I have heard and I am going to receive.
