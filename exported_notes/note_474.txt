*Theme&#58;* _His Children_

There are great and precious promises for the children of God in the Holy Bible. This exhortation is specifically for *His Children* who are waiting because they yet to inherit some of His promises. 

Even when a situation appears hopeless, *His Children* should not be hopeless because, *_faithful is He that promised who also will do it_*

There is no possibility of failure in God's promises because&#58;

✓ *He is faithful.* He cannot forget or ignore His promises to *His Children.* He even promised that He will watch over His promises (words) to fulfill them.

✓ *He is mighty.* Mightier than all obstacles to the fulfillment of His promises to *His Children*

✓ *He is immutable.* There is nothing that can cause Him to change His promises to *His Children*

✓ *He is eternal.* The challenges of *His Children* are temporary but He is eternal, the Everliving One. He will take out the challenges of *His Children* at the appointed time if they remain as *His Children.*

✓ *He is Omnipresent.* He is everywhere and is right  there with *His Children* where they are facing some temporary challenges.

✓ *He is Omniscient.* He knows fully, the cause and the solution to *His Children's* challenges.

✓ *He is Omnipotent.* He has all the power to do all He has promised to *His Children.*


Are you one of *His Children?* Listen to the counsel of the Holy Ghost in *Hebrews 6&#58;12* and, take note of three key expressions there.


_That ye *be not slothful,* but followers of them who through *faith* and *patience* inherit the promises._
*Hebrews 6&#58;12*
The counsels are, we need to&#58;
✓ be hardworking (*not slothful*)
✓ hold onto our *faith* that God is faithful to fulfill His promises.
✓ have *patience* while waiting to inherit the promises.
