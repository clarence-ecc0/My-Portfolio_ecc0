“I WILL NEVER LEAVE THEE”

Text&#58; Hebrews 13&#58;5; Deuteronomy 31&#58;1-6 

Naturally, human beings worry about the future. However, with the reassuring promise from God to
never leave us, believers can step into the future with full assurance of victory and success. We can
safely lean upon His words, knowing that He will not lie and we shall receive strength for the journey
ahead. 

WARNING AGAINST COVETOUSNESS 
Due to concern for what the future holds, believers may be tempted to desire and accumulate
material wealth like sinning friends, colleagues, neighbours and relatives. However, we are warned,
“Let your conversation be without covetousness”. We must not let greed for insatiable possessions
prevent us from progressing in our Christian journey. 

WALKING WITH CONTENTMENT 
The journey ahead of believers is one that requires us to ”be content”. Contentment is one of the most essential needs in today’s competitive world. Contentment is a feeling of satisfaction with what
you have and who you are. It is also trusting in what God has said.

When we are assaulted by hardship, we should remember that God’s love for us remains as
constant as ever and His thought is “to give (us) an expected end” (Jeremiah 29&#58;11). In all
circumstances of life, whether good or bad, God is always near to help, succour, bless and cheer us.

WAYFARING WITH COURAGE 
As believers, we must learn to trust God. He knows how much strength we possess and will never
leave us to struggle through our own. With the presence of the Lord with us, we can face the challenges of life without fear; “for the Lord thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee,
nor forsake thee”. Therefore, we should demonstrate courage every step of the way.
