Mathew 16&#58;18
The key of the church is in our hand and we are going to defeat every Goliath in Jesus name.

I. To defeat Israel and make them a failure. 
2. To decrease them

Every Goliath seeks this against the Israel of God. It is a battle of the heart.

Point-3&#58; Our watchfulness against a more deadly Goliath
We have a more deadly Goliath that faces the people of God in Israel and even today. We will see this even in the life of David.  

Romans 8&#58;37
We are more than conquerors through him that loved us
The greatest enemy is represented by the name, GOLIATH

G = Greed.
1 Samuel 27&#58;8-12; Isaiah 56&#58;10-11
David was greedy and destroyed others to take their materials. He stole, killed and lied to Achish. There are people that do what David did out of greed. This is the deadly Goliath that Satan has raised to defeat many & many are they that are playing daily into his hand

O= Offence
1 Chronicles 21&#58;1, 7-8; 14-
Satan sneaked into the life of David to provoke him to sin. So Satan by his sneaky means killed about 70,000 people of Israel.
Mathew 13&#58;41
If we conquer physical Goliath & are unable to stay from sin (e.g. pride), it will harm us more.

L= Lust
1 Peter 2&#58;11; Proverbs 6&#58;25-29, 32
Lust in a man wars against his soul. Satan used this as another deadly weapon against David. David through adultery was destroying his own soul

I= Idleness 
Proverbs 24&#58;30-31; 2 Samuel 11&#58;1
David tarried at Jerusalem idling away & entered Satan's trap. If he had gone to the war, what happened with Bathsheba wouldn't have happened. 
Idleness truly makes a man's heart to become Satan's workshop 

A= Anger
2 Samuel 12&#58;5; Mathew 7&#58;1-5; Ecclesiastes 7&#58;9
Those that are unable to control their temper will be so easily moved by Satan to enter trouble. The judgment David gave was what was meted to him in return. David who would steal another's wife, killed the man through his own hand has no qualifications to judge another who just took another's lamb


T= Transgression 
Psalms 51&#58;3-13; Proverbs 15&#58;16
He that goes away from the commandments of God exposes himself to Satan. David's offence, lust, Idleness,anger, transgression exposed him to satanic attack. He lost over 70,000 men.


H= Hypocrisy 
2 Samuel 12&#58;1,5; Romans 2&#58;1- ; Romans 2&#58;21- ; 
David could condemn a man that took a lamb wrongly after he had taken a man's wife and killed him. He went to the throne of grace & was forgiven.
We should not worry so much about the physical Goliath that confronts us. We can easily bring that one down. We should rather worry more about the more deadly Goliath, the problem of greed, offence, lust, Idleness, anger, transgression and hypocrisy.
May the Lord save me from every Goliath in Jesus name.
