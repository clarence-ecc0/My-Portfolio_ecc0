TOPIC&#58; THE NOTABLE POSSIBILITIES IN THE NAME OF JESUS
John 14&#58;14-15

Point-1&#58; The mighty, powerful name of Jesus 
Mathew 1&#58;21; Acts 4&#58;10-11.

No man can save himself because sin, as bondage has tied him up - his hands, his feet's and even his voice cannot speak for him. He needs a Savior who is a yoke-breaker to set him free from the bondage of sin.
Check your life; have you set aside the living stone, the sure foundation and are trying to build a house without the foundation for the Lord? It cannot be done. 


Point-2&#58; The mediating, propitiating name of Jesus
1 Timothy 2&#58;3-6; 1 John 2&#58; 1-2,12

Christ is the only mediator between man and God who can bring man back to God. God does not reject the mediating role of Christ between Him and the sinful, offensive man.
Despite our many sins, Christ, the Mediator, is taking us to heaven if only we will listen to Him and observe all the things He has commanded us. 
Do you have a testimony that Jesus is your Savior?

Point-3&#58; The merciful provisions through the name of Jesus 
1 Peter 2&#58;1-2,10; Hebrews 7&#58;25

We do not come to Christ physically by walking to Him, but through our hearts by surrendering to Him.
