Wherever we find ourselves in life, God wants us to use the opportunity to glorify Him. That's exactly what Christ did at the wedding here. Jesus at the marriage of Cana is a divine confirmation that God is satisfied with the marriage institution.

Marriage relationship will be joyful and fulfilling if Jesus is there as a guest. But Christ cannot be there if the environment is not conducive to the Holy Spirit, hence the need to keep our tabernacle cleansed.

We must have implicit confidence in God that He can solve all of our problems. Mary had that kind of confidence in Christ and she wasn't disappointed. 

Wine is for merriment. We need wine in our marriages. Because the wine can run out, we must have Christ in our marriages always so that we will always have wine in it. 

In marriages, what is our role as guest? Do what Mary, the mother of Christ did. Did you find a challenge in the marriage you attended (the home you visited), find a way to solve the problems, intervene, offer counsels and if you discover Christ is not there, invite Him there. 

After Christ left the temple that He cleansed, it was eventually destroyed. As 1 Corinthians 3&#58;16 says, every temple that is defiled will be destroyed. The judgment is still the same.
