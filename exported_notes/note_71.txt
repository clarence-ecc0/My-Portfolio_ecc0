{{text:TOPIC&#58; RECEIVING AND RETAINING GOD'S GRACE THROUGH LIVING FAITH, NOT DEAD FAITH

Ephesians 2&#58;8-10; James 2&#58;14-17

Point-1&#58; Transforming grace through definite decisive faith
"I am a sinner by grace" is a fallacy. Nobody needs grace to be a sinner. 

Titus 2&#58;11-14l



a. Transforming grace through trusting faith

Titus 2&#58;11-
The Bible said the grace that provide salvation has appeared to all men. It did not say it was accepted by all men.  Grace teaches, instructs us. Sinners do not accept the instructions of grace and therefore although grace appeared to them, they rejected it and allowed it leave them. Those that possess grace do not live careless life. Grace doesn't permit this. 
Grace makes (transforms) us to do restitutions even without any external compulsion. 




b. Transparent godliness through tenacious faith
Hebrews 10&#58;22-23; 2 Peter 1&#58;3-4, 5-6; 3&#58;11
The life a true believer lives is not according to his human power but according to as the divine power has enabled him. That divine power has given unto us all things that pertain to life and godliness and has made us partakers of the divine nature ( possessors of the divine nature do not steal, live promiscuous lives, etc.). 


c. Transcending goodness under tried faith
As our faith is being tried, we are increasing in goodness !good deeds), we go beyond expectations, that is transcending grace. 
Faith if it hath no love, long suffering, intemperate, gentleness,meekness, etc, it is dead.


Point-2&#58; Trampled grace by dead, deadening faith

James 2&#58;17; Jude 1&#58;4

a. Misunderstood grace by nominal church goers
James 2&#58;19; Hosea 8&#58;3, 12
They go to church and chant, they believe in God yet, their lives are completely opposed to the way of God. 


b. Misusing grace for non-christian behavior 
1 Corinthians 15&#58;2

c. Misapplied grace by natural beasts
Jude 1&#58;4-13

We should not misunderstand, misapply and misuse grace.



Point-3&#58; Triumphing grac through distinct distinguishing faith

Romans 5;1-3; 1 Timothy 1&#58;14

a. Overcoming the world and worldliness by grace through faith 

1 John 5&#58;4-5; 
The thing that overcomes the world is our faith in Christ. Christ as the Son of God into the world so that He can lead children of men to heaven as sons of God. 

James 4&#58;4
When a bride of Christ leaves Him and loves the world (gives to the world what he should have given only to Christ), he/she is an adulteress or an adulterer. 
1 John 2&#58;15
All that is in the world (festivals, ceremonies, partying, pride of life, etc.) is not of God but of the world. He that loves them hates God.

b. Overpowering our weakness and willfulness by grace and faith
Our willfulness can only be overcome through grace.  Peter loved Christ and he believed that by his great willfulness, he could overcome Satan. He failed.

c. Overturning others wandering
 in the wilderness by grace through faith.
Caleb and Joshua through grace believed that they could overcome the enemies but the others were prone to wandering in the wilderness because of lack of faith. I have made up my mind that I will not be a wanderer like others. 

Proverbs 21&#58;16; Psalms 119&#58;10-11
Those that wander away from understanding (God's commandments) will remain in the congregation of the dead. It can happen to those in Deeper Life that are wandering away from the great things of His laws.
