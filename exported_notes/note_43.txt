SPREADING THE WORD AND THE WONDERS OF FULL REDEMPTION 

Point-1&#58; The promise of full redemption by the Lord
Luke 1&#58;72-79; 

a. The undeniable promise of thorough redemption
Psalm 130&#58;7-8; Titus 2&#58;17; 2 Timothy 2&#58;13; Heb 6&#58;18

b. The unlimited promise of total redemption 
Jeremiah 15&#58;21; Exodus 6&#58;6-7; Psalm 78&#58;22, 41; 

The house that the Lord has built for me, no "Mr Terrible" can force me out of it. When we run away because of evil powers (around us, our properties), we are actually limiting the Holy One of Israel, we are declaring that He is unable to protect us or defend our cause. 

c. The unwavering promise of time-tested redemption 

Roman 3&#58;24-26; Colossians 1&#58;13
Christ is the Just that justifies us, redeemed us, acquit us.
It is erroneous to believe that a man that is saved still has devils in him. If it be so then, he wasn't really saved. Holding this notion is an error and is contrary to the doctrine of Christ. To keep on talking about Satan is popularizing him, a popularity he does not deserve. 

I have to develop a new vocabulary that talks more about the grace of God, the power I have in Christ, the promises I have in the scriptures and to claim them and believe by faith that they have been given to me; I need to talk less about Satan and devils and demons. As I check the scriptures, I really cannot see Christ talking much about Satan, neither did the apostles or the early believers. With all the havoc Satan wrecked on the church in the wilderness, he was rarely mentioned in the old testament. I didn't hear Moses, Joshua, Abraham mention him once even though he fought against them. This is a lesson for me. 


Point-2&#58; The preaching of the four-fold redemption by His laborers
1 Corinthians 3&#58;9-1; 2 Corinthians 6&#58;1
If we are truly working together with the Lord, we must involve Him in the work. That's why we must pray for His grace and commit the success of whatever work we are doing unto Him. If we are truly working together with God, we can't be walking together with a backslider without talking to them of the urgent necessity of reconciliation with God because, that is the key thing that God, our Partner we claim we are working with will want addressed immediately.

a. Preach redemption from the pollution of sin 
Isaiah 1&#58;15-18; 1 Peter 1&#58;18-19; 2 Peter 2&#58;20

"Cease to do evil...; learn to do well" is a command that must be obeyed. 
Some training we acquire in our practice may make us deceptive, tell lies, or do things that are not acceptable in the sight of the Lord. We must learn, strive to drop them off. 

b. Preaching redemption from pleasure of sin
Roman 1&#58;28-32; 

Our redemption takes our interest away from the pleasure of sin
If we invent evil things (including technology, fashion, way of life, etc.) in the world, the degenerate life is still in us and we are not saved. 

c. Preaching redemption from the power of sin
Romans 6&#58;6-8,11-12; 1 John 3&#58;5-10

If I am unable to dissociate myself from a particular habit, it implies that thing has power over me. Christ was manifested to take away our sins including such habits. If sin remains in me, it means I have not allowed Christ to do His perfect work in me because He has the power and willingness to do it. The pleasure of that sin may be what is holding me captive, restraining Christ from intervening. 
If I am still committing sin, I am of the devil. This will not be my portion in Jesus name. 
Whosoever is born of God does not commit sin. This should be the #1 priority in the life of every believer. 

d. Preaching redemption from the plague of sickness 
James 5&#58;14-16

Holiness & forgiveness are more important than healing and deliverance from sickness. As we seek the later, let us not take our eyes from the former.


Point-3&#58; The partakers of finished redemption with liberation 
John 19&#58;30

a. The triumphant possessors of faultless redemption 
I John 5&#58;4-5 ,18; 2 Peter 1&#58;3-8

When evil, sickness, etc comes to touch you, as a sinless child of God, take your stand and resist them. They can't succeed if you stand on your kingdom right. 

b. The timeless proclaimers of fruit-bearing redemption 
Acts 20&#58;26-28

c. The timely propagation of the finished redemption 
Mark 16&#58;15-20; 

If anyone decides to give you a poisonous thing that will terminate your life, he is fighting Christ, and trying to contradict Him.
