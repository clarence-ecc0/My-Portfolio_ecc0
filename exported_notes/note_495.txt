{{text:Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin.
*Romans 6&#58;6*

There are certain things we must first understand for the text above to have meaning for us .
✓ The OLD MAN is our degenerated, corrupted SPIRIT-MAN, the generator of sinful thoughts and passions. It is not just our flesh. That was what was crucified with Christ at baptism.
✓ Death means separation from something that is corrupted. The *First Death* means separation of humans from one another. For instance , a man will separate his beloved wife from himself and allow her to be buried beneath the earth. A woman will also accept that for her only child. This is the first death;, the physical death that we are very much familiar with.
✓ The *Second Death* means separation between the man and the God that created him. This refers to the Spirit-Man in us and it is similar to the way God separated Lucifer and one-third of the angels in heaven from Himself because of their rebellion.
