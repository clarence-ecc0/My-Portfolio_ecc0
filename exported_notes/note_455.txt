"So likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses."

A man that is bitter against another man & unwilling to forgive is like one drinking poison and expecting the other person to die. All the adverse effects of bitterness/anger (high BP,  lowered immunity, headache, stress, etc) are experienced by the angry man, not the person he is angry with. 

I read once the story of a man with his daughter.
It so happened that the man raped his daughter and this provoked the daughter so much that she hated her father mortally, with the deepest passion. 
In the course of time, the father ran mad and he was placed in a MENTAL home. The daughter refused to visit him because of her great hatred.

In the course of time, the daughter became deformed, bent over and lived her life in pain, bitterness and hatred. The rape by the father was a great sin but so also was the wrath or bitterness of the daughter which generated unforgiveness. 

The situation of the Father and daughter continued for a long time until one day, the daughter decided to go and see the father at the mental people's home.

When she saw her father, she had pity on his state and forgave him in her heart. The father looked into the eyes of the daughter and saw mercy, forgiveness in them and his madness went away, but that was not the only miracle for the day. The stunted deformity of the daughter was also healed.

If you look at this story very well, you will see the fingerprint of Satan. Satan instigated the Father to rape his own daughter and, who knows , possibly, Satan may have instigated the daughter to live a careless life of seductive dressing at home. This may have provoked the lust in the Father. Certainly, the bitterness and unforgiveness of the daughter was from Satan. 
This father and daughter were both slaves of Satan and were held bound in his prison for a long time. Both were sinners before God and suffered PHYSICALLY greatly here on earth, and would have still ETERNALLY greatly in hell.
