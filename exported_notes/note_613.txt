Point-1&#58;


i. Relationship with God
God doesn't keep malice. As soon as the prodigal son came back, His father was the one that ran to him and welcomed home. Prayer is not difficult. All it takes is to have a relationship with Him. Even greater than our relationship with an earthly father is our response when we have a Father-son relationship with God.

ii.  Resigning to regain King and subject restoration 
1 Kings 2&#58;11
It is when self dies that Christ will fully reign in our life. It was when Abner and Ishibosheth died that David reigned over all of Israel. When Christ is fully in control of our lives, our prayer becomes mighty
When praying, resign from self


iii. Receiving the resources of the Master as servants for responsibilities 
Do not reject being a full servant of Christ, obeying Him in all things. When we become fully His servants, our prayer becomes very mighty.
