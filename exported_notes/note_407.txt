{{text:BTB Study 332&#58; Fellowship among the brethren 

Acts 2&#58;42-47
Basis for fellowship 
Fellowship provides strength, encouragement, and opportunity for personal growth.

Barriers to fellowship 
Sin is the major barrier to fellowship. 

Benefits of Christian fellowship 
Christian fellowship strengthens us to grow in grace; speedy answers to our prayers; encouragement in times of weariness, corrections in spiritual growth.

Q1&#58; Basic requirement of true fellowship. Love for God and the entire brethren

Q2&#58; Some barriers to fellowship among brethren&#58; Unfaithfulness, laziness, love of this world and it's gilded toys

Q3&#58; Some of the benefits of being in fellowship with the brethren&#58; Encouragement, Assurance of making it to heaven, Receiving answers to prayers, Help from others brethren.

RO EXHORTATION 

TOPIC&#58; CALL TO STEADFASTNESS 
1 Corinthians 15&#58;57-58
Thanks be to God who gave us THE VICTORY
There are various forms of victory but there is one victory that is termed,"The Victory".
God expects us to be steadfast in personal evangelism, in godliness, in doctrine, in good works, in watchfulness. 

Psalms 57&#58;7; 112&#58;7
Our steadfastness must come from the heart. We are not attending activities because we will be rebuked if we do not. But the steadfastness that pleases God is the one that is due to the fixity of the heart that I will perform this activity. 
As workers, challenges, pressure increase but the greater the pressure, the greater the manifestation of God's grace in our lives. Is there hardship in the land? Trust God. My heart is fixed, oh, Lord, my heart is fixed, trusting in the land. Famine made Jacob to discover Joseph.  

Colossians 1&#58;23; 2 Thessalonians 3&#58;13; Hebrews 10&#58;23
If there are a thousand reasons why we should not remain steadfast, and there is only one reason why we should continue, we will continue. The abiding presence of God is a single,good enough reason to continue in steadfastness

In our journey, God knew there was a possibility of weariness in our steadfastness and that's why He said we should not allow it. 

Hebrews 11&#58;8-9
It was when there was famine in Canaan land and in journeying to Egypt to buy corn that Joseph would be recovered. Adversity has good things to bring to those that are steadfast in the Lord. 

The payback period can come any day. A man gavey a bed space in the university to a medical student and left the university afterwards. They never met again until 35 years when the giver of the bed space had serious kidney failure and he ended up in the medical student he helped, the chief medical director of the private hospital he was taken to. Payback day has come. We must continue steadfastly in well doing. 

Job 11&#58;13-16-19
The only barrier to steadfastness is iniquity. This must be put away before we can stretch out our hand to God. 

GS Message
TOPIC&#58; FRUITFUL FELLOWSHIP WITHOUT FEAR IN HIS FAMILY

Acts 2&#58;42

Point -1&#58; Fruitful fellowship of brethren in the Beloved

Acts 2&#58;40-42; Ephesians 1&#58;6-7; 1 Corinthians 1&#58;9
The continuation in fellowship is in the apostle's doctrine. There is no fellowship if there is no binding doctrine
There is also commonality of forgiveness of sin among the brethren. If this is not there, then what they have is merely a union of natural people.
If someone is not in fellowship with Christ, he cannot be in fellowship of the members of Christ's Church (brethren in the Beloved/Christ). In the fellow under Christ, there is no division among the brethren verbally or in the heart. 

1 John 1&#58;3,6-7; Leviticus 6&#58;2-4
Those that claim they have fellowship with Christ and walk in darkness, the Holy Ghost says, they are liars. In fellowship, there is nothing hidden. There couldn't have been anything that Peter was doing that James did not know but, we can tell that there were things Judas Iscariot was doing that the other brethren did not know (negotiating to sell Christ). He was not in fellowship with the brethren and the Lord. 
In deception, or lying, there is no fellowship.
When a husband and wife deceive one another or lie to one another, they are not in fellowship. When a woman refused to give her body to the husband, she has violently taken away what belong to the husband ( her body). They are not in fellowship until the woman restores her body to the owner.

Philippians 2&#58;1-5; Ephesians 4&#58;25
There is fellowship of Spirit and there is transference of spirit (e.g., Esau transferred his Spirit of hatred for Jacob unto his descendants hundred of years afterwards).
