As believers, we should go out there and preach with our word and our life. (last week's STS)

The place of signs & wonders in evangelism is to attract men & women unto Christ. They lift up Christ before men and then He draws them unto Himself.

The Lord says, come unto me as you are and I will not cast you out but, do not remain as you are.
Christ is sufficient for every crisis that is, or may come up in our lives.

Is the faith for salvation different from the faith for healing? An interesting question. 
We may have faith for salvation and we are saved by it. But the same person that adequately believed that the King of the Kingdom had given him entrance to His kingdom may not have the same level of belief (faith) that his sickness has been healed by the King (probably because he still feels the pain of the sickness). Somehow, he sees the healing of his body as a more difficult task for Christ than his admission to heaven. 
But if the same simple pronouncement from the King was effective , to give us access to the kingdom (a greater task),why can't we believe the King enough to receive his healing as well (a smaller task) just because of our physical experiences. Such is the case that many people that have gotten salvation from Christ are unable to get healing from Him because of the littleness of faith for healing. The pains might still be there but we just need to believe that we are healed already. The manifestation will follow quickly.

GS MESSAGE

Isaiah 6;8
Today, God has an end time message He wants to send to the world. He is asking us to volunteer for the task. 

Who shall "I" send (the unity of the GodHead); and who will go for "us" (the plurality of the GodHead). This affirms the trinity of the GodHead.

The baptism of Christ was conducted by the Trinity.
A time comes in your life when you will hear the voice of God. Many people hear the voice of God and walk away. We must give an answer when God talks to us

Isaiah was already sent. From chapters 1-5, he was already serving God. But a new call came in chapter 6, "Whom shall I send, who will go for us?" Isaiah came and declared, RESEND ME.

When a new call comes, and we give a new answer, we start getting a new revelation.

For instance, the book of Isaiah has 66 chapters. The first 39 chapters talk about the old testament Israel and the remaining 27 chapters deal with the new covenant. Compare this truth with the fact that the Bible has 66 books divided into 39 old testaments and 27 new testaments. Isaiah's book represents the pattern of the whole Bible.  That's how mighty God's revelation to Isaiah was.

TOPIC&#58; The recommissioning of the renewed people of God

Isaiah 6&#58;1-4; 9&#58;6
Holy, Holy, Holy (referring to the Father, Son and the Holy Ghost).

Point-1&#58; Confession after conviction by God's glory 
When you see God's glory, conviction will come upon you and it will bring confession out of your mouth.

Isaiah 6&#58;3,5; 2 Chronicles 7&#58;1-3,14. 
That I saw God's glory, does that mean I have arrived, that I am 100% acceptable to God because I saw His glory? NO!

Even the priests of God could not enter the temple because of the glory of the Lord. All Israel also bowed down in humility when they saw the glory of God. Everything is not okay because we have seen the glory of God. We are just given the opportunity to see His greatness and to humble ourselves. We are enjoined by the sight of His glory to pray, seek His way, humble ourselves, and turn from our wicked ways. It is only after this (our response to seeing His glory) that He will hear our prayers, forgive our sins and heal our land. 

Paul's affliction of 'thorn in the flesh' was not cancer or any other sickness but simply the Pharisees that buffeted/persecuted him unceasingly. The OT tells us that the Israelites would have thorns in their flesh from the Canaanites (persecutors)  they could not displace. Numbers 33&#58;55; Joshua 23&#58;13

John 2&#58;11,13
After the glory of Christ, the next thing He went to do was to cleanse the temple. 
Hebrews 2&#58;10-11
Christ came to bring us into the glory of righteousness. When God brings us to glory, He cleanses us and makes us one with Himself. Him that sanctified us and we that were sanctified by Him are all of one.

Isaiah 6&#58;6-7
Is it not surprising that Isaiah who had been preaching sound doctrine from chapters 1 to 5 still had sins that needed to be taken away in chapter 6. Sometimes, the sound of our preaching is so loud in our ears that it silences the tiny voice of conviction of sin in us. What would have been Isaiah's end if chapter 6 hadn't happened? It is a question for reflection.
