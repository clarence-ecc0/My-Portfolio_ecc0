Pt-2&#58; Our full transformation through the perfect propitiation of the Prince
In the old testament, the day of atonement was observed continuously (once every year) because one atonement was not enough forever. Christ came to make one atonement and it is final forever. His peace shall have no end and in the same way, the effectiveness of His sacrifice (atonement for our sins if we chose to remain in Him) shall have no end. If we do any other sacrifice today, we are ignorantly demonstrating that what Christ did was not perfect. That is a great error.
