{{text:Title&#58; A Dialogue With Death 

Every man has an appointment with death that will be kept. Julius Caesar was once reputed to have said, *_"... Death, a necessary end, will come when it will come."_*

Man thinks of death as something awful which indeed, it is. Yet, death is nothing other than a relocation agent; death relocates man from his temporary abode to his permanent home. 

One man was soliloquizing one day and had this conversation with *'Death'.*

Mr Death&#58; "You have been thinking and talking about me recently. I felt I needed to visit you and clarify certain views about myself"

Man&#58; "You don't scare me; I will fight you to a standstill."

Death&#58; "With what? Arms, fasting, prayer? Many tried and failed. You must have known that by now. Where are kings Nebuchadnezzar, Herold, David? There is no hedge against me when the last grain in your hourglass ⌛ has run out. I am the relocation agent, relocating both the good and the bad to the habitation they chose for themselves. This is my calling and my pride"

Man&#58; "Good, thank God I did not send any application for relocation."

Death&#58; "Not completely true. I received your application with your relocation date from your Maker, *the Judge of the whole earth*. You personally filled your desired relocation destination. 

Man&#58; "And where did I choose?"

Death&#58; "Let's see; from the updated document I have access to, as at the beginning of this conversation, you indicated 'hell'."

Man&#58; "That's a lie. I never filled any form nor said anything about relocating to hell."

Death&#58; "That's part of why I am here, to clarify things about how men fill their *'relocation form'.* You don't have to say anything or fill any physical form to indicate your chosen final relocation address. Your Maker gave all humans His rules of life and has instructed me to relocate them to one of two possible destinations (based on their observance of the rules of life) whenever their time is up, when the last grain in their ⌛ has fallen through."

Man&#58; "If you are talking about God, let me tell you that I obey Him. I pay my tithe, I help people and I am a leader in my church."

Death&#58; "Let's check your record briefly; I can see that you have an unsettled anger against David because he lied against you; I can also see that a lady called Lucy that assist with some cash has been appreciating you with her body. You have also not forgiven the exam officer in your school who hid your your final papers and caused you to spend an extra year in school." These are a few from your records that have not been addressed and by the rules of life, that is a choice for hell."
