{{text:TOPIC&#58; RECEIVING AND RETAINING GOD'S GRACE THROUGH LIVING (NOT DEAD) FAITH

James 2&#58;14-17; Ephesians 2&#58;8
All that we can achieve that heaven will consider good work will be by grace through faith. To withstand persecution, show love to people that hate us, etc. All these are possible through the help of grace that is available to those that maintain active faith on God's promises.

Point-1&#58; The transforming grace through definite, defining faith

Titus 2&#58;11

A. Transforming grace through trusting faith

Titus 2&#58;11-12; Romans 12&#58;2
The Bible did not say that the grace that brings salvation has been accepted by all men; it said it has appeared to all men. We must accept it to give us salvation. Grace teaches (instructs) us and we must obey it to give us salvation. You can't live a frivolous life and still you have grace. The two are not compatible. If you can't say 'no' to temptations, you don't have grace and, if you as a temptress, you cannot resist Satan who commanded you to tempt people with your lifestyle (dressing, etc.), you don't have grace. There must be physical manifestation of transformation before presence of grace can be verified in a life. Grace is not inert.

B Transparent godliness through tenacious faith

Hebrews 10&#58;22-23; 2 Peter 1&#58;3
The divine power of God has given unto the ability to be virtuous (avoid fighting, brawling, etc.). Partakers of the divine nature do not steal, commit immorality, seduce people by their bodies but live Holy and transparent life. 

C. Transcending goodness under trying faith
There is goodness under normal moral level. Grace takes us higher than the normal moral level. The good Samaritan provided first aid at the site, he took the man to the inn and paid in advance for his treatment and left the instructions that if extra is spent in the care of the man he did not know, he would come and pay the extra. That is transcending goodness. 

1 Peter 1&#58;7-9; 4&#58;10,12-16; Mathew 5&#58;24
In offices, we will be tested to see the level of our faith, whether we will keep to the instructions of the scriptures. 

Point-2&#58; Trampled grace by dead, deadening faith

James 2&#58;17; Jude 1&#58;4
If our faith ha no peace within and with neighbors, if it cannot tolerate long suffering, if doesn't have weakness, gentleness, it is dead. 

A. Misunderstood grace by nominal believers
James 2&#58;19
Nominal Christians believe there is God but do not tremble at His laws towards obedience. The fear we have that God's judgement is coming should propel us to obedience otherwise our believe (faith) in God is dead 

Hosea 8&#58;2-3,12
Israel believed in God and yet cast His laws aside as a strange thing. There are nominal believers today that have accepted homosexuality and anyone that preaches against that is seen as a strange thing. 

B. Misusing grace for non-Christian behavior

1 Corinthians 15&#58;2

C. Misapplied grace by natural beasts

Jude 1&#58;4,10,12-13
Natural beasts (men ND women) have crept into the church unaware. We need to be vigilant so that we do not swallow their poisons. Hypocrites are twice dead - dead before they came to know the Lord and after knowing Him, they backslided and are dead again. 


Point-3&#58; Triumphing grace through distinct, distinguishing faith.
Our faith must distinguish us for the faith to triumph. If our faith is similar to that of the majority, the result the majority get will be similar to your result.

Romans 5&#58;1-28(); 1 Timothy 1&#58;14

A. Overcoming the world and worldliness by grace through faith 

1 John 5&#58;4-5; James 4&#58;4
The only thing that overcomes the world is our faith. 
We are the bride of Christ (man and woman). If we leave Christ and start having fellowship with the world, we are spiritual adulterers and adulteresses.

1 John 2&#58;15-16
All that is in the world is made up of the lust of the flesh, lust of the eyes and pride of life. Anyone that loves these has no love of Christ because Christ is against all these. 

B. Overpowering our weakness and willfulness by grace and faith 

The willfulness in our lives can only be overcome by grace and faith

C. Overturning others wandering in the wilderness by grace through faith 
Our brethren, colleagues may be prone to wandering away from God's standard. We have to make up our mind not to follow them. This is only possible by grace through faith. Others will but I cannot.

Proverbs 21&#58;16; Psalms 119&#58;10-11
Those that are prone to wandering away from the path of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead. 
God's word must be settled in our hearts as our second nature.
