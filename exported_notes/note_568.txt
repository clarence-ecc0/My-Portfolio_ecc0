{{text:TOPIC&#58; LIVING THE GOSPEL BEFORE THE WORLD

Introduction&#58;
Acts 11&#58;26.
Christianity is not a religion. It is the totality of the way of life of a believer. It encompasses his beliefs, his deeds, his associations, his thoughts, the life he lives in private and in public, his position on issues and utterances in his community, his thoughts that are hidden from his neighbors but which are open secret before God. 

It is the gospel life we live, not the gospel life we project, profess or proclaim that takes us to heaven. 
If ye know these things (the gospel), happy are ye if ye do them (if ye live the gospel life)


POINT-1&#58; Preaching the gospel without living the gospel before the world 

Romans 2&#58;17-21
God expects that there is harmony between the word of the preacher and the way (deeds) of the preacher, the messages of the apostles and the acts of the apostles must be in harmony. The apostles (and the believers) were called Christians in Antioch because there was harmony between their messaging and their lifestyle.

Duplicity of life is not permitted in the sight of God especially by those in leadership positions who are Shepherding the flock. A leader's sin is a leading sin.


POINT-2&#58; Professing the gospel without living the gospel before the world 
To profess the gospel means to claim that one is living by the gospel standard. It is our deeds that give weight, evidence to our profession. 

There are many that profess that they know God but in deeds they deny Him (Titus 1&#58;16). Professors of Christianity have in reality denied the knowledge of God if their deeds are contrary to the way and will of God. Our work is the critical source of evidence of our claim of knowing God. When we live the life that is outside the gospel, we project error to the world around us about Christianity. Our lives will be preaching another gospel which is not the original gospel.

✓ The Lord Himself stated clearly that there will be many that will profess that they were serving Him that He will deny (Mathew 7&#58;22)

✓ While they profess knowledge and service to God, they are well deep into their abominable lifestyle. (Jeremiah 7&#58;9-11)

✓ Judgment is coming for all who profess a relationship with God but are projecting the wrong gospel to the world, and condemning the gospel by their lifestyle (Romans 2&#58;1,3)


POINT-3&#58; Practicing the gospel life as evidence of Christ-likeness before the world 

Acts 11&#58;26
The believers that are living the gospel life before the world are the true practitioners of the Christian faith. They are the true ambassadors of Christ, and as a country will honor the words of his ambassador, so will Christ honor the declarations of His ambassadors. 

An ambassador of Christ, a representative of God should be able to stand and challenge the people, "which of you convinceth me of sin?" (John 8&#58;46);  "whose Ox have I taken (1 Samuel 12&#58;3-4)

A true ambassador of Christ will exercise himself (place a rigid rule on his life) regularly to ensure when he checks his conscience  he wont find any offense he has committed against God or man. (Acts 24&#58;16). 

While exercising ourselves, we do it without being soft on ourselves, making provisions (excuses) for the flesh (Romans 13&#58;14)

We have placed our hands on the plough, we will not look back. We will consecrate unto the gospel life in all our endeavors. Through, we will escape every snare of the Fowlers in Jesus name..
