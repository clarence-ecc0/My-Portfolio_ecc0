TOPIC&#58; THE BLESSEDNESS AND THE BENEFITS OF A PURE HEART

Point-1&#58; The provision and the proof of purity of heart

Psalms 24&#58;3-4; Acts 11&#58;26
The believers in Antioch lived that purified life that resembled Christ whom they preached and were thus called Christians (which by definition means, Christ-like) 

a. Personal salvation with peace in the heart.
Ephesians 2&#58;14-22; 2 Corinthians 5&#58;17; Romans 5&#58;1; Phillipians 4&#58;7,9

b. Proven sanctification and purity of heart
Mathew 5&#58;8; Hebrews 10&#58;14,16,22
True believers are washed with pure water (the water of the word of God).
An unsanctified heart has these qualities 👇 

H- Hypocrisy or hatred in the heart
E- Envy in the heart
A- Anger and animosity in the heart
R- Retaliation or revenge in the heart
T- Tempestuous temper in the heart

c. Persevering Spirit and purpose in the heart
Daniel 1&#58;8; Psalms 17&#58;3; 2 Timothy 2&#58;21; 3&#58;16-17
Have I seriously purposed that my mouth (and my heart) will not transgress? Onuche, think about this very solemnly. 


Point-2&#58; The promise and the power for the pure heart

a. The promise of pure heart
Mathew 5&#58;8; Psalms 89&#58;34
The promise for those that have a pure heart is "blessedness". Salvation has its opportunity costs. There will always be "alternatives forgone" in order to satisfy the desire to see God. Going to heaven has its 'opportunity costs'. The promise of seeing God is for the community but it is attained personally. We must labor for it through the approved pathway of unfailing evidence-based faith in Christ


b. The prayer for a pure heart
Psalms 51&#58;7,10; Ezekiel 36&#58;26,37
If you had a pure heart before and missed it, there is a prayer you can pray for a return of the pure heart. David is an example of such a one. He got what he missed as all of us can, even today. Ezekiel teaches us that we need to ask God (pray) to get the pure heart.

c. The power of a pure heart
Luke 10&#58;27,19; Acts 1&#58;8
As we possess the pure heart, we are given the promised power that goes with the pure heart. 
There are people in the community and at work places that are tough. Demons obey when we command in Jesus name but even with our many prayers, some human beings are not moved. It is the promised power we receive through pure hearts that helps us to manage the very difficult people around us, navigate around these satanic implantations on our highway to heaven without sinning in Jesus name. 

Point-3&#58; The performance of prophecy for the pure in heart.
God is faithful to fulfill all He has declared (prophesied) unto His servants.

a. Sure performance for the obedient pure in heart
Genesis 26&#58;2,12

b. The sure pronouncement for the obstinate in pure heart
Jeremiah 18&#58;7-8
The obstinate in pure heart can still make it to God's presence if they repent and turn back from evil. Saul entered not into God's presence because he remained obstinate in his transgressions.

c. The sure prophecy from the only Potentate on high.
2 Peter 1&#58;19; Luke 1&#58;45
