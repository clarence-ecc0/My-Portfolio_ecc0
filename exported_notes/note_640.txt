Point-1&#58; The inseparable righteousness of the ambassadors and apostles under persecution 

The apostles (high up) and the ambassadors (down there) all had the same experience of salvation.
What the apostles were doing in Jerusalem, the ambassadors were doing everywhere they were scattered to.

Acts 8&#58;1
An important question&#58; If you are scattered from your home and you find yourself where you have no house and no apostles or church, what will you do? Nobody with you to see your faithfulness, what will you do? Here, the ambassadors went about (without homes) to spread the gospel

A.The courage of the ministers under persecution 
Acts 5&#58;40-44
In the early church, they preached with courage and it was so for DEEPER Life in the early days

B. The commitment of ambassadors under persecution 
The ambassadors lost their shops, their market, their customers, their sources of income when they were scattered from Jerusalem and yet they kept preaching. How will you act if you lost these? Will you still have commitment to the church? 
Acts 8&#58;4

If one believer coming from church and was kidnapped and you read it, what will you do? Will you still go out to preach, evangelize or you will stop?

C. The conversion of multitude during persecution 
The church did not stop because of the persecution but preaching was sustained and multitude was added to the church.
Acts 17&#58;3-4,11

Point- 2&#58; The inspiring revival of affection and attendance during persecution 
As we hear the gospel, our affection for Christ will show in church attendance.

The Lord is not interested in stuffing our heads with knowledge but to show our love for Him

A. Association with the righteous, not with past associates
Acts 9&#58;19,26-28
As soon as Saul was saved, you find him associating with the disciples of the Lord. Paul's love for Christ showed in his attendance with those that love the Lord also. 

B. Affection for righteousness, not for past attachments 
Before we knew the Lord, we had our associates but after knowing the Lord, our affection for righteousness separated us from the old attachments (associates). The people they are associated with now behaved holily and godly like them. We can't tell those that Paul associated with in the Jewish religion but we could see those he associated with when he became a believer were  believers themselves.
1 Thessalonians 2&#58;10; 2 Timothy 2&#58;10-12

C. Attendance with readiness, not for passing activities 
Hebrews 10&#58;24
When we attend meetings, let us strive to provoke one another to love more. 
Even at the time of the apostles, some people have the manner of forsaking the assembly of the saints.

We need to be careful in our dealing with new converts. We need to know their hearts so that they do not come and pollutethe saints.
