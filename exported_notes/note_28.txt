Pt-2&#58; Our full transformation through the perfect propitiation of the Prince
In the old testament, the day of atonement was observed continuously (once every year) because one atonement was not enough forever. In the new testament, Christ came to make one atonement and it is final forever. His peace shall have no end and in the same way, the effectiveness of His sacrifice (atonement for our sins if we chose to remain in Him) shall have no end. If we do any other sacrifice today, we are ignorantly demonstrating that what Christ did was not perfect. That is a great error. 

Roman 3&#58;23-25; 1Jn 2&#58;2; 3&#58;8; 5&#58;18
There is something like a spiritual magnet in every sinner that picks up sin anywhere it sees it. When Christ came in, He removed the magnet and threw it away from our heart. Christ in our hearts does not attract sins but repels sin away from our lives. 

2Cor 3&#58;18; Jn 8&#58;32,36.



Pt-3&#58; The future translation of the peacefully pure by the Prince 
When you hear that a real child of God has died, he only changed his residence, he only changed his address and has gone to a better place. We shouldn't cry so much because we cannot see him again. He has actually gone to a better place. He is happy where he is. This is only for the people who are peacefully pure (peaceful and holy).

Hebrews 11&#58;5-6; 2Kg 2&#58;5, 11; Acts 1&#58;9-11; 
All truly transformed believers must have this testimony that 'THEY PLEASE GOD'. We must strive to have this testimony. 
I must maintain peace and purity in my heart to have any hope of seeing God. 

1Thess 4&#58;15-16-18; Col 2&#58;13-14; Jn 14&#58;1-3
To die without the guilt of sin is the most glorious death ever. 
We were in the darkness of the kingdom of Satan, sickness and have been translated into the kingdom of Christ, of deliverance, of dominion over sin.

Christ has gone to prepare a place for those He came to earth to prepare for the place. He will come again to take them to the prepared place.
