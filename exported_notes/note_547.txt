TOPIC&#58; THE LITTLE MEMBER THAT DETERMINES GREAT MATTER FOR TIME AND ETERNITY 

The tongue itself without the heart can do no evil or good. The condition of the heart determines the outcome of what the tongue speaks.

Point-1&#58; The little fire that devours great men
Itbis not the fault of the tongue for what it spews out. It is the condition of the heart. The heart controls all the members of the body. Make the tree good and it's fruit will be good. 

James 3&#58; 5-8; Isaiah 5&#58;24; Exodus 5&#58; 2
No man can tame the tongue. Only a transformed heart can. As long as a man has an untransformed heart,his tongue will speak what his heart commands him to. The little tongue devoured Pharaoh , Nebuchadnezzar, Herod and even the anti-Christ.

Revelations 13&#58;4-6, 20; 20&#58;10
The tongue eventually led to the destruction of the anti-Christ. The devil and the false prophet were equally sent to the lake of fire.


Point-2&#58; The little foxes that destroys great ministry 

James 3&#58;6
It is the little words that we throw at each other that spoil the relationship. 

Ezekiel 13&#58;4; 2 Peter 2&#58;1




Point-3&#58;  The little faith that ..... our great Maker
The relationship between the little faith and little tongue. We manifest our little faith through our little tongue.
The little tongue of Rebekka caused her to plant deception in the heart of Jacob and hatred in the heart of Esua.
The tongue has no fear but the heart. When the heart is afraid, it communicates it to the tingue which now vocalizes it. When we are fearful in the heart, the tongue will tell it. We tell lies with the tongue when fear is in the heart. 

Isaiah 57&#58;11
When we fear God, believe that the Sender will protect the one He sent, we won't be afraid of man or Satan. 

Luke 12&#58;28-32
Little faith makes us doubt God. But if we know the faithfulness of God, why should we doubt? It is the Father's pleasure to give us the kingdom with all the riches therein, pleasures therein, etc.
