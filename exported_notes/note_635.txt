Explanations&#58;
Tribute&#58; this is not the tax paid to Caesar, the publicans collect that.
Exodus 30&#58;11-12,16


Christ's question about the children of the king&#58; Children of the kings do not pay tax for the maintenance of the kingdom, all else (except exempted) must pay

The necessity of waiving our rights when holding onto it will do harm to a greater good.

Implications for believers&#58; 
(1) Pay your legitimate taxes, duties, levies even if the leadership is corrupt (government duties, taxes; estate levies, etc). Christ paid the temple tax (tribute) even though He lamented that the leadership had converted the temple to a den of thieves and robbers. 
He also admonished that believers should give to Caesar what belongs to Caesar and to God what belongs to God (Matthew 22&#58;21). We are required to be subject unto the higher power (Romans 13&#58;1, 6-7); to magistrates (Titus 3&#58;1) etc. 

(2) Pay your tithe and other contributions required to support the work of God in the place where you worship IF YOU BELIEVE THE PLACE IS TRULY GOD'S HOUSE OF WORSHIP. But, If you do not believe the assembly is not a true place of worship for God then, why are you going there to worship God. So long as you take a place as a house of worship for God, pay your tithe and support other contributions that are for the propagation of God's work.

Christ left us an example that we should follow (1 Peter 2&#58;21)
✓ He paid the temple tribute which He even had the privilege not to pay as the Son of God
✓ He commanded that believers should give to Caesar his dues - taxes, duties, levies, etc.
