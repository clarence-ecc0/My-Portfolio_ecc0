{{text:An exposition on the events recorded from Revelations 20&#58;1-10.
The pre- great tribulation saints have gone to be with the Lord in the cloud for 7 years while Satan wrecked havoc on the people left behind 

At the end of 7 years, a great Angel (probably Christ because it is He who has the power to bind Satan and lock him in a prison that none can open) came down and bound Satan and cast him into the bottomless pit. At the same time, those that died for the name of Christ, who refused to serve/receive the beast and his image are resurrected from the dead to reign with Christ. This is the FIRST RESURRECTION. The wicked people who served Satan and the beast did not resurrect at this time but will be when Satan is loosed from his prison.

At the end of the 1000 years of Satan's incarceration, he is loosed again from imprisonment accompanied by the resurrection of the wicked who had died at their first death. Satan will go around to gather the wicked all over the earth under the command of Gog and Magog (Read Genesis 10&#58;2; Ezekiel 38&#58;2,22 to get an account of these two. Gog was established in Syria and the generation must have spread to other nations of the earth). 

Satan and all that he gathered under the command of Gog and Magog will gather to fight against Jerusalem, the home of the saints. God Himself will destroy Satan and his gang as prophesied in Ezekiel 38&#58;2,22) and all will be cast into the lake of fire. This represents the 2nd death for the human beings who lived in disobedience to God. (first their body died and they were separated from living people on earth; now, they die again (2nd time) and their soul is separated from God eternally.
The saints of God have no part in the second death as prophesied in Revelations 20&#58;6.
