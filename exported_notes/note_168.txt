Last week's STS&#58; WARNING AGAINST COVETOUSNESS

TOPIC&#58; FORGIVENESS AND PEACE FROM CHRIST OUR SAVIOUR 

Luke 7&#58;44
Some people are like Simon the Pharisee who will not create a conducive environment for sinners to repent. 
When we go out for evangelism, while one is preaching, the other one(s) is/are praying that the sinner will accept the message and be saved.


Point-1&#58; The forgetfulness, fault-finding and pride of Simon
Simon invited Christ to his house but not from his heart. He judged Christ not to be a prophet if he could not discern that the prostitute was a sinner.

Luke 7&#58;36-39
The woman came as she was. She did not come pretending or with hypocrisy. She came not covering her hair and no decorum. A church like deeper life may become guilty because they will frown at her and judge her. Deeper Life may give her a clothe to cover her head. None of the disciples attempted to push her from weeping on Christ. Such done could easily destroy the fragile conviction of the sinner. We should not be a barrier to people's salvation. 
Allow sinners (women with hairs uncovered, paintings, ear rings & even men with earrings , etc.) to come to the church as they are, without giving them rules and regulations. For sinners to be converted, we must allow them to come to the house of God as they are, to hear the raw word of God and let God do the convincing. Many of us have been guilty like Simon the Pharisee.

What are the perceptions of Simon concerning Christ?
a. He saw Christ as a man, not as the Son of God and the Savior of the world. Luke 7&#58;39; Mathew 9&#58;3; John 9&#58;24

ii. Sinless perfection of Christ above men. 
Hebrews 7&#58;26; Mathew 1&#58;23

iii. Soul-saving propitiation in Christ, the Messiah 
Roman 3&#58;23-24


Point-2&#58; The foundation of forgiveness and peace from the Savior 

Luke 7&#58;47-
i. Conviction and sorrow for sin

Luke 7&#58;37-38; 2 Corinthians 7&#58;10; Mathew 5&#58;4
The very presence of the Son of God will break down a sinner. 

ii. Confession and separation from all sins

Proverbs 28&#58;13; Acts 5&#58;31
When people are under conviction and are crying for their sins, do not stop them. Do not give cheap speedy comfort. 

iii. Conversion and submission to the Savior 

Acts 3&#58;19; James 5&#58;19-20
True repentance leads to conversion. A believer who backslides can be converted from the error of his way. Upstanding believers should help to convert the backslider from the error of his/her way. 
There are many people that have been brought to the church but not to Christ, they have not been converted. They have come but they are not sorrowful for the sins they committed therefore, they are not saved. 


Point-3&#58; Her faith in the faithfulness of the Provider of salvation 

Our salvation is not based on our feelings, excitement, feeling of joy, etc. Once one has truly come to Christ in submission and He declared she/he is saved, he/she is saved fully. The feelings of joy, etc. which may not be there at the beginning will come later. 
The thief on the cross may not have had any joy when he received salvation on the cross but he was truly and fully saved. Once the Provider of salvation has spoken concerning us, nothing else matters

i. Faith in the faithfulness of the Saviour 

Luke 7&#58;48-50; Romans 10&#58;9-10
No matter what people around feel or think, what has the Savior said unto you. If He says you are saved, that is final, irrespective of the sayings and feelings of the people around. 

ii. The fellowship and followership with the saints

Acts 2&#58;40-42
As Christ asked the woman to go, it did not mean she left and never came back to fellowship. We know from the scriptures that she came back and continued in fellowship.


iii. Fishing without falling back into sin. 
 Christ wants those that have been saved to go and speak to their networks without falling into the old sin with them. 
Mathew 4&#58;19-20; John 4&#58;28-30
The new convert has become a fisher of men from the sea of humanity.
