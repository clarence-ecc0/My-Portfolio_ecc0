            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _The God of All Grace_


_"Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain *mercy,* and find *grace* to help in time of need."_
*Hebrews 4&#58;16*

God is the God of mercy. He is also the God of Grace. What then is mercy, and what is grace? See the two illustrations below 

📌 In mercy, I was *NOT given* what I earned (deserved). I did wrong and therefore earned (deserved) punishment for my offence. I was forgiven and not punished. What I obtained here is mercy.

📌 In grace, I was *given* a reward that I did *NOT* earn or merit. The master called laborers into His work where they worked for *12 hours* and were paid the agreed wage for the *12 hours.* I was brought into the work very late and only worked for *1 hour* and yet, I was given the full wage for *12 hours.* In this illustration, I received a wage that I did *NOT* earn. That is what grace does. It gives us what we did not merit.

God is always on His throne of grace. He has offered grace and mercy to all men who have not earned any right to enter paradise. To those that accept His offer, He helps them in their hours of need and will bring them to paradise.

 What does *accepting the offer of grace* mean?

For a sinner, he must repent (turn away) and abandon his sinful lifestyle. If this is done sincerely from the heart, grace and mercy will locate the sinner, and be with him, to help him in his hours of need.

For a believer, he must not go back to the sinful lifestyle he abandoned when he first believed. He must continue in the godly way he has been shown by Christ and the scriptures. Surely, grace and mercy are always with him, to help him in his hours of need. 

The verse below is what the Holy Spirit said concerning the gift of grace.

_"For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, teaching us that, *denying* *ungodliness and worldly lusts,* we should live *soberly,* *righteously,* and *godly,* in this present world;"_
*Titus 2&#58;11-12*

In accepting grace, *there are things to deny* (ungodliness & worldly lust) and *there are things to live by* (godliness, righteousness & sobriety)

I pray to God that we will accept His offer of grace;  we will come to His throne of grace, the only place where we can obtain grace and mercy when we need help in Jesus name.

Shalom.
