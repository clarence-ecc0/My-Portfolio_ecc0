{{text:TOPIC&#58; THE ESSENCE OF PLEASING GOD IN LIFE AND MINISTRY 

Hebrews 11&#58;6; Galatians 1&#58;10; 1 Thessalonians 2&#58;4
This is a question we should all ask ourselves - Am I seeking to please men or to please God in the work of the ministry that I do? If it is for men, then, I am not a servant of Christ. I MUST be a servant of Christ.

Romans 8&#58;8; 1 Thessalonians 2&#58;15; Isaiah 2&#58;6; Judges 14&#58;3; Hebrews 11&#58;5
If my deeds are focused on pleasing the flesh, I cannot please God. We must crucify the flesh (pride, sensual pleasure, etc.) on the altar of sacrifice. Our position as recognized leaders in itself cannot please God if sin/flesh has not been dealt with. We do not do/want something because they please us well, they must please God well first. Enoch and Samson are opposite in character in who is pleased in their askings and deeds. As faith transforms us, it gives us the mind of Christ that puts the desire to please God (and not any man or ourselves) in all our ways and deeds. 

Matthew 3&#58;17; John 8&#58;29; Philippians 2&#58;5; Hebrews 11&#58;6
Christ Himself declared that He lived to do always those things that pleased the Father. This should be our attitude as well. Lord, help that this will be my life at the office especially, no matter the challenges that I am facing there. 


Point-1&#58; The life of faith that pleases God

Hebrews 11&#58;4; 9&#58;22; 11&#58;5; Genesis 5&#58;22-24; Amos 3&#58;3
Both Cain and Abel offered sacrifices unto God but Abel provided what was more excellent (blood that makes atonement) than Cain, who provided fruit of the ground that was under a curse (he offered a cursed item as sacrifice). 

Walking with God is only through faith and such walk with God (without backsliding) please God. We have daily agreements with God in all our walks, in all our deeds, talks and thoughts.

1 Thessalonians 4&#58;1; James 2&#58;18-20,26; Hebrews 11&#58;7
Dumb faith, dead faith (God is speaking and the man is not hearing/accepting). A dead faith cannot please God. A faith that is man-centered (confidence in man) or self-centered (me, me, me!) cannot please God.  Faith without practical action (evidence) is dead.

Hebrews 12&#58;28; 11&#58;8; Isaiah 51&#58;2; Hebrews 11&#58;11; Hebrews 10&#58;23
The heaven and hell that we have not seen should move us with fear to obedience (just like Noah was moved over a flood which he had not seen)  Abraham left the known to the unknown (the direct opposite of the saying "seeing is believing") as an example of one who believed without seeing. 
Only faith in God through Christ atoning sacrifices pleases the Lord. 

Point-2&#58; The love in fullness that pleases God

Hebrews 11&#58;5; Deuteronomy 10&#58;13; 30&#58;6; Hebrews 11&#58;15-16
Why did Enoch decide to walk with God even in an era of godlessness? His heart was circumcised by God. Those that followed God completely do this because they know that there is a better country that God is taking them to. 

Genesis 24&#58;6-8; 22&#58;1-3,15-16; Hebrews 11&#58;24-25; Exodus 32&#58;10-14
The trust in the country Abraham was going to was so clear that he took an oath from his servant not to take Isaac back to his home that he left behind. 
We cannot claim Genesis 22&#58;17 without going through the sacrifice of verse 16
As we look at Hebrews chapter 11, we realize that all that the heroes of the old testament did was tied to their unshakeable faith in God no matter the threat to them, or the pleasure they would miss by trusting in God. 
Moses could have become the Pharaoh, an emperor but he refused. God even desired to make him the progenitor of a great nation and he refused yet again. He preferred what would maximize the glory of God and even at the expense of suffering affliction with God's people. 

Hebrews 11&#58;25; Psalms 119&#58;30; Hebrews 11&#58;26
Does my choice have pain, persecution, challenges accompanying it? These would not determine my decision rather, I will give attention to the question, will it glorify God and achieve His purpose? 

Hebrews 13&#58;12-14; 1 Peter 4&#58;14; Hebrews 11&#58;27; 13&#58;5-6; Psalms 56&#58;4,11
Bear the reproach (rejection by people) for the name of Christ joyfully. Moses knew the power & wrath of Pharaoh & yet, was not mindful of it because he saw & feared the might of God (invisible) than the visible, dangerous Pharaoh. 
Love that was generated from implicit faith in God caused the heroes of faith to walk with Him & please Him. 

Point-3&#58; The labor for the flock that pleases God

Hebrews 11&#58;30,32-34
For those that have implicit faith in God, lions on the way cannot hinder their obedience to do God's will. All that the heroes of faith did was for the glory of God & to secure the flock of the Lord, His elect (the children of Israel). Through faith they subdued kingdoms. There are kingdoms that are against us and like the heroes of faith, we will subdue the kingdoms in Jesus. We will subdue this land
