✓ God has given us sleep and dreams to forewarn us of eternity. There shall be sorrow unending (as in a bad dream) and joy unending (as in a good dream). Learn from your dreams what is ahead for you. Only the flesh can die; the spirit can never die. 

[B&#93; Do you have assurance in death? 
✓ Christ & the apostles did. The agony, the shame, the pain were all (and will always) be there before the sedation of death. But after the sedation, the infirmities of cancers in the flesh that generated the pain, agony is surgically cut off by divine hands and we are awoken to a painless body without infirmity. 

✓ Christ went through that sedation of death and the divine surgery and came out without the infirmity of the mortal flesh. Any wonder that Peter would be locked up to be killed and he was just sleeping peaceably, any wonder that Stephen would face death without any pang, any wonder that Paul and Silas would just come out of death experience and are locked up for whatever that may still come and all they could do was to "sing praises unto God"?
