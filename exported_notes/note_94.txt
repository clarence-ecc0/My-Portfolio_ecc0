Point-1&#58; Jesus the Nazarene, our Saviour, Sanctifier, Strengthener
Mathew 2&#58;13; 2 Peter 1&#58;3-4
Personalize the promises of the Bible anytime you come across them and believe them deeply from the heart.

a. The name above all things below the sun and beyond the stars 
Philippians 2&#58;9-11; Hebrews 12&#58;2; 
Things in heaven are beyond the stars, and things on earth and underneath the earth are below the sun. The knees of all things in these realms must bow to Christ


b. The name that saves and, shields from sin
Matthew 1&#58;21; Acts 4&#58;12; 2&#58;31, 37-42
When we hold onto our meat (our sin) that we are commanded to bring out, saying I am sorry without obedience has no meaning and power. I must obey the instruction and continue in (a) the doctrines being taught, (b) fellowship with other brethren, (c) sharing of bread, and (d) in prayers


c. The name that heals all sicknesses from self and from Satan 
There are sicknesses that people bring upon themselves&#58; Nebuchadnezzar through his utterances brought madness upon himself; Gelhizier brought sickness upon him; Herold accepted the inordinate praises given to him and brought God's wrath upon himself, but thank Gid, Abimelech took instructions and he was spared. People who smoke, take hard drugs that bring sicknesses upon themselves.

There are sicknesses from Satan and the name of Jesus will take them away from people that accept Christ from their hearts.

Acts 3&#58;6,16; 4&#58;29-30; Mark 16&#58;17-20

Point-2&#58; Jesus the Overcomer, making us overcome self, sin and Satan 


a. The only begotten Son making us holy beloveth sons 
John 1&#58;12, 14, 18; 1 John 3&#58;1-3
Receiving Christ is different from receiving man-made religion. In receiving Christ,we belong to Him and not to the founder or pastor of the church. 

Although we are already like Him in character, we are not yet like Him, with the ability to enter a room that is locked), but when He shall appear, we shall be like Him in such manner also.

b. The offering for our secured salvation and steadfast sanctification.
Ephesians 5&#58;2, 25-27
It is an erroneous teaching by a minister that nobody can be holy. 

c. The Overcomer in our stead, for our sake
Jesus was an Overcomer from all eternity but still came to earth to make us overcomers as well.

1 John 2&#58;13-14
The children of God have the power to overcome the wicked one (Satan). When he pushes them to fight, steal, commit fornication, they overcome him and will not succumb to him. 

Point-3&#58; Jesus, the Prince of peace, seen suffering, soon Sovereign 
Isaiah 9&#58;6
The peace in the kingdom of Christ has no end, and this is the portion of all that are in His kingdom. 

a. The passover sacrificed for sinful souls and sincere saints
I Corinthians 5&#58;7
If we have truly accepted the sacrifice of Christ, we must become new lumps and this is only possible if we purge out the old leaven (old sinful behaviors of immorality, fighting, telling lies, etc) in the old lump. The leaven in the old lump is cast out and a new lump without the leaven can emerge.

b. The purifier of selected servants for sacred service
Malachi 3&#58;3; Titus 2&#58;14; 1 Peter 1&#58;22
Servants of the Lord are sacred people. We are not like Jehoshaphat who will tell Ahab, my people are like your people and your people are like my people. We are different, we are not like the world.
Saul (the old lump) was zealous for evil works but Paul (the new lump) was zealous for good, godly works. When people see a peculiar person in Christ (a new lump), they can tell. 

c. The prophet with the sole significant sentence for every soul
Acts 3&#58;19-26; Hebrews 1&#58;1-3; 
Key things required include (1) repentance, (2) conversion and, (3) blotting out of sin.
Many claim their sins have been blotted out (3) when numbers 1 & 2 have not occurred. This CANNOT happen. 
The foremost thing Christ has come to do, the great blessing He brought to us is in "turning everyone from his iniquity"
