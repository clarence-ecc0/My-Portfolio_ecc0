[C&#93; God's intention concerning our children 
We are here to be reminded of Psalms 127&#58;4-5 that likens the children of the youth to arrows in the hand of a mighty man. When we realize that things can happen for or, against our posterity through our children, we will be more cautious about their upbringing. God has designed that with them, we are to speak with the enemy in the gate. 
✓ One can only imagine how aghast Jacob would have been, to be alive to see his posterity fighting against the very commission God gave to him; to spread the message of salvation throughout his land of inheritance (for his children's salvation) and afterwards, to the gentile nations up to the end of the earth for their salvation. 

[D&#93; What happened to Jacob's children 
If we look at what Psalms 127&#58;4 said in [C&#93; above, and look at what the children of Jacob did in [B&#93; above, we may rightly ask the question, "what really happened to the children of Jacob?"
✓ The children of Jacob were charmed out of his hand by the enemy (Satan). As we carefully observe what they did against the gospel in Jerusalem and in the gentile world (see references above), it is clear these children of Jacob were not speaking WITH the enemy at the gate but rather, were speaking FOR the enemy at the gate. 
✓ The destruction of Jerusalem by Nebuchadnezzar first, and then the Roman empire later was not because they were too strong for the God of Jacob. They only occurred because the children of Jacob had abandoned the God of Jacob and were speaking FOR the enemy at the gate, not WITH him. 

[E&#93; The lessons we must all learn
The revelations of Psalms 127&#58;4-5, and what happened to Jacob's posterity are both warnings to us, that we should strive to keep our arrows perpetually under our influence (like arrows in our hands). We must hold onto them tightly, teach and exemplify the true Christian life before them and by it, shield them from being charmed out of our hands by Satan. When our lifestyle contradicts our faith, we send confusing messages to our arrows. 
✓ Always remember this&#58; the roaring enemy is ever-ready, still moving around, looking for our children  to devour (charm out of our hands). The whole of our posterity after us can be brought down if we lose hold of our children. You need to strive for this if you want to see your posterity (generations after you) in heaven. Don't you want to be that man in Isaiah 58&#58;12? Think about this.
