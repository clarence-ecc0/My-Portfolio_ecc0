The war against the Midianites 
Number 31&#58;1-54
God told Moses two things&#58; (1) that he should ask the children of Israel to fight the Midianites and, (2) that he would die after the assignment.
Moses told the children of Israel the first part of the vision (which was meant for them) and the second part of the vision which was him to himself,he kept to himself. He did not even share with Joshua who was to take over nor Eleazer, the high priest. 
Lesson&#58; it is not all that God revealed to us that we discuss with our families, other leaders in the church, etc.

Division of the booties of war without partiality
In the division of the booties, there was equity. The priests were fewer and they received less than the Levites. 
Apply wisdom in your family as you use your resources to train the elderly children. Be sure you have a reserve for the younger children.

God promotes us, saves us, do gracious things in our lives, but there is no thanksgiving unto God. Think deeply upon this.
