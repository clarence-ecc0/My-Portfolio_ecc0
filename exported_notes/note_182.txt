There is a faith that enables you to enter through the gate. This faith does not require any work from us but just believing
There is the faith that enables you to continue after entering. There is also faith that enables us to exit the world, ending our race well.

James 2&#58;14-19


Point-1&#58; The deadness of empty faith without evident proof
I believe, I believe, and there is no fruit of your believe, you have a dead faith.

i. No saving faith without spiritual fruit

Titus 1&#58;16; John 2&#58;23-25; 
In words, they claim Christianity but in deeds, they show they are not His.  

ii. No sanctification faith without scriptural fruit 

Acts 26&#58;18; 15&#58;9; Gal 2&#58;20; Hebrews 12&#58;13-24
You say you are sanctified and get offended easily when you are being cheated? There is no sanctification yet.
If I am sanctified, my spiritual eyes will be opened, to understand the source of the offences against me, that Satan is behind it all. That should affect how I respond to provocations. 
The 'I' that is within me, that gives excuses like Adam, that is not easy to placate when offended is crucified with Christ. It no longer exists in me any longer. When you are sanctified, you are no longer sensitive to reproaches of man, provocations from men/spouse. A sanctified member of DLBC will not be offended and start fighting because someone reproaches his GS. 

iii. No steadfast faith without steady fruit 

1 Peter 5&#58;9; Psalms 78&#58;8-11; Hebrews 3&#58;14, 18-19
Steadfast faith gives us steady fruits. This is not the faith that brought us into the kingdom (faith for salvation) but the faith that keeps us going steadily (no matter the roughness of the journey) till the end. 
If a believer has delayed manifestation of God's promised blessings and he is not bothered (delayed marriage or childbirth), he has steadfast faith. 


Point-2&#58; The deceptiveness of external fruit without essential faith of

James 2&#58;17-

i.  The superficial fruit without faith in the Savior 

Hosea 10&#58;1-2, 12; Mathew 23&#58;23,25,28
Some people bring fruit only unto themselves. A true believer Acts based on what he believes God will love, not what will produce praise of men.
Love, mercy, faith, judgment are the weightier matters of the law.
Isaiah 58&#58;1-2; Revelations 2&#58;4-5, 19-20
There are people that like to come to church, a participate in all the activities of the church but their character is not borne of the Spirit of God. These are superficial believers. 

ii. The spectacular fruit without the fruit of the Spirit 

Mathew 7&#58;21; 1 Corinthians 13&#58;1-; Mathew 24&#58;24-25
Some people produce spectacular fruits (do miracles, healing, deliverance, prophesy, preach spectacularly) and yet, are far from God. 
 Our priority should be on having the fruit of the Spirit, not the gift of the Spirit.
Some people, when they see miracles, prayer, they do not examine the doctrine of the person.

2 Thessalonians 2&#58;9-22


iii. The simulated fruit without the foundation of surrender 
Many people are ready to serve God according to their will but are not ready to surrender unto Him. 
Luke 16&#58;13-16; Romans 10&#58;3

Point-3&#58; The demand of explicit faith with expected fruit
James 2&#58;18,19


i.  Examine your faith in the light of the new testament 

2 Corinthians 13&#58;5
We need to always examine our lives and be sure we are truly on our way to heaven.
2 Corinthians 13&#58;5

ii. Evaluate your faith in line with noticeable trembling 
Psalms 2&#58;11-12; Phillipians 2&#58;12-15
Do we ever tremble over anything we read in the Bible?
Whatever you cannot do without murmuring, don't do it. It brings condemnation.

iii. Express your faith with a life of noble truthfulness 

Anywhere you are, express your faith, what you believe whether there arw enemies or not. Whenever we tell a lie, we are afraid of the man before me and have forgotten the anger of God at the lie we are telling. When you are living a lie, you are living in bondage.

Ephesians 6&#58;13, 15 

To be a believer in Deeper Life for all these many years and there is an attack, and then I am running to prayer house for help? something is wrong with me.
