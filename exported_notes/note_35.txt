Point-1&#58; The present placement of temporal blessings these end times 
1Timothy 4&#58;1-2

a. The wealth and promotion of the godly
Romans 8&#58;14-17
The brother of lowly degree can rejoice because all his needs are provided by God. His wealth that He has given to us, nothing can destroy them. 

b. The withering and the passing away of grass
James 1&#58;10; 1 Peter 1&#58;24; Psalm 103&#58; 15-16
The place we labored so much to be known and respected, we return there later and nobody knows us. Why all the labor for recognition and praise by those who want to be rich and famous?

1 John 2&#58; 15-16
When we leave at burial, nobody will bury our account books and buildings with us. When Queen Elizabeth was being buried, all the paraphernalia of office were taken from her. The officiating priest referred to her as, our sister, Elizabeth, he removed the title, crown & the Sceptre of office. Empty we came empty, empty we will go. 

c. The wastage and the perishing of the goodliness
James 1&#58;11; Isaiah 40&#58;6-8; Luke 12&#58;16-21. 
Some people cannot come to the Bible study because their ground (business, academics, etc.) bringeth plentifully. 
The rich fool was a man of planning, activity and in the midst of all these, he had no plan for where he would spend his eternity. He knew he had a soul and did not make any plans for the soul; he thought his plans for his flesh for many years were actually plans for his soul. But God has the final say. He was wrong then as many are wrong today. 

Point-2&#58; The profitable perseverance of believers while enduring temptations.

a. Resisting the tempter with the sword of the word 
If Christ could be tempted, who are we to expect otherwise. The Lord gives me the grace to say no. If Satan was so daring and courageous to tempt Christ, the first man without sin and the first woman without sin. Who are we? As Christ resisted with the sword of the Word, so will we.
Satan does not concentrate on one channel of temptation and can tempt us in any direction. But whichever way he chooses, the grace of God can give us victory. We will know by God's grace when temptation shows up but we will need to be watchful and prayerful over this

The Lord was hungry after fasting for 40 days and, His temptation came from there. If we are hungry for the pleasure of the flesh, food, knowledge, power, etc. that is where our temptation will come from cos our resistance is lowest there

Important to note that after Christ quoted the Bible to Satan in verse 4, Satan himself quoted another verse of the Bible for Christ in verse 5. Christ countered the misquoted scripture with another scripture. When Christ quotes the Bible, He always wants to guide us in the direction of 'light for the world'; when Satan quotes the Bible, he always wants to guide us in the direction of 'darkness for the world'. 

What God intends to give you, do not out of impatience, attempt to get it prematurely from the devil. He tried that with Christ and Christ resisted him. We should too. 

We should be bold to command Satan to get out of our way when Satan through his servants are misquoting the Bible to mislead/guide us towards darkness in the world.

b. Refusing appealing temptations in supplication with willingness
When you are praying for grace to resist and you want to overcome, be sure you are not already desirous of what Satan is enticing you with. Be truly willing to reject what Satan is offering. You must pray that your flesh must come to the same desire level as your spirit.

c. Rejecting attractive temptation with the steadfastness of the warrior. 
Luke 8&#58;13-14; 1 Timothy 6&#58;9-10. 
Be careful that when the Prince of the air sends his emissary to you with attractive proposals, you do not fall for it. It has stopped many from bearing fruits

Point-3&#58; The promises for prized tried beneficiaries in endless triumph

a. Reversing the cravings of thoughtless, trampled beasts. 
Jude 1&#58;10-13
Many people are corrupted by the things they possess&#58; wealth, knowledge, beauty, position, etc. 
We should not speak evil of dignitaries just because we have such possessions. 
Unsanctified believers because of the corruption inside them, foam out their own shame. When men foam out their shame, they have lost grace.

b. Renewing the consecration of tested, trusted believers
Philippians 3&#58;7-10, 13-14
When you choose to abandon the natural gift you have that enables you to commit sin effortlessly, you have counted that natural gift as loss for the name of Christ. We should deliberately give up these. 

c. Receiving the crown of tried, triumphant bride
2Timothy 4&#58;5-8
Watch over all situations around you&#58; when crises come up, promotion comes up, friendship comes along, enemy comes along; watch thou in all things. Be ready at all times, even this night. Are there things that you can say, Christ should not meet me in this condition? Be ready!

I have fought a good fight&#58; fighting against Satan, the flesh, corruption, is a good fight, not a bad one.
