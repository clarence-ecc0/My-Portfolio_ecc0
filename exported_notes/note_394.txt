James 3&#58;8-12
In these verses, James is not talking about himself or other Sanctified believers but he is talking about the carnal man who is uncircumcised and because he is like that, his tongue cannot be controlled/tamed. 


Point-1&#58; The contradictions in the unstable tongue in an unconverted heart. 
It is the unconverted heart that makes it impossible to tame the tongue. 

James 3&#58;8-9
Sweet and bitter
The same tongue brings blessings cursing; something pleasant and at the same time in the same person, something painful. It's like the same source of water producing bitter and sweet water at the same time, on the same tree, we see paw paw and banana? 

Tender and threatening
We see people that are tender and also threatening. This ought to be so. We saw that in the dealing of Pharaoh and Moses. 

Confession and covetousness 
We see this in Balaam. He said he wouldn't go to Balak and yet kept consulting God if he should go. He prayed to die the death of the righteous and yet covetousness was in the heart. When he couldn't get the money from Balak, he still gave him counsel that destroyed many Israelites 

Blessing and corruption 
Balaam was like this also. Would bless Israel yet teaching Balak how to corrupt them. 

Praise and murmuring 
We see this among Israel (Exodus 15&#58;11) and in the same chapter, they are murmuring (Exodus 15&#58;24). This shows the heart is not converted and is responsible for the double talk (praising and murmuring)

Willing and willful
Jeremiah 42&#58;1-7; 44&#58;16-17
This was the situation of Israel at the time of Jeremiah. Willing on the one hand (running to the Bible study) and yet exhibiting their willfulness (live their life of sins)

Much love yet much disloyalty 
Ezekiel 33&#58;30-32

Delightful and deceptive
The example of Pilate who will say he found no fault in Christ and yet will crucify Christ.

Nice and naughty 

If we have this kind of heart described above, we are still unsanctified and need to be Sanctified.
