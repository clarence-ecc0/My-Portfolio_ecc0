Satan's eye is still on more people but he will fail if the people willingly come to God for protection. (Isaiah 49&#58;24-26)

HINT&#58; Destruction of the idols&#58; We shall enquire of those that want their idols destroyed and follow them to their homes to destroy them. 

THE 'NEARLY CHRISTIAN'
✓ Many are like King Agrippa who was 'almost a Christian until this day'. Almost a Christian is not a Christian just as "He almost passed the exam" implies that he failed the exam. Be among those that are 'altogether a Christian until the day of death.

✓ The Bible described some people as "ever learning but never able to come to the knowledge of the truth". There are many like that in the church. A key objective of the crusade is to make learners come to the knowledge of the truth, and to live life by it, and to depart this world in it. 

✓ There are some people that have abandoned God's way because the person they were looking up to did that. This is an error! You can't abandon God's way because your pastor (who first taught you about God) has backslided and abandoned God's way. That will be unwise for you. The wisest thing that you can do is to pray for him and God shall accept him again If he comes back otherwise, he will pay with his life in eternity. 

Pursue peace at all costs. 
✓ God's word teaches&#58; "follow peace with all men". This is necessary for anyone to see God and yet, there is no human being on earth that can do this by his power. Satan will mobilize people to try you sore until you fail. Your wife, neighbors, classmates, leadership of market association, etc. You can't do it by your power but with grace, it can be done.             ✓ Remember the story of Isaac whom God said should remain even when there was famine. They took his well, and another and he did not fight back. God gave him 100fold (not 💯) increase. God said, "vengeance is mine". Do not take the place of God.  ✓ Obedience to God in a difficult situation always bring increase. Jacob was cheated in Laban's house and everything that Laban had became his eventually; Lot took the best land but God restored everything to Abraham. Trust God to fulfill His own promise because He will. 
✓ Do not live your life in fear of "they will take me for a fool, a weakling, etc." These thoughts are from Satan. Actually, fear come from Satan, trying to provoke you to miss God's blessings. 

The half-cleverness of sinners&#58; 
✓ When men want to do evil, they look at the front, back, right and left and, if they see no one, then they do it. But, they never look upward, where the Judge was watching from. Neither do they look at the ground that was a witness, nor at their shadow that was a faithful witness. 
✓ When Cain killed his brother, daddy & mummy did not see him but God saw him and the earth witnessed it. His curse came from the earth and his punishment also (Genesis 4&#58;10-12). When King Coniah did great wickedness in the land, his curse/punishment came from the earth (Jeremiah 22&#58;29-30). 
✓ Some wicked people are constantly under a curse from their own house, not from the people they cheated but the stones in the wall, and the timber of their houses (Habakkuk 2&#58;9-11)

Foolishness in some wisdom and wisdom in some foolishness&#58; 
✓ In the ministry work that we are called into, there is a serious need to learn how to be a fool so that we can make progress with God's work. 
✓ We have an example of Christ who knew Judas Iscariot was a thief but kept up with him for the work the Father gave to him to be accomplished. We may say today (carnally) that that wasn't wise. 
✓ We also have the example of Paul and Barnabas that God Himself set up as a team. Satan struck, John Mark left them (ref) and the argument that ensued led to the disbandment of the team. A bit of tolerating the other person would have preserved the fellowship. Later on in life, Paul requested for the same John Mark that he was profitable to him in the ministry (2 Timothy 4&#58;1). 

Non-transferability of the benefits of righteousness. (Ezekiel 14&#58;13-20)
✓ Key lessons from above verses.  Righteousness delivers from adversities. The benefits of righteousness  are not transferrable to close relations. They can be enjoyed for a while as God waits for them to change (Romans 2&#58;4-11)

✓ You may have been born in church, baptized in church, served in church, could have married in church, died in church and still end up in hell, because you were only in the church and not in Christ.
✓It is necessary to be born again, take up our cross and follow Jesus. Many have religion, but have no relation with the living God!
