Joshua 1&#58;8; 24&#58;15; Psalm 119; Ephesians 5&#58;21-25

INTRODUCTION 
The manual that Abraham followed such that God trusted him. Genesis 18&#58;19

Joshua was commanded to follow this manual. Towards the end of his ministry, he told the people that he and his household would follow it even if others wouldn't. Joshua 1&#58;8; 24&#58;15
The manual that Moses' mother and father followed and preserved him, not fearing the threat of Pharaoh.
The godly manual that Jonadab used to train his children (the Rachabites) such that not even prophet Jeremiah , the prophet over the nations, in the very house of God and under the instruction of God to test them, when he persuaded them to do wrong, they would not.

The manual that my friend Lan was given by his father that he should abandon the common idols of the community.

When the Father and the mother submit to the authority of the manual, no power on earth can bring them down. It is the brick for building a spiritual house. No storm, wind or flood can bring down the house. 
It contains the rules of life for a godly home, made by the owner of life. It consists of commandments, statues, judgment, laws, precepts, instructions, testimonies, way, words, ordinances). Psalms 119 from the first verse unto the last (176) speaks of it except in 3 verses (90, 122 & 132).

For a godly home, the manual for a Christian home has instructions for all (children, parents, helpers). Try and imagine the way of life in Abraham's home (Abraham, Sarah, 

The rules of life concerning the children in a godly home&#58;
Hand over your children to God from early life as baby Samuel, Timothy were by their mothers.

Teach them the rules of life practically&#58; by words and demonstration 

Believe, then show them that the favours bestowed and the dangers escaped by the family were not borne out of good luck but through the grace and mercy of God at work in the home.

Teach them to learn how to speak to God (prayer) & trust Him for their needs before they speak to Daddy/mummy. Direct them to go and talk to the BIG Daddy of the family first whenever they mistakenly come to you without talking to Him.

Teach them to learn how to wait upon the Lord for answers to their prayers.

Avoid favoritism for any of the children 


The rules of life concerning parents in a godly home
Submit to God first. You can only lead people on the road that you yourself have traveled on. A laborer must be the first partaker of his labor. 
Then, submit to spouse next (in godliness). The scripture says, submit ye one to another.

Shield the children from the marauders in the world. Watch what the children wear (especially the female children), watch and hear.
Our wives should like the virtuous woman (Proverbs 31). They are decision makers (the Shunammite woman, Esther) ; they are people who fear/love God (Ruth, Rahab); they are meek promise keepers (Hannah)

Show love to one another without dissimulation, the godly love of Christ (Christlike love). Showing love does not mean agreeing to every request. In fact, love may actually lead to the opposite. We disciplined the children out of love, not hatred.

Respect by all to all. (1 Peter 3&#58;1, 5-9)


Treat your house helps as children God has given you to support, not as slaves. Relationship does not come through biological birth alone. Even nature has shown us that we can have fathers, mothers, brothers, and sisters through marriage (inlaws).

ONLY say yes to your partner's request after God has said yes to you. Neighbors/relations prosperity means nothing to me; they can't change the plans for my life.
Gal 1&#58;10

The rules of life concerning helpers in a godly home&#58;
Show respect to your master, not as eye service.
Obey the golden rule&#58; do unto them as you would want someone else to do unto you when you too become a master.

See your service to your masters as service unto God.
Faithfulness in all things.

In general&#58;
If we are able to follow these instructions, there is no doubt that there will be peace and progress in the home. Prayers will always be answered. Fears of things or one another will be kicked out of the home, etc. 
There is one that will be greatly disappointed if you live your life like this. His injurious project in your family will be terminated. He is Satan and he will do everything to prevent you from living your life in a godly way.
If you are sincere, if you are serious, you have the power to resist him. Gen 1&#58;28; Mathew 28&#58;18; Luke 10&#58;19; Mark 26&#58;17-18,20

May our homes be like the home of Zechariah, Elisabeth and John the Baptist where everyone was filled with the Spirit of God in Jesus name.
