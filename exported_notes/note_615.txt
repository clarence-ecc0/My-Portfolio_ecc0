Point-1&#58;
A. Superficial conformity without real conversion

B. The scripture comprehension without reformed conscience

C. The superhuman connection without redemptive Christ

It is only those that continue to do the will of the Father in heaven that will enter heaven, not those that claim to have superhuman connection (with which they did many wonderful works) and yet have no relationship with God. From the time you were performing miracles up till now, the day of reckoning, I never knew you; not that I knew you before and no longer know you but, "I never knew you". That is Christ's answer.


Point- 2&#58; The mainstream experiences of divine life through Christ 
2 Peter 1&#58;3
The things that God gave to His children, is there evidence that you received them, that you have escaped the corruption that is in the world

A. Salvation with peace and humility
Romans 5&#58;1-2; 15&#58;13
A truly saved soul has peace with God. And because we know that we were not saved with the works of our hands, we have no pride but are humble before God and man.

B. Sanctification with purity of heart
John 17&#58;6,9, 14-16,17; 1 Thessalonians 5&#58;22-24


C. Spirit baptism with power from on high 
Luke 24&#58;49; Acts 1&#58;4-5,8; 2&#58;39

Point- 3&#58; The Messiah's expectations of deeper life without controversy 

A. Deeper and deeper in submission 
Mathew 26&#58;39
Christ was deeper in His submission to the Father, "not My will but Thine be done". We are expected to be like this. 

B. Lower and lower in selflessness 
Philippians 2&#58;3-5, 14-16
We need to esteem others better than ourselves. We shouldn't be concentrating on ourselves only but always look at what will benefit others. 


C. Better and better in serving others
Luke 22&#58;25-27
Christ does not want any man to be seen as a benefactor in His church. That is the way of the kings of the gentiles. 

D. Brighter and brighter in service
Isaiah 30&#58;26
If my life is shining brighter and brighter, then I am in deeper life. If otherwise (possibly because of circumstances), my light is shining lower and lower, I am not living a deeper life. 

E. Cleaner and closer to the Savior
Ephesians 5&#58;26
The deeper is the type that is Holy without blemish 


F. Freer and fresher in the spirit
Galatians 5&#58;22
All the fruit of the Spirit are fresh experiences- faith, love, self-control 

G. Purer and whiter in secret
In the secret, we are pure and white.
1 Thessalonians 5&#58;21-24
Whatever you see people do (including leaders), do not follow follow, but you prove all things and only hold fast that which is good


H. Nearer and nearer to His stature
Ephesians 4&#58;11-14
The apostles being mentioned are people that watch what they say or do, they are sober, etc. They do not prophesy because they want to please people or to be acceptable to the people but they speak according to the will of God. 
In deeper life, we must move on until we reach up to the measure of the stature of Christ


I. Stronger and stronger by His strength 
Because we are getting stronger and stronger by His strength, the reaction that would have silenced us in the past, we are able to say those things today not minding their reaction or action.
Job 17&#58;9

J. Richer and richer in the spiritual 
Ephesians 3&#58;17-19


K. Higher and higher in sacrifice 
In deeper life, it is higher and higher sacrifice not lower and lower sacrifice.
2 Samuel 24&#58;24
What I offer unto the Lord must come from my sweat, something I value in my life, something that costs me much.

L. Steadier and steadier in suffering 
Previously, we were easily jolted when some happened but now we are more steadfast in suffering.
Acts 20&#58;24
Some people, just making a jest of their faith, it is enough for them to move, if there is a downturn in the biz, that is enough for them to move. 
But if these do not move us (not even Nebuchadnezzar), that is a deeper life experience.
