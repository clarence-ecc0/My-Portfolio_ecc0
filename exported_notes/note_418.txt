{{text:BEGORE HE CAME TO DIE 
His teaching, crying out to man on how to re-enter the paradise
In Proverbs chapter 8 and 9, we see the indescribable love of Christ as He showed us the events at the beginning of creation as well as the way back to live the paradise life. 

✓ We were the love of His life and He opened His mouth to cry out to us in wisdom (Proverbs 8&#58;1-3), upon the high places of the earth (our heart Where He is knocking) to the simple (Proverbs 9&#58;3-4), inviting him to come and feast on the bread He has prepared (Proverbs 8&#58;5) while admonishing the simple (unbelievers ) to ignore the foolish woman (Satan's agents) also crying at the high places of the earth (the heart) to them to turn in and eat stolen water & bread in secret/darkness (Proverbs 8&#58;15-17) ignorantly going to their death (Proverbs 9&#58;18). 

Stolen food is sweet, stolen wealth/property is sweet, stolen sexual gratification is sweet, stolen pleasure, consumed in secret is sweet..... but, the eaters know not that the other guests that went to the feast of the clamorous woman before them are in the depths of hell.

There are many people that have died from this community and have gone. Do you know where they are right now? I can assure you of this from the word of God that, if they accepted the invitation of the clamorous woman and feasted on her stolen things, they are in the depth of hell right now. 

Why will anyone here today want to join them there? God forbid. God has pleaded with man to turn away from sin and live (Ezekiel 18&#58;31-32). Won't you heed, Won't you consider the invitation of Christ? Paradise is still possible. Paradise is for forgiven sinners. Everyone that you will see in paradise is an ex-sinner, even Enoch. 

✓ Christ is indeed Wisdom and Understanding (Proverbs 8&#58;12,14). He is the promised Messiah of God who was with the Father from the very beginning (Proverbs 8&#58;22-23, 26-28) and the love of His life (out of all that He created) was the man whom He created (Proverbs 8&#58;30-31). He has given us the ultimate warning on how to re-enter paradise and live there eternally (Proverbs 8&#58;32-35), or remain eternally separated (death) from God in hell (Proverbs 8&#58;36).

AFTER HE CAME TO DIE 
His sacrifice that enables sinners to return to paradise
He was despised by MAN whom He came to save (Isaiah 53&#58;3)
He was stricken, smitten of God yet man (the Pharisees and the leadership of Israel and the gentiles) esteemed (believed) that God struck Him down for His own offences- He spoke against the temple, the law and Moses (Isaiah 53&#58;4)

But (the Holy Ghost says), He was coming to be wounded for our transgressions, and to be bruised for our iniquities and the chastisement (beating/punishment) for us to have peace again (re-enter paradise) was laid upon Him by the Father (Isaiah 53&#58;5). 

The Father was satisfied with His Judicial system (His administration of justice) that laid the price for our redemption upon Christ (Isaiah 53&#58;10) because through it, He will justify us and bring us back to paradise (Isaiah 53&#58;11). See what Christ did that none else could do for us, not even the animal sacrifice which was merely a symbol of what Christ came to do&#58;
✓ He poured out His soul unto death 
✓ He was numbered with the transgressors (He died with robbers who represent sinners like us)
✓ He bared the sins of many (us)
✓ He made intercessions for the transgressors (us)

As He said in Proverbs 8&#58;36, those that hate/reject Him love death (separation from God; remain outside paradise). Remember Revelations 22&#58;15.
