{{text:HOW MUCH IS ENOUGH?
This world is like an invitation to a buffet. You can only take what you can consume; you can't go home with anything, for anyone. They have to come by themselves and eat their portion, and go home. 
A man saw pounded yam, jollof rice, roasted yam with palm oil, goat meat, cow meat, fish, chicken, bush meat, etc. Only a glutton will gather all that his stomach cannot accommodate. Such is the material thing of this world. Only a glutton will want to gather all the wealth of this world. 

Many fought and tried to gather much and they have gone home (dead); we are here gathering and we too will leave the buffet and go home, and new people will come after us and will gather and still, they too will go home. Do men remember this when they poison others so that they can take their land, when boys kill one another so that they can possess a girlfriend. Nations have gone to war over women, and land, and wealth. Eventually, they left them behind. Remember Alexander the great; Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon, etc. where are they now? They didn't go away with the land. We are here living on it. 

Gluttony has no extra advantage over moderate eating.

"And when they did mete it with an omer, he that gathered much had nothing over, and he that gathered little had no lack; they gathered every man according to his eating." (Exodus 16&#58;18)

The study of the blood of a man that picks and eats food from a rubbish dump and the one that is well fed showed an insignificant difference in nutritional content. Gorging ourselves with food does not have extra advantages.

 ✓ It is God that teaches us how to profit, the way to do our biz and gain. (Isaiah 48&#58;17). If we allow Him to teach us, we will make good, sustainable profit. Honest profit might be small in the beginning but in the end it will be large & enduring. 

The hypocrisy of man is great. 
✓ All that we have came to us as gifts from God (1 Corinthians 4&#58;7). Man receives the gift of a thing or person - child, wife, cars, etc. and on these that he possessed as gifts, he exercises so much authority/dominion, desiring and commanding that his WILL be done upon them. If he wants his car painted black, the car has no Will of its own but must submit and be painted black. What he expects of his wife or children is what they must be/do, and he created none of these. 
✓ Here is the hypocrisy of man - God did not only create the man but even the elements that were used to create man, including the spirit of God (the breath of life) that made man a living soul all came from God. And yet, man who acknowledges that God created him will want his own WILL, rather than that of God to be done always. That which he wants done unto him by what and whom he owns, he cannot permit himself to do unto God who owns him. An error. 
✓ Imagine a servant who is a supervisor over other servants of his master. This supervisor commands complete authority over the other servants and yet will not submit to the authority of his master who made him the supervisor. An error indeed.
