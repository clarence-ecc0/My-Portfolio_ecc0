Point-1&#58; The purchased redemption and remission of sins

Romans 3&#58;23-26; Colossians 1&#58;12-14; Hebrews 9&#58;12,14-16,26-27
No matter the wealth of Zacheus, his money could not redeem him. All of humanity is justified freely (without cost) that is seeking for God. You have reason to praise God
Humanity is released/redeemed from slavery to Satan. We are sinners (Satan's slaves) by nature, habit. I am redeemed from sin, slavery and sickness. 
At creation, man had no sickness because he was created in God's likeness !which had no sickness). It was after Genesis chapter 3 that sickness came through the devil. The forbidden fruit came with sickness.
 
Since Satan was stronger than Adam, he was stronger than all of his descendants. Liberation from Satan depends on God's mercy. 
We need deliverance from self. Self destroyed Nebuchadnezzar, Absalom, Herold, Dathan and Abiram. We need deliverance from self but we cannot deliver ourselves.

Point-2&#58; The provided redemption and recovery from all sicknesses 

Galatians 3&#58;13
The sicknesses in you that you see today, you will see them no more. GS declared.
Christ has redeemed us from the curse of the law. Christ was made a curse that the blessings of Abraham might come upon the gentiles (us).
Curse came from men. Goliath and old man cursed David but it could get to David because David had hidden himself in the tabernacle of the Lord. I am hidden in the tabernacle of the Lord and the curse of men cannot get to me in Jesus name. Jesus has been made a curse for me that the blessings of Abraham would come unto me. 
Abraham's blessings include dead organs came alive, had good eyesight at hundred years to see the three visitors, he was strong and could (without the servants) slaughter Isaac as a Sacrifice.
I have the physical, spiritual and emotional blessings of Abraham. 
What I confess is what I possess.

All Abraham did was through faith. I receive a new level of faith tonight and will do all that Abraham did.
