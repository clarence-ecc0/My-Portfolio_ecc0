TOPIC&#58; THE HEAVENLY BENEFITS OF UNITY AMONG TRUE BRETHREN 

Psalms 133&#58;1-3
There are blessings that money cannot buy. When we are united in God these blessings will come upon us.
The blessings we derive from unity is both good and pleasant. Some good things are not pleasant (e.g., drugs that treat our sicknesses are good but are not pleasant). God's blessings when we are united are both good and pleasant.

Point-1&#58; The pleasant unity through enduring love
Psalms 133&#58;1

A. Transformed for unity
For brethren to live in unity, there must be transformation first; a heart that fears God first 
Jeremiah 32&#58;39; Ezekiel 11&#58;19-29; John 17&#58;17-18,21

B. Together in unity
Psalms 133&#58;1; Romans 16&#58;5-6
A transformed wife should be able to stay together in unity with the husband. Similarly, children should be able to stay together in unity with their parents. If this is not the case, another visitation to the cross is required. 


C. Actively dwelling together 
Psalms 133&#58;1
When the devil knows that your breakthrough is at hand, he causes disunity. Families need to watch out for the devil's intrusion especially when they are praying (waiting on God) for something.

Point-2&#58; The precious uniqueness of excelling leadership 
Psalms 133&#58;2
Our blessings when we are united is compared to the  precious OINTMENT that was poured upon AARON that ran upon his  GARMENT

A. The uniqueness of excelling leadership

Exodus 7&#58;1-2


B. The uniqueness of the garment 
Exodus 28&#58;1-
Aaron's garment was for glory and beauty. God had to anoint men specially to prepare his garment.
When God looks upon the unity of brethren, He sees something as precious, of great worth as the garment He has to specially give the spirit to two men to put together 


C. The uniqueness of the ointment 
Exodus 30&#58; 22-23
God commanded that PRINCIPAL spices of great worth should be used to prepare the ointment. When God sees our unity, its worth is like that ointment He Himself caused to be make for Aaron's anointing. Principal spices were chicken by God in its making.

Point-3&#58; The perpetual usefulness unto eternal life
Psalms 133&#58;3; Isaiah 55&#58;10-11
Every word of God that comes to us from His pulpit shall not return to Him void but they shall accomplish the purpose for which God sent it to us through His servants.
As we see plants that are wilting in the evening and yet, when the dews of heaven fall upon them during the night hours, they become reinvigorated and are thriving again in the morning. Such is what happens to weary servant of God when God releases His dews upon him or her. There will NOT come a time when the servants of God will be useless. They are perpetually useful. Although they may become tired because of the heat of the day yet, when the night of rest comes, God commands His dews upon them and they are reinvigorated again in the morning.

Deuteronomy 32&#58;2; Exodus 16&#58;13-16
Special provision as manna came as the dews of the Lord fell upon the ground. We need the dews from heaven to provide for us what we cannot afford and this comes when we are united.
The dews also refer to the word of God that refreshes us and breaks the curse of the enemy upon us. The Lord will be as a dew unto us.
