{{text:✓ In Christianity, pride of what you have achieved in ministry is foolishness. All that we have individually achieved is what we were enabled to do, as well as commanded to do. Christ exemplified this in the story of the house master and his servant in Luke 17&#58;7-10


Luke 17&#58;7-10
[7&#93;But which of you, having a servant plowing or feeding cattle, will say unto him by and by, when he is come from the field, Go and sit down to meat?

[8&#93;And will not rather say unto him, Make ready wherewith I may sup, and gird thyself, and serve me, till I have eaten and drunken; and afterward thou shalt eat and drink?

[9&#93;Doth he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded him? I trow not.

[10&#93;So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants&#58; we have done that which was our duty to do.

None is able to pay off the principal sum paid for his soul not to talk of making profit for God. This is because the money paid for our redemption is very costly,it is the precious, sinless blood of Jesus Christ. That's what Apostle Peter meant in 1 Peter 1&#58;18-19

1 Peter 1&#58;18-19
[18&#93;Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers;

[19&#93;But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot&#58;

The book of Psalms made reference to the costly price paid for our redemption and concluded that none is able to pay it back or off.

Psalms 49&#58;6-8
[6&#93;They that trust in their wealth, and boast themselves in the multitude of their riches;
[7&#93;None of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him&#58;
[8&#93;(For the redemption of their soul is precious, and it ceaseth for ever&#58;)

✓ Looking at what you have done for Christ in the ministry where He placed you, empowered you and gave you your commission to execute, do you think that you have paid off Christ's investment over your life? 

✓ With what- Gold, silver, offerings, tithes, service? Think again. 

May the Lord give us the wisdom to understand how poor we are to pay off Christ investment in us. The day we know of this is the day pride in ministry achievement will leave our mind.
