TOPIC&#58; THE SUFFERINGS THAT STRENGTHEN GOD'S SERVANTS 

The servants of the Lord suffer. Suffering can build us up if we have the right attitude but if we have the wrong attitude, it can break us down. Persecution can bruise us, tear us apart or toughen us, develop us and make us more resilient, and have a stronger backbone. 

1 Peter 1&#58;6-8; 2&#58;19-21; 3&#58;17-18; 4&#58;1,13-16; 5&#58;1, 10-11
Many believers and preachers do not understand we can suffer wrongfully. God is glorified if we suffer wrongfully for doing the right thing (evangelize well, preach well, do good). Christ suffered in order to bring us to God, the same thing for us, that we might also bring sinners to God. We are partakers of Christ suffering if our suffering  is bringing sinners to Christ. 


Point-1&#58; The support of a good conscience while suffering 

1 Peter 3&#58;16-18; Hebrews 10&#58;22
While suffering, check your conscience that it is for Christ. There are various types of consciences.

#Evil conscience 
#Defiled conscience&#58; There is a defiled conscience. Unto the pure, everything is pure Titus 1&#58;15
#Accusing conscience&#58; Romans 2&#58;15
#Convicted conscience. The sinners conscience convicts them. John 8&#58;9
#Seared conscience. 1 Timothy 4&#58;2. A conscience that does not accuse us when we sin or have sinned is a seared conscience. It takes men to hell. 
Acts 24&#58;16; Hebrews 9&#58;14; 
#Good conscience&#58; 1 Timothy 1&#58;5. This is one of purged conscience.
Romans 9&#58;1. Here Paul is saying, what I am saying here is exactly what I will say if Christ is here with me. Also, that what I am saying, the Holy Ghost bears me witness that I am speaking the truth.

2 Corinthians 1&#58;12; 4&#58;2; 1 Timothy 1&#58;19-20; 3&#58;9; Hebrews 13&#58;18
The New Testament makes a lot about our conscience. Good believers are conscious of this and it guides them in their life decisions. 
From the beginning, up to the end of his ministry, apostle Paul was always checking up on his conscience.


Point-2&#58;  The strength of a godly character free from sinning 

1 Peter 4&#58;1-5; Psalms 4&#58;3-4; Exodus 20&#58;20
He that has suffered in the flesh (is suffering from persecution because he would not sin) hath ceased from sin. God called us unto Himself that we sin not.
People that commit sin act as if they do not believe in God. If they believe God is seeing them as they commit sin, and God is recording their offences, to administer His judgement against them at the appointed time, they will not continue. 

Psalm 39&#58;1; 119&#58;1; John 5&#58;14; 8&#58;11; Romans 6&#58;2, 7-8, 11, 18, 22; 1 Corinthians 15&#58;33-34; 1 John 3&#58;8-10; 5&#58;18
Christ does not give us license to go and sin. He forgives us, saves us and directs us to sin no more. If we have heard and believed in the word of God and continue to sin, we do not belong to Christ but are of the devil. Awake to holiness and sin not.
Since I am born of God, Satan, my manager, etc. cannot force, coerce me to sin. If I sin, it is because there was a transaction and I desired what was on offer more than salvation (e.g., keeping my job) and therefore chose to sin. I cannot loose my job or property if God doesn't want me to loose that job or property. I cannot die before my time if God does not want me to die then. Christ has taken the sting of death away from me and my Household.


Point-3&#58; Our suffering for the great commission as good steward

  1 Peter 4&#58;10-19
Whenever I speak or minister, let it be as that it came from God so that God will be glorified in my speech or ministration. In doing this, I may suffer but the end result is that God is glorified. The Lord will give me the stamina to stand in the face of opposition to declare the good news with boldness in Jesus name.
When or if we suffer, we should commit the keeping of our souls to God
