Point-1&#58; The perfect gifts from the Father by the word.
James 2&#58;17-18

a. The precious gift of salvation for sonship
John &#58;1&#58;12-13; Ephesians 2&#58;8-9; Titus 2&#58;11-14
Salvation is a gift from God. It is not a product of your struggle, but a product of God's grace. 
Frivolity, foolish talks get out and sobriety comes in when grace comes in .


b. The purifying gift of sanctification and steadfastness.
John 17&#58;17,20-21; Hebrews 13&#58;12-114; 1 Thessalonians 5&#58;22-24; Ephesians 5&#58;25-27
Christ wants that both the early disciples (Mathew, John, Peter, etc.), and later disciples (is) are sanctified. Sanctification does not scatter believers but unite them together. It makes the church a glorious church. 
Those that do not strive to be sanctified want the church to retain some or all of its depravity, like the world. 


c. The powerful gift of the Spirit
Acts 1&#58;4-5, 8; 2&#58;33,28, 36,39; 1 Corinthians 12&#58;1,7-11; Luke 11&#58;13
If natural people (evil naturally) give good gifts to their children who ask for them, how much more will God who is good, give the Holy Spirit (a good gift) to His children that ask for it.

Point-2&#58; Profitable graciousness as first fruits through the Word. 
James 1&#58;19-21

When there is pride, we will not be able to receive the word of God. We must be meak. The wrath of man (in any way/form and for any explanation) cannot work the righteousness of God. 

a. Renewing the en-nobling wonders of swift hearing & slow speaking
James 1&#58;19; Ecclesiastes 5&#58;2
Swift to speak and to wrath and, slow to hear are the things that bring disunity in families. Fast interpretation of other people's actions lead to disunity. If we are going to have peace in the church, family, society, everyone should be slow to speak, fast to hear and slow to wrath. Do not be too hasty to utter or do anything. 

b. Renouncing the ensnaring word of superfluity 
James 1&#58;20; I Peter 2&#58;1,11
If the pastor, or leaders, or workers are angry, the church will not be able to save sinners that are in the church. Malice (angers, already settled in us), wrath must therefore be laid aside. 

c. Retaining the engrafted for sustained salvation 
James 1&#58;21b; Mark 4&#58;20,24; 1 Thessalonians 2&#58;13
We need to show respect to apostle Paul and the rest. God revealed unsearchable mysteries to him, he went to the third heaven. We should be careful of how we equate ourselves to such people that God gave special privileges highly rated them (e.g., Moses). We shouldn't say the words written by Paul are the words of Paul but rate them as they are in reality, the words of Paul. 


Point-3&#58; Our practical godliness in faithfulness to the word
James 2&#58;22
The essence of Christianity is to be a doer of the word. 
If, as preachers, we ask people to pay tithe but we do not pay ourselves, we are not doers of the word. 
As preachers of sanctification, do we demonstrate sanctification in the home, church and community or we just preach it

a. The doers of the word in all sincerity
Romans 6&#58;17-18; Colossians 3&#58;14-17

b. The deceivers in their waywardness without salvation
Mathew 7&#58;21-23
Eternity has only 2 places, not 3 and we all will be in one of the places or be in the other one. 
Those who say, Lord, Lord, usually are preachers or leaders of ministry. Many will not enter heaven but those that do the will of the Father in heaven. It is not what we see them do but what the Father see in them in their secrets. These are people who count their own words superior to God's word. 
It is not just the ability to do miracles or heal people but it is being able to live that sanctified life.

c. The disciples committed to the word of the scriptures
John 8&#58;31; 15&#58;3-8; 
The words of Christ include forgiveness, restitution, love our neighbors including our enemies. If we continue in all His words, then we will be considered as His true disciples. Anyone that does not have holiness and pursue peace (these are among the words of Christ) shall not see God.
