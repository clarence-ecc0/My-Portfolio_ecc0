{{text:TOPIC&#58;  REFLECTING CHRIST SHINING LIGHT IN A DARK CORRUPT WORLD

Mathew 5&#58;13-16; Proverbs 4&#58;18; Job 17&#58;9; John 9&#58;5; 12&#58;46; 1 John 1&#58;5; Philippians 2&#58;14-16
The light (life) of a believer is like a light that shines more and more unto the perfect day, stronger and stronger every day. A believer has no business with backsliding, rather it is front-sliding.
The word of God in both the old and new testaments are very clear, that God's children must shine as light in the very dark, perverse, crooked world.

The Lord likens the lives of true believers as salt and light that sweetens our environment. 

Point-1&#58; The usefulness and characteristics of salty, sanctified servants.

Mathew 5&#58; 14a; Leviticus 2&#58;13; Ezekiel 43&#58;24; Job 6&#58;6; 2 Kings 2&#58;19-22
The expression salt applies to our private lives, our families, our worship and in the world around us. The salt must not be lacking in all our offerings (worship)

Everything in the church may look good and wonderful but how about the people that are inside the building? Are we nice to ourselves as the building is nice to us? We need to add salt to our relationship in the church. The believer in a community is a healing agent just as salt healed the water of the community in the time of Elisha. 

MARK 9&#58; 49-59; COLOSSIANS 4&#58;5-6;
Every sacrifice shall be salted. Have salt in yourself (i.e, have virtue, desire for God's word, passion, loveliness,etc.).
The opportunity that we have with those outside (unbelievers) should be exploited. We must redeem such time so that we can wisely bring unbelievers into reconciliation with God. Our speeches must be salted as we speak to the unbelievers.

All believers must demonstrate the characteristics of salt which include&#58;
a. Pure and white. Our lives must be pure and white

b. Separate and clean. We keep salt in containers so that it is separate and remains clean. Believers must be separate from the evil and contentious world.

c. Preserved and unsoiled. 

d. Sweet and seasoning. Our lives must be sweet and seasoning to the people around us. We are not unpleasant to those around us. 

e. Useful when diffused. When we are diffused into (mixed with) the people around us, we are useful to them. 

f. Unique and universal. 

g. Preserving and influential. Believers are preserved from sin when they are connected to us. We are able to influence them.

h. Purifying and healing&#58; Does our lives heal people or wound them?

i. Constant and predictable. Saltiness is predictable, sweet sometimes and at other times (when we are annoyed) not sweet

j. Needed by saints and sinners

k. Indispensable irreplaceable. There is no alternative to salt and to you as a believer

l. Serviceable to God and man. Both God and man need us as believers. 


Point-2&#58; The uselessness and casting away of savourless, sandy salt

Mathew 5&#58;13; Mark 9&#58;50; Luke 14&#58;34
Christ made it known that it is possible for salt to lose its savour. We must guide against it as those that have ear to hear.

1 King 9&#58;6-8; 2 King 17&#58;17-20; Luke 9&#58;25; Mathew 3&#58;8, 10; Mathew 7&#58;11; 13&#58;47-45-50; Mathew 25&#58;26-30; John 15&#58;6-7
Sometimes, sudden death is not good because it denies us a chance to examine oneself. There is no second chance to reconcile with the Lord, the Father. 

1 Corinthians 9&#58; 27; Mathew 13&#58;40



Point-3&#58; The useableness and character of shining spotless saints 

Mathew 5&#58;14; ; Romans 13&#58;11-14; Philippians 2&#58;14
Let your light so shine (let your Christianity so shine, shine so brilliantly), before men so that they see your saltiness and glorify our Father in heaven. 
We come to every task and demonstrate our saltiness without any murmuring or debates. 

1 Thessalonians 5&#58;5-9; Acts 26&#58; 18; 1 Peter 2&#58;9; 1 John 1&#58;5; Mathew 13&#58;40-43&#58; Daniel 12&#58;3; Mathew 5&#58;16
To open the eyes of unbelievers is all that the saint of God is expected to do. As God's saints, ambassadors, we are expected to manifest godliness as our character. Our testimonies that we are in the light should manifest in our character exhibited in the community. 

Let your light so shine, so pleasant, so helpful, so revealing (of corrupted things covered up), so convict others, so minister grace to others, so convert others, so glorify God (not self) so that saints and sinners will glorify your Father in heaven.
