{{text:The dual nature of man (spirit and earth). 
✓ The earth, the flesh of man, the plant (as food) and beast/fowl (as meat) are all of one. The Lord God made all of them out of the earth and to the earth they shall all return, to be recycled back again. (Genesis 1&#58;24-25; 2&#58;19; 2&#58;7). 
✓ Only the spirit of man came from God and will return back to Him again, to enter paradise (if he was obedient) or hell (if he rejected God and was in league with Satan/demons in their disobedience).   ✓ What do you know about the eternal abode of your spirit? Have you thought of that recently? Dreams show us that spirits can experience pleasure/rest or sorrow/fear. One or the other of these two is waiting for all men. You can't escape both.

The many spirits of man 
✓ A man has one and more spirits. It is important that a man finds out what other spirits are in him because that knowledge will show him where he will spend his eternity. There is a common spirit that all men have from God and it is the spirit that keeps us alive, our breath of life that made us a living soul (Genesis 2&#58;7 and Ecclesiastes 12&#58;7). 

✓ But the Bible tells us there are other spirits that are in man working with and influencing the one spirit God gave us. These other spirits can remain in man or leave him. Christ mentioned it several times (seven more wicked spirits dwelling with one that was first delivered, (Mathew 12&#58;43-45). We also know that in some people, the Holy Spirit from God dwells in them (e g., the Holy Spirit dwelth in David 1 Samuel 16&#58;13; the Holy Spirit came upon the disciples in the day of Pentecost Acts 2&#58;1-4; Holy Spirit left King Saul and evil spirit came upon him 1 Samuel 16&#58;14). Again, what kind of spirit is living in you? It is good to find out in preparation for your eternity.
✓ When we are living according to God's commandments, His Holy Spirit that dwells within us, bears witness before God that our spirit is acting right, that we are His children (Romans 8&#58;14,16). 
✓ The spirit of the devil takes people to destruction, makes them do things that destroy them (idolatry, immorality, fearfulness, killing including abortion, unforgiving attitude). 

# The Bible teaches us that the absence of the Spirit of Christ in a man means he is not of Christ (Romans 8&#58;9). The presence of spirits that are not of Christ also means the man is not of Christ.  

Find out the other spirit(s) that are in you because that will determine where you will end up after death, in heaven or in hell. 
✓ A follower of Christ (church-goer) may have a spirit that is not from God without knowing and may be misbehaving or mis-talking under its influence. It has to be cast out through prayer if not he will not enter heaven. 
For example&#58; (1) Peter had a bad spirit &#123;Mathew 16&#58;24&#125;; (2) James and John, the sons of thunder also had a bad spirit &#123;Luke 9&#58;54-56&#125;). But, we know that Christ prayed for Peter & the other disciples and they were saved. 
✓ Every follower of Christ that has a bad, evil (demonic) spirit needs prayer because he/she cannot enter heaven with such a spirit. The spirits of fighting, revenge, bitterness, immorality, cheating (people or your spouse), greed, lack of mercy, etc. are all evil spirits and they are abominable before God.

How to know the nature of the spirit that is in us by looking at our lives
✓ Lets look at our way of life and compare with the Bible standard; find out if our behavior in the house, at the market, in the community is the way that real children of God should behave.  Please, find out. Do you still tell lies, cheat in business, spoil people's daughters or sons; steal people's husbands or wives from them, harbour evil thoughts against people? You cannot be a child of God, set for heaven if you are like that. Find out where you are headed and turn around, turn again and be delivered. 

Sold? unsell yourself from the demons of captivity. 
✓ People sell themselves to demons, powers that will destroy them because they get pittance from them. We just make friends without finding out if the person can add value to, or remove value from us. Do not be like King Jehoshaphat before Ahab and his son (review the CSY write up on Jehoshaphat. Consider dramatizing this with IBM/OGT). 
Ahab sold himself to Jezebel and unfortunately, Ahab could not unsell himself.
