{{text:GOD'S SUFFICIENCY IN TIMES OF WEAKNESS
Psalms 107&#58; 4-5, 6-7, 10-12, 13-14, 17-18, 29-20, 23-27, 28-30, 43,1.

Introduction&#58;
In our walk with God, there will come several times when we will be weak because of diverse trials and tribulations. These, come many times from the enemy, man's disobedience to God, and sometimes, from natural causes which happens to God's people as they happen to their brethren in the world. It is at such times that sinners should cry unto the Lord in repentance, and saints should sustain their faith in God's faithfulness for He will surely lead them to the Rock that is higher than them, even their Rock of refuge. God is acutely aware of His saints' insufficiency when diverse troubles assail them and has promised that those that put their trust in Him will never be put to shame. He will always be their sufficiency. God's REST will always come to the BEST after their TEST. 

Point-1&#58; Profound weakness from diverse TESTS

The many trials of David from Saul and the enemies that attacked him through their tongues (spoke ill of him). 2 Samuel 3&#58;39; 79&#58;1-4

The many trials of Job were engineered to crush him and make him curse God. 

The test of Nehemiah (to run and hide in the temple) was engineered to make him doubt God's protection
Ezra 4&#58;4; Nehemiah 6&#58;9


Point-2&#58; Undeniable strategy that is all-time BEST

Let the weak say I am strong (faith)

Those that put their trusts in the Lord will never be put to shame. God's REST for the BEST after the TEST. (Trust)

Though a host should encamp around me, God is my sufficiency (Trust)

David always comforted himself in the Lord, even when the Amalekites attacked and destroyed Ziklag (Submission)

Job said, though He slay me, yet will I trust Him (Trust). 

I esteem His word more than my necessary food (Submission)


Point-3&#58; God's sufficiency for the saint's REST 

For my power is made perfect in weakness

An angel strengthened Christ when He was weak

When we are weak then are we strong
