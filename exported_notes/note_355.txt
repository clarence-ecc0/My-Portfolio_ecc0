1 Corinthians 12&#58;25-31
The body of Christ, the church is one. We must be united and so be useful to one another.


Point-1&#58; Purposeful relationship of members as set by God

1 Corinthians 12&#58;12-21
a. Converted baptized members saved by grace
1 Corinthians 12&#58;13-14; Romans 6&#58;3-5; Titus 2&#58;11-14


b. Crucified buried members selfless and godly 
1 Corinthians 12&#58;15-18; Romans 6&#58;6-12; Phil 2&#58;3-5; 1Timothy 6&#58;6,11
No member of the body is more important than the other but all of them work harmoniously for the purpose of the whole body. The members of the body collaborate and praise God and are not comparing one with another. 
Our old man (character) is crucified with Christ. In our struggles in life, our focus is not only on our own success but on the success of our brethren.

c. Consecrated beneficial members set for good

The members of the body will say, "the Lord has set me here for good".

1 Corinthians 12&#58;22-27
The members of the body that are feeble are delicate. 

a. The compassion of members towards the feeble

We will strengthen members that are feeble. 
The things we say about a feeble sister or brother, if he gets to hear, will it strengthen him? Comfort the feeble minded and not to condemn them., be patient with all men and do not condemn them.  

b. The care of members within the flock
1 Corinthians 12&#58;24-25; 2 Corinthians 8&#58;16
Some people may have lack and some members may want to look down on them. God is not like that. He cares more for those members that have not.
Members that have lack, we do not need to wait for the overseer or RO before we package our gifts and give those that have lack


c. The character of members as followers
1 Corinthians 12&#58;6; Romans 12&#58;15; Galatians 6&#58;2,9-10
If you are the only one that God has given the grace to help, do not complain about those that are not able to help for the moments. 
1 Timothy 5&#58;21


Point-3&#58; Prescribed responsibilities of ministers as sealed by God

1 Corinthians 12&#58;28-31

a. The appointed ministry of apostle and other ministers
Ephesians 4&#58;11-14; Jeremiah 23&#58;4; 3&#58;15; Acts 20&#58;28
The gifts God gave us for us to use to edify others- teach, train and mature the people of God. As we are ministered to and we become matured, we will no longer be like children that can easily be manipulated. All our lack of&#58; knowledge and things are supplied as ministers minister to our needs. 

b. The assigned ministries of ambassadors and all members
1 Corinthians 12&#58;29-30; 2 Corinthians 5&#58;18-20; Acts 8&#58;4; 
I am an ambassador of Christ. As all of us are Christ's ambassadors, and anywhere we are, we preach His word as His ambassadors.

1 Corinthians 12&#58;31; Philippians 2&#58;3-7
I covert the best gift that makes me an excellent ambassador.  To be Christ's excellent ambassador, that is an acceptable motive before God. Lord, help me to have an excellent Spirit to serve you better.
