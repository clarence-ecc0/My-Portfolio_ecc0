TOPIC&#58; THE EXPERIENCE OF PURITY AND PEACEFULNESS IN FULL SALVATION

Mathew 5&#58;8-9
In salvation, there is, forgiveness, freedom, and fire (that burns out our impurities). 

The family of Reuben, Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh had impure heart. They desired what they lusted after without finding out what God may have for them in the remaining land to be conquered. 

Lot saw a flourishing Sodom and lusted after it. He had impure heart. Solomon wasn't pure in heart. In any direction he turned, he could only see women, acquiring 1000 of them. 

The actions of Achan wasn't from a pure heart. To cause the death of his brethren because of his lust for material things was out of an impure heart.

The disciples arguing amongst themselves over who would be the greatest, just after Christ had informed them of His forthcoming death was an act out of an impure heart.

a. The peaceful heart in His peace

Romans 5&#58;1; Isaiah 26&#58;3; Phillipians 4&#58;7-9
When we come to know and accept the Lord, we are justified (just as if we have never sinned) and we have peace. That is the litmus that we know Christ because the He, the Prince of peace lives within us. The peace of God that passeth understanding will continue to increase in us. The 'peace of God' came to us from the 'God of peace'
In  between Phillipians verse 7 we have the 'peace of God' and verse 9 where we see the 'God of peace' is verse 8 that tells us the lifestyle that makes us enjoy both verses 7 & 9.

Romans 16&#58;20

b.The purged heart by His propitiation 

The GS spoke of an acquaintance he heard when he was in C&S church who hated him when he left the church because he received salvation. The person rejected him but he persevered and one day, invited him to service. He accepted and became born again in himself. We learn we should harbor anger, frown, animosity, etc cannot win souls unto Christ. These cannot prepare me for My good work

2 Timothy 2&#58;21; Psalms 51&#58;7


c. The pure heart at His price

Psalms 24&#58;3-4.
He paid for our pure heart. He died to achieve this. 
David the king, with all he had (kingdom, fame, wealth, power), he wanted to know who would be qualified to see God. Yet, David would mess up Bathsheba and wrote a letter that would kill Uriah. What was David's spiritual state when he did all these, when he used his position to do evil. Thank God he prayed and was delivered. We must have time for prayer (after all our over busy schedules) to ask for forgiveness for our corruptions. 




Point-2&#58; The promise and provisions of pure heart, our privileges 

Mathew 5&#58;8
Christ did not pronounce blessings on everyone but those that merited it- those with pure heart. Our habit of saying, "God bless you" to everyone, including those openly living in disobedience to God is not good. 

a. The persons with pure heart in the past 
Some people had pure hearts before we came into the kingdom
Enoch had pure heart, so did Hezekiah. Daniel was of that group as well. Apostle Paul was also like that.
He always has people around him- Barnabas, Silas, Timothy, etc. We should avoid separating ourselves from brethren so that we do not fall into temptations that may overwhelm us.

b. The promise of pure heart for His people

Ezekiel 36&#58;25-26
If someone keeps falling into old sin, he doesn't have the new heart yet.

c. The provision ofa pure heart at present 

Acts 15&#58;9; 2 Peter 1&#58;3-4
Christ has put no difference between the apostles and prophet and evangelists of that time and of our time, the believers of that time and our time. Many believers today do not have this provision of a pure heart and Christ puts a difference between such believers and the believers of old.

The Lord has made me to be a partaker of the divine nature because He has helped me to escape the corruption that is in the world through lust.
