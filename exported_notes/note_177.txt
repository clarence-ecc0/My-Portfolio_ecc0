Point-1&#58; 

a. The impartiality of Christ in His teachings 
John 3&#58;1; Mathew 5&#58;1, 20; 18&#58;3-; Luke 13&#58;3,5, 22-24
Christ instructions that all must be born again to see God's kingdom shows His impartiality. At this time (in chapter 18), the disciples have already succeeded in casting out devil's and yet Christ told them unless they are born again, they will not enter heaven. 

b. The invariability of Christ as a Teacher 
John 3&#58;2, 34; Mathew 22&#58;16
When you care for what people will say of you or do to you, you are likely to become variable. Christ cared for man and  was thus invariable. We will meet tough people who will challenge or threaten us. We must be firm and remain invariably like Christ.

c. The inflexibility of Christ even in tough times.
Even as Children of God, we are not going to go to heaven on a bed of roses. 

John 8&#58;28-30
May we all have the grace to still speak the truth in front of those who have the power to crucify us, the very thing they hate to hear. 

Hebrews 13&#58;8-9
What a beautiful thing if people will accept Christ because they see our inflexibility on all issues that relate to godliness and truly believe that we are genuine. 

Point-2&#58; The royal law of love for the royal priesthood 

James 2&#58;8; 1 Peter 2&#58;9
The royal law of love has not been abolished but shall remain eternally. 

a. Practicing the royal law without discrimination

Mathew 25&#58;34-40
Treat the people you come across in your daily life as you would have treated Christ. If you do this, Christ in reality will say that your service was to Him. We shouldn't be so busy, fearful or untrusting that we cannot offer assistance to those that truly need it. 


b. Performing the royal law without defilement 

James 2&#58;10-11; Romans 13&#58;8-9,10

c. Proclaiming the royal law without deviation 

James 2&#58;8, 12a; Mathew 22&#58; 37-40; Mark 12&#58;29-
The first sin and the great sin is, not loving the Lord your God with all your soul, with all your might.
