TOPIC&#58; THE SUPERNATURAL VICTORY OF GOD'S COMMISSIONED SERVANT

Joshua 1&#58;1-4, 5-
We are not to fear any persecutor, God is nearer than any persecutor. We should therefore be courageous. The Lord repeated "be thou strong and courageous" thrice within 9 verses showing that the things that could take away Joshua's courage are there but he must not allow them to take away his courage. 

We should learn to turn what we read into prayers, praying God's words to Him

Pt-1&#58; The Collective Consideration Of Their Great Commission

Hebrews 13&#58;5; Psalm 2&#58;8
These scriptures have been fulfilled and are still being fulfilled as we go out with strength and courage. There is no need to fear when we have such passionate assurance from the Lord Himself.


Pt-2&#58; The Contagious Courage Of A Godly Conqueror

Joshua 1&#58;6; 10&#58;12-14,24
The Lord says the ONLY thing I need to be an overcomer is to be strong and of good courage. We must observe not to be diverted by anything. Surely, Satan will attempt to bring his diversions but we must observe to "turn not from it (the Lord's path) to the right hand or to the left hand". We must avoid distractions by all means and at all times. 

Because we are in the Lord's business, we will do impossible things and all will see that the Lord is with us and approves of our assignment in Ofante.
