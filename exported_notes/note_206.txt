*Theme&#58;* _Our preservation through the storms of life_


_"God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble._
_Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;_
_Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Selah."_
*Psalms 46&#58;1-3*

God does not often keep storms away from us but preserves us while they are raging. Storms do not mar but make saints of God;

Many believers do not enjoy His supernatural interventions in their travails *_because they allow their fears to overwhelm them and blind their eyes to God's protective power over them;_*

Are you going through any storms or difficult situations now? *_Look up and see God in your storms. Sing, smile and praise Him in your storms, and you will see His glory._* He is your refuge and a very present help in troubles.
