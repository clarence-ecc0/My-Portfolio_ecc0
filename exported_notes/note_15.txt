            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _The army of two._

They come as an army of two, the *Seen* and the *Unseen one,*  to the habitation of the righteous, to do him double harms, temporal and eternal.

✝️ The *Seen one&#58;* he carries his killing weapons, empowered by the *Unseen one,* his master, he has his eyes on the physical booties of the righteous- his land, life, money, and other properties to possess; 

✝️ The *Unseen one&#58;* possessor of the soul of the *seen one,* in his continuous war with his Maker, has never lost his interest in the righteous&#58; his precious soul, so elusive to him for so long, he convinces himself, finally, he must possess this too, through mind game, to add to that of the *seen one* and of many and all to perish together with him in hell, his eternal habitation by the decree of his Maker. 

✝️ So they come as an army of two, the *unseen one* hiding his true identity behind his servant, the *seen one,* to trouble the peace of the righteous, and to  take away his life and properties as he once tried, with 'righteous' Job. 

✝️ With his well-advertised imagery of fear, he entices the righteous ever so diligently, to rebel against the law of his God by hating his tormentor and by accepting the weapon of retaliation he offers against his own servant, the tormentor, that he too, the righteous may become his, through disobedience to the commandment of his God that tells him, _"vengeance is mine, I will repay",_

👉🏾 Oh, how cleverly evil is the *unseen one,* that he uses the war he engineered to make both the tormentor and the tormented become his. 



👉🏾 May the righteous remember, even in his afflicted state that he is in a spiritual warfare,  that his God does not permit him to use the carnal, hateful weapons of hatred and retaliation against the *seen one.* 



👉🏾 May he, in his fears and pains remember that the war of the righteous is actually the war of his God, as he recollects the faithful promise on his head that assuredly tells him&#58; 
 _"..... I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine. When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee&#58; when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned; neither shall the flame kindle upon thee."_.   *Isaiah 43&#58;1-2*

📌 Let the righteous, still in his trepidation remember, that the righteous Job, once the target of the *Unseen one,* had an end that was more glorious than his beginning. 

📌 Let him also determine, that his end too shall be like that of righteous Job, not like that of Balaam, that *'Lost Prophet'* whose desire for a righteous end was not attained, because he yielded to the *unseen one's* overtures, that turned his eyes from eternal glory to perishable treasures of gold.

📌 Let the righteous continue to say, _"the Lord is my Shepherd, I shall not want",_ for he will not only see the King in His glory, but his life also, even in this temporal habitation shall be blessed. 

Peace, peace, unto the righteous against the army of two.]text_span:[]}}
