            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _When men attempt to tempt God_

Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God&#58; *for God cannot be tempted with evil,* neither tempteth he any man&#58;      *James 1&#58;13*

The Bible reference above tells us that God cannot be tempted with evil. Men sometimes try to do this, to launder their fruits of unrighteousness by sponsoring noble deeds that God approves of.

✍️ Our deeds and the means of accomplishing them matter to God. Our attempt at bribing God with thanksgiving offerings for success in ventures that He expressly detests or detests the means of their accomplishment may earn the applause of men here on earth but in heaven, it cannot generate any wave of approval.

*Kindly reflect on the scenarios below and share your opinions*

✍️ A young lady wins big in the idolatrous, God-detesting *Big Brother....* show and goes to a church to give thanksgiving offerings to God;

✍️ A young man brings the fruits of a corrupted business practice to an orphanage to help the needy or he takes it to a church to sponsor/support gospel work;

✍️ A young lady living under occupation by an army of invaders offers herself as a sacrifice to the unit commander in order to attract daily necessities to keep her parents and siblings alive; 

✍️ A young female undergraduate goes into unofficial prostitution on campus in order to raise money to support the aged parents or the siblings' education.

✍️ An evangelist goes to a community of polygamous men and, because he needed to establish a church there, he vowed that he will never preach against polygamous practices. 

These scenarios (thanksgiving offerings, helping the needy, supporting the family, evangelism) have one thing in common&#58; they are all examples of noble deeds aimed at gaining God's approval. Unfortunately, they are also accomplished through means that are detested by God. These are examples of how men often attempt to tempt God with evil. God cannot be moved by these deeds. 

✍️ We cannot fight God's battles with His enemy's weapons and hope to gain His approval. Any inspiration that guides us along such a path is devilish, sensual, from beneath, and not from above. 

We have His warning in the Scriptures on this&#58;
_And if a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, *except he strive lawfully._*  *2 Timothy 2&#58;5*

May the Lord give us discerning wisdom both to understand the ways of the *Wicked one*, and to overcome them in this end time in Jesus name.

*Shalom*
