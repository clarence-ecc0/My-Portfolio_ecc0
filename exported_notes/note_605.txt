Many pastors have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden my portion under foot, they have made my pleasant portion a desolate wilderness.


Psalms 11&#58;3
If the foundations be destroyed, what can the righteous do?

There are many preachers that only talk about prosperity, health, etc but do not talk about repentance from sin. Such preachers have been destroying the foundation of salvation.
If the foundation has been destroyed, what can the righteous do? The righteous need to rebuild the foundation. The foundation of salvation is Jesus Christ and He is laid in people through repentance and avoidance of sins. This we must do. 

Rebuild the foundation&#58;
✓ of your life if it has been destroyed&#58; repent and believe (obey) the gospel again.

✓ of your congregation if it has been destroyed&#58; teach them to repent and believe (obey) the gospel again.

✓ of your close associates if it has been destroyed&#58; guide them to repent and believe (obey) the gospel again.

✓ of your family if it has been destroyed&#58; lead them to repentance and to believe (obey) the gospel again.

Remember prophet Elijah as you read Isaiah 58&#58;12
 
Isaiah 58&#58;12 says&#58;
✓And they that shall be of thee shall build the old waste places&#58; 
✓ thou shalt raise up the foundations of many generations; 
✓ and thou shalt be called, The repairer of the breach, 
✓ The restorer of paths to dwell in.
