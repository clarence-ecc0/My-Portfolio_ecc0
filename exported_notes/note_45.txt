a. The regrettable statement on the temptation of subjects
Job 31&#58;33-
God does not tempt anyone. It is a regrettable statement when men say God tempted them. God did not tempt Abraham, He tried his faith.

b. The revealed source of temptation to sin
Luke 4&#58;2-3, 13; 1 Chronicles 21&#58;1; 1 Corinthians 7&#58;5
It is the devil that tempt people. 
When there is a tendency to tell a lie, Satan is at the root of it. He was the one that misled Ananias and Saphira to their untimely death. 
Defraud ye not one another. 

c. The root and the route from temptation to sin
James 1&#58;14-15; Joshua 7&#58;21; Hebrews 3&#58;12-14
The thing that you see accidentally is not the sin but when you appreciate it, gaze at it, it causes passion (lust) in you and you desire it, temptation has gotten hold of you. It has shifted from being an accident to a real sin.

Point -2&#58;  The ruinousness of succumbing voluntarily to sin
James 1&#58;14- Ezekiel 18&#58;30-32
Getting to heaven is not child's play. When a child of Satan is coming to you (your boss, friend, etc.) and you know his/her presence defiles your heart, you have to, with all your might close your door against him/her.

a. Spiritual death of all living succumbing to temptation
Psalm 7&#58;11-14; Isaiah 59&#58;2-8; 1 Timothy 5&#58;6

b Sad death of the lifestyle of succumbing to temptation
Hebrews 9&#58;27

c. Second death of a lifetime of yielding to temptation
James 1&#58;15-16; 2 Chronicles; Revelation 21&#58;8, 27

If you cannot say no to temptation, there will be a spiritual, sad death.

Point-3&#58; Resources for the saint's victory over temptation

a. God, our protector and preserver from temptation 
Genesis 20&#58;3, 6-7
If you commit sin because someone lied to you (like Abimelech was deceived by Sarah/Abraham), you will still perish, the fact that you were deceived notwithstanding. You are free if however, you repent and restitute upon knowing the truth. 

b. Grace, our portion and power over temptation
2 Corinthians 12&#58;9-10; Hebrews 12&#58;14-16
God's grace is always sufficient for us, to keep us from temptation if we accept the grace. Note&#58; the evidence of our accepting grace is that we deliberately deny/abandon ungodliness, worldly lust and decide to put on righteousness, Godliness and sobriety in this present world. 

c. Godliness through prayer and perseverance against temptation
Mathew 15
