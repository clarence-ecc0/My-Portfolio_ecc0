Point-2&#58;
James 2&#58;8; 1 Peter 2&#58;9
The royal law of love and all the other moral laws have not been abolished. It is what we have in the heart that manifest in our lives. We must strive to maintain the royal law in our hearts.
When you think right, you will act right. Think about others first, then you will be living by the royal law.

a. Practicing the royal law without discrimination 

James 2&#58;8; Mathew 25&#58;34-40; 41-45
Live by the royal law, in your place of work, your relationship with your directors, others, etc should typify royal law. Imagine that your neighbor is Christ and you met Him very hungry. Will you give Him your food? The royal law requires that you help him with your food to quench his hunger. 
May the Lord open our eyes to see Christ's reward on the final day. If we think that just preaching from the pulpit without helping the needy is all that is required, we may be shocked by the rewards that we will receive. 
Our salvation must bear fruits of kindness 



b. Performing the royal law without defilement 

James 2&#58;10-11; Romans 13&#58;8-
In trying to fulfill the royal law of love, if we do it to satisfy our lust for the person, we have offended in one of the laws and we are transgressors of all the laws. 

c. Proclaiming the royal law without deviation 
When we proclaim the royal law to others and not do it first to ourselves, we are unwise. 
Mathew 22&#58;37-40; Mark 12&#58;29-31
All the laws in the old testament and all the prophets have written are all focused on two things&#58; Love the Lord, and love your neighbor. 

Point-3&#58; The renewed law of liberty for all regenerated people

What you preach, teach, let it be what you will be found doing. Hold sound doctrine with tenderness. 

a. The mirror of the perfect law of liberty 
James 1&#58;23-25
The word of God is a mirror that shows us our spiritual face, how we look (our appearance) before God. If after the message, we fail to pray over our image the Lord showed us in the mirror, we are like people that look at the mirror without doing anything about our defamed image God showed unto us. 
Key counsel here is, after every message, pray greatly on whatever that is revealed unto you about your appearance before God.

b. The ministry of the probing law of the Lord
