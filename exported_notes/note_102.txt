Point-1&#58; The promise from the Almighty
1 Kings 18&#58;1; Numbers 23&#58;19; Psalms 105&#58;42, 42-45
2 Peter 1&#58;3; Romans 4&#58;18-22

Every other word we have heard in our lives, the word of God we heard supercedes all of them. 
If God fulfilled His promise to Abraham at 100years of age, He can do it for me at 50 years. 

Point-2&#58; The preparation for abundance
1 Kings 18&#58; 2-9, 10-24, 30-39

As Elijah was going to Ahab as God commanded him, there was no doubt in his mind that the promised rain would come. Just like Obadiah who was promoting God's work in the face of persecution against God's people, even so I will act by God's grace. 

The boldness and spirit of Elijah will come upon me in Jesus name. As the enemy Ahab could not see Elijah, even so will my enemies not see me in Jesus name. They could not see Elijah in the open at the brook nor at the widow's house in the open as well!

When you are standing on the Lord's side, there will be no trembling or fear. 

King Ahab ran errand for Elijah at the word of authority from his mouth. Similar words of authority will proceed from my mouth always in Jesus name.

Against 450 prophets of Baal, Elijah alone with God is the majority. So it is for me as well in Jesus name.

1 Kings 18&#58;41-45; James 1&#58;17
