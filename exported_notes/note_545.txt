{{text:STS&#58; Jesus, the good Shepherd 
The Scriptures used physical things to explain spiritual matters. Christ said, "I am THE good Shepherd", not I am a shepherd. He also said, "I am THE Door", not I am a door. He was definitive, He is the only good Shepherd, the only Door.

The characteristics of the thieves and robbers include their interest in economic exploitation of the sheep, to Lord over them and to pollute them. The characteristics of the good Shepherd include, sincerity, caring to the point of dying for the sheep.

The core responsibility of a believer that can ensure him security (admissible to heaven) is to constantly ABIDE in Christ.

TOPIC&#58; THE SHEPHERD'S PERFECT LOVE FOR THE SHEEP
John 10&#58;11-18; 15&#58;13-15; 13&#58;1, 34, 35; 21&#58;15-17
Christ went through great agony to save us. Therefore, whatever we have to pass through, because of Christ, we have to endure and pass through it and save a soul. 
There is a level of faith you will reach that even the sole amount you have (with nothing else) you can give out because you know that the Lord will raise whatever you need at the time of need. 

When you reach the level of faith of our GS, you have assurance that as you pray for people, God will answer your prayers for them. 

Live your life like someone that will be admitted to heaven. That is the first and greatest peace. If you have this peace, all other levels of peace will come afterwards because nothing else can trouble you again. 

Point-1&#58; The perfect love of our Savior 
1 John 4&#58;9-10, 18-21
Our love for God is perfected when we love the brethren. It is our love for the brethren that shows how we live the Lord.

Romans 5&#58;6-8; 1 Timothy 1&#58; 12-15; Titus 2&#58;11-14; Ephesians 5&#58;25-27; Deuteronomy 30&#58;6
When grace comes into our lives, it does not leave us where it found us. He takes us to a higher height. The scriptures says that Christ redeemed us from all iniquity. It did not redeem us to remain in the iniquity.

We do not calculate what we gain or loose when we give unto God. Esther said, "If I perish, I perish"; It's the same with Shadrach and his friends; Ruth as she left Moab, Rahab as she bargained for her future and her brethren, Daniel as he went into the Lion's den. This is Sanctification.

Point-2&#58; The pure love among the saints 

When you love, you will not do evil to your neighbor.

1 Peter 1&#58;22-23; Romans 13&#58;9-14;12&#58;9-21; Ephesians 5&#58;2,25,28,33; 1 Peter 3&#58;8-9; 1 Corinthians 13&#58;1-8a
When we are constrained (have our own lack) and yet give to those in need, we are showing pure love. We saw this often in our parents who, though they were going through difficulties and were complaining of the famine yet, gave us our three square meals, and we ate not realizing that they may not have eaten anything themselves. 
We are called to bless whenever we are cursed, not to render railing (shout back) for railing (shouted at), curse for curse.


Point-3&#58; Our persevering love for the Shepherd 

2 Corinthians 5&#58;14-15; Mathew 10&#58;37; 24&#58;12-13; Revelations 2&#58;4-5; Psalms 119&#58;47-48, 97, 113, 127-128, 159-160, 163, 165, 167.

Because Christ is the one that gave us our wife, children or parents, it will be unreasonable if we decide to love the gift we received more than He that gave us the gift. It is worthwhile to always check our lives and see if we now love our gifts more than the Giver.
