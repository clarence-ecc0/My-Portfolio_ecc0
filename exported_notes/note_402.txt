# The Isaiah 28&#58;15,16 leaders, who persist in evil.
✓ God has a message, even a warning to those that are in leadership authority (particularly in the church) and continue in evil, who have succeeded in convincing (rather, confusing) themselves that even though, they disrespect and misrepresent God, no evil will come upon them (Romans 2&#58;3). 
✓ Their confidence (God calls it "refuge/shelter of lies") will not be able to cover them when the disaster comes. Their confidence is likened to a bed shorter than the man lying on it, and a covering narrower than the man it is supposed to cover. Read Isaiah 28&#58;14-15,18-20.

[B&#93; The wickedness of unrighteous leaders
✓ Young people are rejecting God (rebelling against His authority) because of backsliding church leaders. They are seeking power from idols and abandoning God, the source of all power; they are patronizing Satan. All these because of how some modern pastors have presented God's church as a merchandising vocation. 
✓ The name of God was blasphemed all over the world because of the Jews (Romans 2&#58;24,23). Today, the exact thing is happening because of what Christians (particularly the leadership) are doing today. They have become obstacles to the salvation of many. May nobody's blood be required at our hands in Jesus name. 

✓ Many church leaders have become Hosea 10&#58;1 leaders. They are empty vines that bring fruits unto themselves. They steal the honor that should be given to God and the converts have become theirs, not God's. When some leave a church, they carry 'THEIR"  converts.

✓ A son honoreth his father and a servant his master. In Malachi 1&#58;6, God was asking the priests (pastors), if He is our Father and Master, where is His honor and fear? We should ask ourselves this very pertinent question. God expects us to honor Him and His Son, Jesus Christ. (John 5&#58;22-23).
Take note that the question here was directed to priests (pastors). Are you a pastor that does not show honor or fear before the Lord? 

[C&#93; The sinfulness of smooth preaching in the face of grievous errors
✓ It is a great sin before God for the watchman to be preaching smooth/sweet/ deceptive message of peace and prosperity of the body when the soul is in mortal danger of everlasting burning. Jeremiah fought unsuccessfully against this to prevent Nebuchadnezzar from taking over Jerusalem but the false, smooth prophets of Jerusalem withstood him, preaching "Peace, Peace, when there was no peace". (Jeremiah 5&#58;31; 8&#58;11). In the end, Jerusalem was taken. The false prophets caused the heritage of Abraham to be taken over. Isaiah said that if the Lord hadn't spared a small remnant for Jacob, their story would have been like that of Sodom & Gomorrah where everyone was destroyed (Isaiah 1&#58;9)

A difficult time is coming and people need to be prepared for it. It is in the time of peace that preparation for war is made, and the war of the anti-Christ is coming upon the whole world. Who can withstand it without the gospel's preparation? 

"If thou hast run with the footmen, and they have wearied thee, then how canst thou contend with horses? and if in the land of peace, wherein thou trustedst, they wearied thee, then how wilt thou do in the swelling of Jordan?" (Jeremiah 12&#58;5)

We may be complaining about the brutality of the security men today (that is not strange if we remember John the Baptist's counsel to soldiers Luke 3&#58;14) but remember, amongst them are some that still fear God, who are moderating the harshness. We all know what we went through when we had money in the bank and could not get it. In the government of the anti-Christ, all those that have the fear of God would have gone. Mercy and grace will be taken away from the earth. If you cannot be a good Christian now that there is mercy and grace, I wonder how you can manage to be a good one then.
