The lessons of dreams and death. 
✓ Our daily sleep experiences in which we travel to dreamland and wake up are actually moments of temporary deaths while our final death on earth when we go to the unchangeable dreamland will be our moment of permanent sleep. Death and sleep are the same (John 11&#58;11-14); the only difference is in duration and the presence of absence of breathing (God's spirit in us). 
✓ God has given us sleep and dreams to forewarn us of eternity. There shall be sorrow unending (as in a bad dream) and joy unending (as in a good dream). Learn from your dreams what is ahead for you. Only the flesh can die; the spirit can never die. 

Need for caution in interpreting dreams and visions, the so-called, "The Lord spoke to me"
✓ A study of the book of Job shows us three friends of Job that one can conclude were righteous judging from their knowledge of God and all they said to Job.  They said all the correct things that were correct about God but the condition under which they said them and the person (Job) to whom they said them to were all wrong.

✓ How did it begin? In Job chapter 4&#58;12-21, Eliphaz who appeared to be the leader of the three friends had a vision in which a spirit told him in summary that people we think are righteous may not be righteous after all. From Job chapter 4 up to Job chapter 31&#58;40 When Job said, "The words of Job are ended", when he stopped all arguments with his three ignorant friends, they were saying many wrong things against Job and these caused Job to charge God foolishly, the exact design and desire of Satan. 

✓ When God eventually intervened, He did not praise Eliphaz and his two friends for all the glorious things they said about God. Rather, He rebuked them and said that they spoke foolish things about Him. Now when we recall that Eliphaz, the ringleader got his inspiration from the vision of the spirit in Job 4&#58;12-21, we can conclude that that vision was not from the spirit of God but that of Satan, the enemy of Job. 
✓ We need to be careful how we interpret dreams, visions, and the usual "God spoke to me". Compare what you heard/saw with the word of God and the life you are leaving as well as the lust that may be in your heart (Ezekiel 14&#58;4). This doesn't mean God does not give visions and dreams to His people (Jacob in Genesis 28&#58;12; Joseph in Genesis 37;5,9; Joseph the husband of Mary in Mathew 2&#58;19). Our carefulness is because Satan also gives counterfeit dreams and visions and they appear to people as if they are from God. The Bible tells us in 2 Corinthians 11&#58;14 ".... for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light."
