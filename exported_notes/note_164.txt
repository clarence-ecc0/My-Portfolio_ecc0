Sometimes when something negative happens in your life, you are dejected but at such instances, something profound like seeing the Lord as Isaiah did may happen to you. Darkness or no darkness in your life, He is still holy and faithful. He has not left it and He is ready to attend to your prayers. 

Jesus Christ the same yesterday, today and forever. The same power that touched Isaiah and he was transformed will touch us and accomplish the same transformation.

We need power to open our mouths and preach to people, to overcome the urge to commit sins.
