TOPIC&#58; WORKING EARNESTLY FOR EVERYONE'S ESCAPE FROM SODOM

Genesis 19&#58;15-17; Revelations 11&#58;8
Jerusalem (where Christ was crucified) spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt. Every city of the world where you find great sin, iniquity, spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt. 

Point-1&#58; Pleading with list sinners in Sodom for their emancipation 

Genesis 19&#58;12
Lot had the privilege to bring out of Sodom whatsoever he had in the city (servants, herdsmen, cattle, etc.). It was a great opportunity but he only left with his wife and daughters. The rest were destroyed. I have the privilege also to bring my people out of Sodom (our city of sin is spiritually called Sodom). 
Angels were brought for a ministry of destruction Lot was given the ministry of saving people, unfortunately he limited himself to his son's in-laws and forgot all acquaintances, servants, etc. Verse 12 talks about the ministry handed over to Lot while verse 13 talks about the ministry of the angels. 

We must be persuasive as we preach to the people to escape from our city of sin. We must do better than Lot who could not persuade his in-laws and was like a mocker to them. 

A. His sons and daughters and servants in Sodom
Genesis 13&#58;5-7, 12-13, 
Lot left all his servants and servants in Sodom. 
As we look for greener pastures to pitch our tent, let's remember that Lot looked for greener pasture and landed in Sodom. He lost all in Sodom.

B. The sinfulness and the debauchery and superfluity of Sodom 
Ezekiel 16&#58;49-50; Romans 1&#58;24-25
Are you living a life such that God has given you over, people have given up on you, the Holy Ghost does not speak to you again? So not allow God to give you over to a reprobate mind to continue and increase in evil

C. The similarities, degeneration of society to Sodom
Isaiah 1&#58;4,10,18
The society we live in today is as degenerated as Sodom and has many similarities. We must reach out to them but we must first be different from them, turn away from everything that is sinful before God. We must be sanctified to be able to do this. 

Point-2&#58; Prevailing on lingering souls in Sodom to escape 

Genesis 19&#58;15
Lot was told to take hold of his wife and daughters (you know who they are, as worldly as their community; you must lay hold on them). 
The Lord has mercy but if we linger in sin, we can be consumed. The mercy of God was so great that the destruction that was to take place in the morning was delayed until the sun came up, just for Lot to escape into Zoar (verse 18-23). Infact, the city was spared because of Lot (verse 21)

A. Earnestly preaching to lingering souls to escape 
Hebrews 2&#58;1-3

B. Effectively persuading lukewarm saints to evangelize
We should be telling our brethren, if the Lord should come today, are you ready for Him? 

Numbers 32&#58;6,23; Revelations 2&#58;4-5
The promises of consecration we made to the Lord, we cannot neglect to do them. We promised we would preach to others (in our prayers, in the hymns we sang, several times over), how are we faring? 


C. Entreatingly prevailing on lax servants to engage 
Mathew 20&#58;6; Galatians 4&#58;15,18-19
There are servants that are lax. We must talk to them to engage with God's work once again, to evangelize once again. We are in December now and possibly in January, we promised that we would be soul winners. Where are the souls we have won now that we are at the 11th hour. 
People that had zeal at the beginning of the race now become lax as in the time of apostle Paul.




Point-3&#58; Purging out learned self-centeredness from Sodom and her excuses

Genesis 19&#58;18-19
The angels have warned Lot to run for his life and he is saying, "oh, not so my Lord". Lot did not learn any lesson from his rash decision to separate from Abraham (he was taken captive and Abraham came to his help; Sodom is about to be destroyed and Lot has been shown great mercy and is about to lose his life and yet, he is arguing saying, "no so, my Lord").
The Lord said, Remember Lot's wife Luke 17&#58;32
