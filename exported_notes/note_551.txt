{{text:THE TEMPLE OF GOD AND THE REMNANT AFTER THE FIRE THAT WILL TRY IT

1 Corinthians 3&#58;10-11,15
"According to the grace of God which is given unto me, as a wise masterbuilder, I have laid the foundation, and another buildeth thereon. But let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon.

For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

If any man's work shall be burned, he shall suffer loss&#58; but he himself shall be saved; yet so as by fire."


✓ The temple of God has a foundation&#58; Christ

✓ Others like apostle Paul Peter, etc have laid the foundation by preaching and living the life of Christ.
# The life of Christ was of suffering and left it for us to follow His footsteps"

1 Peter 2&#58;21
"For even hereunto were ye called&#58; because Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that ye should follow his steps"

✓ All preachers are required to build on the foundation (Christ) of the temple laid by the apostles.

# The Scriptures tells us that Christ, the foundation is a LIVING STONE, and His disciples being prepared are expected to be lively stones. This is so because it is only lively stones that can be laid upon the Living Stone foundation of the temple.

✓ Herein is the challenge of modern preachers as they attempt to build upon the FOUNDATION laid by the apostles. 

✓ Herein is the fiery warning

1 Corinthians 3&#58;12-13
"Now if any man build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble;

Every man's work shall be made manifest&#58; for the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall try every man's work of what sort it is."


✓ If a preacher uses precious materials (undefiled/undiluted word of God) to prepare quality stones (likened to gold, silver, precious stones) for the temple, these prepared stones will go through the fire (of affliction) and will survive the it. They will still be found abiding as lively stones in God's temple.

✓ On the other hand, if a preacher uses fanciful materials (smooth preaching, diluted word of God that only promises blessings and prosperity and good health and says nothing strong against sinful living) to prepare the stones/congregation they will turn out to be wood, hay and stubbles. These cannot withstand the fire of affliction when it comes. There are many congregations that the members are just wood, hay and stubbles. 

# The temple of God (the church) will surely be tried with fire. None of these materials (congregation) can abide in the temple under affliction and it is not their fault. They were not prepared to withstand the fire. 

✓ What happens to the preacher that did not prepare his stones adequately for the trial that came? The Holy Spirit says, he himself can only be saved if he was able to withstand the fire of affliction.

1 Corinthians 3&#58;15
"If any man's work shall be burned, he shall suffer loss&#58; but he himself shall be saved; yet so as by fire."


✓ If a preacher himself can only be saved after he is confirmed by the same fire that tried his congratulation, why do such preachers set their members (and themselves) up for an inevitable failure? If you were not prepared for the fire, you will not be able to withstand the fire when it comes.
Smooth preaching cannot save anyone&#58; neither the preacher nor the congregation.

✓  My brother, my sister, are you a smooth preacher or a listener to a smooth preacher? The end of the journey definitely will not be smooth. Make a decision while you till can.

The Lord bless you as you do this in Jesus name.
