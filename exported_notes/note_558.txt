Christ returned to Bethany where the plot to kill Him was hatched. This teaches us not to abandon the mission field because none can kill us before our time.

Mary anointed Christ with precious ointment while He was still alive (John 12&#58;3). At death, Mary (along with others now)brought again spices to anoint the dead body of Christ (Mark 15&#58;47; Mark 16&#58;1). Her dedication was total. No wonder she was the first to see the resurrected Christ.
