TOPIC&#58; Overcoming life challenges through faith in God's word

Hebrews 11&#58;1; 1 John 5&#58;4; Joshua 1&#58;8
Written word of God, spoken word of God or the revealed word of God have power to overcome challenges if we have faith in them. It gives perpetual victory to those that repose faith in it. 

Imagine that actually you heard God speak to you, you heard Him audibly like Joshua, how much confidence will you put in his promise, "I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee". You will be highly enthusiastic as you get about the instruction from God. But His written word is as His spoken word. We ought to put great faith in God's written word that we read as if we heard Him audibly.


Point-1&#58; Dealing with life challenges by faith in the word.
 Mathew 4&#58;11; Acts 27&#58;22-26; 1 John 5&#58;4
Paul faced the challenge in a mighty storm but he reposed his faith in the assuring words of the angel who was sent to him. He was so convinced that while others saw death (14 days of unceasing buffeting by storm) he was relaxed and ate his food and encouraged others to do likewise. This is practical Faith. We ought to be like this.

Point-2&#58; The Davids who challenge life situations through faith in the word.
Psalms 23&#58;4; 1 Samuel 17&#58;38-
David has such great confidence that whatever God has said is undoubtedly what will happen. Even more, when he had not heard yet from God assuring word that he would protect him, he moved unto the challenge against Goliath, trusting that God will not forsake him seeing he was risking his life for his glory. Certain things are predictable - If I stand to fight God's fight, I have no doubt that He will be happy with me & will preserve me; He will fulfill the numbers of my days according to His promise. 

Point-3&#58; The distractions of satanic situations by faith in the word.
Mathew 8&#58;16; Ephesians 6&#58; 16; 2 Corinthians 10&#58;3-5; Mathew 4&#58;11
In all our warfare, our conquests will ONLY, always come through our faith in God's promises.
