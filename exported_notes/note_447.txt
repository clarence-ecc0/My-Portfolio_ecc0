TOPIC&#58; OUR UNWAVERING COMMITMENT TO FULFILLING GOD'S WILL 

Hebrews 10&#58;7,9; 1 Peter 1&#58;18-20; Ephesians 1&#58;4; Luke 2&#58;49
Even before the foundation of the world, it was decided by our Savior Christ and God the Father that Christ was going to be sacrificed for the salvation of the world. In the volume of the Prophets, Psalms, the laws, it was written that He was coming to do the will of God. It was written that He would be lifted up like the serpent; He would be sacrificed like the pascal lamb that freed Israel from bondage in Egypt. If there is anything we too are waiting for, it is to do the will of God. That is how we will be confirmed to the image of Christ 

Romans 8&#58;29; Philippians 2&#58;9; 1 Peter 2&#58;21; 1 John 2&#58;6; 3&#58;3; 4&#58;17; 1 Cor 2&#58;16
We are the "many brethren of Christ" and are to follow His steps (of doing the will of God). We can do this because we have the mind of Christ 


Point-1&#58; Steadfast consecration for God's perfect will

Psalms 40&#58;8; Hebrews 10&#58;14; Mathew 6&#58;9-10; Psalms 103&#58;20
God has written His laws in our hearts as Sanctified saints. We therefore have the desire to do His will. As God's will is done in heaven, we will also do His will on earth. Angels do not do God's will partially, selectively, occasionally, superficially, hypocritically, seasonally,  
They do the will of God with all their heart, swiftly, sincerely, wholeheartedly, promptly, urgently , loyally, fearlessly, freely (without strings attached). Christ pointed us to the angels, that we should emulate them in the manner they do God's will.

Acts 13&#58;22; Romans 12&#58;1-2; Psalms 143&#58;10; Philippians 2&#58;13
God said, David would fulfill all His will. That's what Christ wants to see in us also. Because He is my God, I want to do His will. I pray Lord, teach me how to do your will. 

Hebrews 13&#58;20-21
If we truly want to be above nominal Christians, God will make us perfect so that we can do His will. The old nature (old man) will struggle with doing the will of God. It is Uod that uive us the grace to do His will. If we find ourselves delaying and holding back in doing God's will, the old man is at work in us. It will not be so with me in Jesus name. The old man calculates what he gains by doing God's will, what the others will do as he does this one. Sanctification delivers us from the old man. Our will is swallowed up in the will of God

Colossians 3&#58;12; 2 Corinthians 8&#58;3
This is the prayer of every good preacher for their members. When we are genuinely saved and sanctified, we are will to submit to God's inspired leaders. 

Point-2&#58; Single-minded conformity to God's prevailing will

Acts 21&#58;10-12,13-14; 20&#58;22-24; 22&#58;14-15; Colossians 1&#58;9
Despite all that the brethren did, to dissuade Paul from going to Jerusalem, they could not but God's will should prevail. A true child of God will not permit the will of any man (Nebuchadnezzar, Pharaoh, etc.) to prevail against God's will because of threat to his life. A true child of God will pray, "Let the will of God prevail and let my will perish"

Ephesians 5&#58;17,25; 6&#58;5-6; 1 Thessalonians 4&#58;3,7; Hebrews 10&#58;36
After we have done the will of God, we must be patient to see the promise (what Gid will do for us). 

1 Peter 3&#58;16-18; 4&#58;1-2; 1 John 2&#58;15-17
Only those that do the will of God will abide forever and ever with the Lord.


Point-3&#58; Swift signal condemnation for God's permissive will

Numbers 22&#58;12, 15-19,20-34; 2 Peter 2&#58;15-; Jude 1&#58;11
When a man does not wish to do the will of God and ask for another will, whatever God permits the person to do is His permissive will and that is not good for you. Know one thing that, God doesn't change His mind. God's anger was kindled because Balaam accepted the permissive will of God. The permissive will of God will destroy the backslider. 
Those that follow the permissive will of God (like Balam) have forsaken the perfect will/way (right way) of God and their end is eternal damnation. 

Ezekiel 14&#58;6-10; Jeremiah 18&#58;12, Psalms 81&#58;11-12
There is punishment for someone who has abandoned the perfect will of God, and the prophet that is deceived (by God Himself) to encourage that man in his permissive will shall suffer the same degree of punishment. 

Psalms 106&#58;13-15; 2 Thessalonians 2&#58;9-12; Romans 1&#58;28,32. 
When men want their permissive will, they may get the satisfaction of their flesh but there will be leanness in their soul (no spiritual benefit). 
Those that have no love for the truth (God's perfect will) will enter into delusions and believe a lie so that they will perish.
