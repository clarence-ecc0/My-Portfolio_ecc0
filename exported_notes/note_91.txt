{{text:TOPIC&#58; JESUS&#58; THE GREAT HEALER AND HELPER, INTERCEDING FOR EVERY INDIVIDUAL 

Point-1&#58; 

a.The gift of God for all seekers of satisfaction
John 4&#58;10,14; Ephesians 2&#58;8; Romans 8&#58;32
Our salvation is not of the labor of our hands. It is a free gift we have when we express faith in the salvation that is available through Christ.

b. The Governor who suffered for all His subjects
Psalms 22&#58;1,16,18,28; Romans 6&#58;22
The Psalms was written over a thousand years before Christ came to fulfill it. He is the Governor among nations of the earth. 

c. He is the Giver of the Spirit for strength 
Acts 2&#58;32-33,38; Luke 11&#58;13; John 7&#58;37-39
Christ received the Holy Ghost from the Father as promised and shed (gave, distributed) it among the disciples (those that repent in the name of Jesus Christ). This could only happen after the glorification of Christ (His death).
Now Jesus Christ is glorified, and has received the Holy Ghost from the Father and distributed to all believers 

Point-2&#58; The high Priest, Helper of saints and seekers
Hebrews 2&#58;17-18; 4&#58; 14-16

a. The Healer of the sick with His stripes
Mathew 8&#58;16-17;Acts 10&#58;38; 1 Peter 2&#58;24
If sicknesses were of God and Christ went about healing them, then He must have been in disagreement with the Father. This will contradict His declaration, "I and my Father are one". Therefore, sickness is of the devil and it happens most times to those that have broken the hedge (those that are yet to be dead to sin, to temptations) God used to shield them. 


b. The Heir of all, for all sons and servants
Hebrews 1&#58;2; Romans 8&#58;14-17; Revelations 21&#58;7
Those that are led by the spirit of the world, of Satan, of bondage, are NOT the sons of God. A true minister of Christ cannot be afraid of his members or of the kingmakers (those that appoint him, minister). If you are afraid of what people will say, you are again under the influence of the Spirit of bondage again to fear. No one in such situation can do God's work perfectly.  We cannot look up to the people more than we look up to God. There should be no man, within or without the church that a child of God should fear more than God. There should be no one!


c. The Hope of all in submission to the Son 
Colossians 1&#58;27-28; 1 Peter 1&#58;3-4; 1 John 3&#58;1-3
If we have the hope to be like Christ (for we can't be with Him if we are not like Him) , we must purify ourselves. 

Point-3&#58; Jesus&#58; the interpreter of Scriptures to the sightless
There are people that read the Bible from Genesis to Revelations yearly and yet, they are unable to see any provision of the Scriptures for themselves&#58; they keep misinterpreting the scriptures; they are sightless. Christ is the one that opens our understanding so that we can understand the Scriptures. He gives us the real interpretations of the Scriptures.
Luke 24&#58;27,32, 44-45;

a. The intercessor for our steadfastness and security 
Hebrews 7&#58;25-26; Romans 8&#58;34-39; John 17&#58;17-21
We should always have confidence in our security in all we do in the name of Christ and everywhere we go to for Christ, because He is always making intercessions for our success.
Sometimes, we see some queer things in dreams and we are afraid that evil things may happen to us. The Holy Ghost said, of things present and of things to come (even from our dreams), nothing can overcome us, harm us, or separate us from the love of God (i.e., obedience to God). Continue in Christ in all circumstances. 


b. The image of splendor shining through the saints 
2 Corinthians 3&#58;18; Romans 8&#58;29
We will be changing from glory to glory, from an already high position to a higher one. 
We are not conformed to the image of any man but of Christ only. 


c. The invisible made visible to surrendered souls in supplication.
1 Timothy 1&#58;17; Hebrews 11&#58;27
Many times, because God is invisible and our strong opposers are visible to us, to move onward against the visible threats from our opposers, we must make the invisible God to become visible to us by faith. Moses did this when he faced Pharaoh. Standing before the visible threatening Pharaoh, he could see the God that spoke to him in the burning bush, he could hear the spoken word to him by God in the burning bush while standing in front of Pharaoh. By faith, make the invisible to become visible while standing in front of your strong opposer.
