{{text:COMMENDATION FOR DUTIFUL SERVICE AND CONDEMNATION FOR IDLENESS 
Mathew 25&#58;14-29
The message today is not targeted at people that do not know the Lord but those have known Him and yet were not submissive to His Lordship. It is about servants who bring service to their Lord as well as servants who would not serve Him.

Luke 12&#58;42-47
The servant who knew his Lord's will and doeth it shall be blessed but he wh knew it and dis it not shall be beaten with many stripes. 

We are saved (and trained) to serve. There will be commendations and blessings here on earth and in heaven for us if after our salvation and training (knowledge of God's will) we are found serving God in words and in deeds. But, there will be condemnations and punishment on earth and later, in eternity if after our salvation and teachings in the will, the word and the way of God, we abandon His service. The Lord's expectations of us is that we are zealous of good works (Titus 2&#58;14).

(1) SAVED FOR SERVICE TO CHRIST
We have the great commission from Christ that should engage our service dutifully as long as we remain on this earth (Mathew 28&#58;19-20)

Titus 2&#58;14; 3&#58;8
There is abundant work in the house of God. We were not saved to become benchwarmers. The owner of the vineyard expects fruits from His vineyard (Luke 20&#58;9-10). 
We are saved to bear fruits for Him. The branches of the vine that bringeth not fruit will be cut off by the Father and cast into the lake of fire (John 15&#58;1-2).



(2) SLOTHFUL SERVICE OF THE CONDEMNED 

 Haggai 1&#58;2-6,7.
The Lord is asking believers after they are saved, to consider their ways. After the people were delivered from the slavery of Nebuchadnezzar, the Lord expected services from them but they were only concerned about themselves.
He did not save us to live unto ourselves, taking care of our own needs only. Israel is an empty vine, he bringeth fruit unto himself (Hosea 10&#58;1).

There are many believers whom the Lord, after blessing them brought fruits unto themselves only. Before the blessings, they were fervent, serving the Lord but after the blessings, they became slothful in their service to the Lord; their resources (talent, time, finances) were consecrated to be used for themselves alone. Such was the case of Demas, an associate of apostle Paul (Colossians 4&#58;14; 2 Timothy 4&#58;10)
The Lord is asking us to consider our ways.


(3) SOUL-WINNING /SATISFYING SERVICE OF THE COMMENDED
Our work shall be commended and rewarded if we obey Christ. 
Haggai 1&#58; 12-13; 2&#58;19

At great peril to his life, even after the Lord had asked Paul to leave Jerusalem because the Jews would not accept his witnessing for Christ, he still preached the message to them. The Lord commended him for his dutifullness. Acts 23&#58;11; 22&#58;17-21
