2. The craftiness and deception of the Gibeonites 
Seeking the face of God in all matters is important. Joshua didn't seek the face of God when they gave him a report of the weakness of Ai, the same way he did not seek God's face before making peace treaty with the Gibeonites. Christians should be careful of the deceptive servants of Satan like the Gibeonites, who approach them with sly and come to contaminate them.
Brothers and sisters should be careful in entering unequal yoke in marriages with unbelievers who may through deception, win their hearts. 
Although the Gibeonites were discovered as fraud, nothing could be done to dissolve, divorce Israel covenant from its treaty. Many are in marriages that cannot be dissolved because they were misled in the beginning.

In summary, we need the Lord's guidance (always) in every decision (small and great) to escape the devices of the wicked one

TOPIC&#58; Eternal reward and recompense to the faithful and the unfaithful 

Luke 16&#58;10

Point -1&#58; The falsehood and the forfeiture of the judged
Luke 16&#58;1-2

A. The condemned accursed way of the unfaithful 
The way of the unfaithful is accursed before God. 
Luke 16&#58;1-7
The children of this world (the unjust) are wiser in worldly wisdom than the children  of light (the just) in their show of worldly wisdom.
The unjust man in the scriptures continued in his unjustness as a means to address his initial unjustness. His portion is the lake of fire. The means does not justify the end.
Joshua 7&#58;11-13

When you put an accursed thing among your genuine things, you make everything accursed. Your property cries out against you.
Habakkuk 2&#58;9-11; Zechariah 5&#58;2-3

B. The commended abominable wisdom of the unjust
The worldly master commended the unjust servant because that is the standard of the world, but God condemned the unjust servant because that is not Christ's standard. Whether the world approves your wittyness in covering up evil or not, God condemns it. 
Covering one evil with another is abominable before God. Dissimulation when we have done wrong only worsens the situation. 

James 3&#58;15
Earthly wisdom will only lead to hell fire. 

C. The constant, absolute word for the un-condemned.
Psalms 119&#58;1, 9,11; 1 Peter 1&#58;25
We cleanse our ways with the word of God, not the word of men. As the word of God endureth forever, so shall those that live by His word endure forever.

Point- 2&#58; The faith and faithfulness of the justified 
Luke 16&#58;9
Mammon of unrighteousness refers to money. 
Make yourself a friend of money by earning it in the right way. Use that rightly earned money for God's work. We can use the Mammon of unrighteousness to advance the education of indigent youth whom their parents are unable to train.


A. Faithfulness in the least to the least with transient riches 
Luke 16&#58;10
A house leader that is unfaithful in that least position will also be unfaithful in the position of a pastor, which is much.

B. Failure of the lost in the lust for tempting riches
1 Timothy 6&#58;9-10
The lust of a man can affect his entire household as in the case of Achan. Why steal the King's trumpet when you cannot blow it without being found out. Where will Achan use the goodly Babylonish garment that he stole? Why steal what you can't use? When Satan controls a man's heart through lust, even the defiling things he acquired will not be useful to him. They only bring judgement and death.
Why did judgement come upon Achan's family. When he was digging the living room and hiding the wedge of gold and the Babylonish garment, there was no way the family couldn't have known. They neither dissuade nor report him to the leaders as God expected them. It was thus just that when God's judgement came, they all fell under the hammer.

C. Faith and loyalty to the Lord for true riches
Luke 16&#58;11
True riches is to have Christ. This requires faith in God and complete loyalty to Him.
