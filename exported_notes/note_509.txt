Abraham died in faith not having received the promises (his children will be like the stars in heaven and the sand of the seashore; Kings and princes will come out of his loins; his son will be the Savior of the whole world, etc.) but saw them afar off. Some of the promises made to Abraham have manifested now even though they did not manifest before his death.
No matter any country one lives in now, there is still a better country where there will be no crime, no persecution, no sickness, no wars, etc.).


Point -1 The faith of heavenward citizens living in earthly cities

A. The faith for promises in their generation 
Hebrews 11&#58;13-17; 1 Kings 8&#58;56
The promise of the son Isaac was received by Abraham in his generation. As at the time of king Solomon, the promised rest has come to Israel, only lost later by their sinfulness.

B. Foreseeing the promises beyond their generation 
1 Peter 1&#58;10
The old testament prophets (Daniel, Isaiah , Ezekiel, etc.) saw ahead of time the crucifixion of Christ, His sufferings and the glory that should afterwards follow. Those things the prophets saw, the apostles now elucidate on for the knowledge of the people. 

Hebrews 8&#58;6; 2 Peter 1&#58;3-4
The song, "the days of Elijah are here" is short-sighted. We have at this time, days that are more than the days of Elijah, because Christ has been revealed, crucified, resurrected and has drawn all men unto Himself. Our generation have greater privileges based on greater/better promises 

C. Fulfilling the promises of our own generation 
Hebrews 11&#58;13,40
In the generation of the prophets, the head of the serpent had not been bruised but now in our generation, the serpent head has been bruised and it is underneath our feet. I have dominion over the serpent now, which they did not have. God had provided better things for us in this generation.
 
Ephesians 3&#58;16-21
Christ in this generation, will grant unto us according to the riches of His (Christ's) glory. In our generation, we have Him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all we can ask, according to the power that is working in us (an opportunity the old generation did not experience except for a few people, the prophets in whom the Spirit of Christ which was in them testified of His sufferings and the glory afterwards). 

Point-2&#58; The faithfulness of heavenly-minded citizens against entanglement 

A. No return to filthy defilement 
There is no pressure that can come upon me now that I will return to filthy defilement. 
Daniel 1&#58;8
Personal decisions are mighty. Daniel had something like that. There should be personal decisions, decree, determination, declaration, devotion that I have made this new year that should be stronger than all the trials and temptations that Satan will send across my way. 

B. Forward move, no retreat to foreign domination 
Hebrews 11&#58;15
I am not mindful of the so-called "good old days" to have any desire to return to them

C. Forward mastery- no recourse to false doctrine 
We should have the mind to say to Satan, "get behind me, it is written"
1 Timothy 4&#58;1
Some people are seduced into the doctrines of devils. The odd practice of mentioning the name of a servant of God (which cause them to mention Christ's name less and less) in prayers, or discussion is a seducing doctrine of devil. Some say the God of Chosen, the God of Baba Abiye, etc. This is an error. 


Point-3&#58; The future of heavenly citizens in eternal country 

Hebrews 11&#58;16
We should take the privileges of our generation and not be desiring the privileges of previous generations. When I pray, I will pray in confidence because heaven will be standing at attention for my prayer

A. The desire of the better heavenly country 
Hebrews 13&#58;14; Psalms 73&#58;24-26
God is my portion forever. 

B. The description of the beautiful heavenly city
John 14&#58;1-3; Revelations 22&#58;3-5
Let not your heart be troubled because there are many mansions that Christ has gone to build for us. 
In heaven, no prison, hospitals, or mosquitoes.
