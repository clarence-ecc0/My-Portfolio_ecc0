*CHECK THE SCRIPTURES YOURSELF* 

            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _Living without worry in a time like this_

Anyone that has faith in Christ is justified from all his offences (sins) and has no fault before God. If I have no fault before God, that knowledge gives me instantaneous peace and I no longer live my life in fear. Everything hangs on faith in Christianity.

But, how does Christ determine if I truly have faith in Him? Does a simple mental belief in my head that He is Christ accomplish that? No! Demons also believe this and in fact, know it as a fact that Christ is Lord. 

My faith is certified by a life of perpetual obedience to Christ. He defined such a life as loving Him *(John 14&#58;15).* This means that I will follow all His instructions and will not deliberately do anything that I know He hates. 

A simple equation relates these three together.
*FAITH = OBEDIENCE = LOVING CHRIST* 

If a man has *disciplined himself* to obey Christ in all he does, He CANNOT lack anything that God has approved for him.  Conversely, it also means he CANNOT have whatever God has NOT approved for him. The expression "disciplined himself* as used above implies a willingness to accept the *'offered grace'* from God. The Holy Ghost said in *Titus 2&#58;11-12* that grace is available to EVERYONE.

When I said, *"he CANNOT lack anything that God has approved for him",* please understand that some things are approved for Brother-A while other things may be approved for Sister-B. For instance, we do not all have the same number of days on earth; we are not all apostles, pastors, miracle workers, etc. 

The Lord gave gifts to us severally according to the measure of grace He gave to us. That which the Lord has approved for me (as His child) will surely manifest at the appointed time. It cannot refuse to manifest. I need to believe this completely in order to have my peace in this troubled world.

Whatever I do not have (as His child) was either, [1&#93; not approved for me, [2&#93; I did not care to ask for it as He commanded that I should (His word said in *Ezekiel 36&#58;37* that I must still ask Him for what He has approved to be done for me) or, [3&#93; the approved time has not come. 

Brother, sister, why worry when you can pray? 
Pray to the Lord; ask Him for your needs but do this in faith remembering that faith implies love for, and obedience to Christ. Your testimony is on the way. 

*Shalom*
