We see therefore that we have a command to win our families and friends to the Lord. Let us be diligent in doing this because it pleases the Lord and it is also beneficial to us. 


The strategies for winning our relations and friends to the Lord
By constantly exposing them to the word of God in preaching (e.g., morning devotion) and practice (our lifestyle of righteousness everywhere and every time. We may want to find out how Ruth was won into the Lord that she appeared to have more faith in the Lord than Naomi who was asking her to go back to idolatry.

Through the demonstration of God's power (That's Elijah's method. The Lord that answereth by fire, let him be God)

By carrying our families to worship services and inviting our friends/acquaintances to worship services

Through literature evangelism (especially our friends)

By asking and adding them to Christian platforms where the word of God is preached in truth
By waiting on the Lord on their behalf.

Through our testimonies of God's provisions, promotions, preservations. 

Invitation of families and friends when we are about to receive ministration from the Lord. We see examples in the life of Cornelius and the Philippian jailor.

The benefits of winning relations and friends to the Lord 
The relationship between Abraham and Isaac presents us with a good example of how a father should lead his family to the Lord. Abraham demonstrated this even in the choice of a wife for his son, and in teaching by example, how to make a sacrifice to the Lord. Isaac knew, as a small boy that woods and fire are needed for a sacrifice but these are not enough when there is no lamb, the key material for the sacrifice (Genesis 22&#58;7). 
The Scriptures recorded in Malachi 3&#58;16 that those that feared the Lord spake often to one another and the Lord hearkened and opened a book of remembrance for them, and declared them as His.
They will be saved and have an eternity with Christ. This will be our greatest gift ever to our families and friends, an eternal gift that faded not away, reserved in heaven foe them. The gift of money, sound education is only temporary. 
The benefits of leading families and friends to the Lord include the following&#58;

1. They will be protected from all evils, and have access to the promises & provisions of the Lord

3. Peace of mind over families & friends, in life and in death. The song writer says, "in death as in life, thou wilt whisper Thy peace to my soul".
