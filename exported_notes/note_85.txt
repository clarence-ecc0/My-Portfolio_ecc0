            _Jesus only is our message_

*Theme&#58;* _Let the gods fight for themselves_

In the contemporary world today, many men in diverse religions fight for their gods in battles the gods are unable to fight for himself. Here, the servants become the protectors of gods rather than the gods being the protectors of the servants. An irony!

The word of God tells us in *Genesis 1&#58;28* that God gave dominion to man *_over every living thing that moveth upon the earth._* Satan and demons are among created living beings that move upon the earth. Man's dominion upon the earth therefore extends to all the demons (the governors of all false religions), and their ringleader, Satan who famously admitted to God that he specializes in _*"... going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it."*_   *(Job 1&#58;7)*

Bible records tell us that several men that were true servants of God challenged idolatrous religions and dislodged them without any direct resistance from the gods. You may have read these accounts in the Bible before but did you take notice of what actually happened in those fights? 


*[A&#93;.* Gideon versus Baal 
📍 Gideon destroyed the shrine of Baal as the Lord commanded him *Judges 6&#58;25-27*

📍 Baal could not fight Gideon but mobilized Gideon's kinsmen to kill him. Joash, Gideon's father resisted them and persuaded them to allow Baal to kill his son by himself if he could. *Judges 6&#58;28-32*

📍 When Baal failed in his first attempt to mislead Gideon's kinsmen, he went and mobilized the Midianites and the Amalekites to fight for him and of course, they failed as the Bible account recorded afterwards.  *Judges 6&#58;33*

*[B&#93;.* Elijah versus Baal

📍 In the contest between Elijah and the 450 prophets of Baal, Elijah prevailed and destroyed all the prophets. *1 Kings 18&#58;40*

📍 Baal could neither save his prophets nor fight Elijah. Baal's only strategy was to move its servant, Queen Jezebel to fight Elijah. *1 King 19&#58;1-2*


*[C&#93;.* Shadrach, Meshack and Abednego versus the great idol of Babylon.
📍Satan influenced king Nebuchadnezzar to build a great idol and to commanded all nations, languages and tongues to worship him through it. *Daniel 3&#58;1*

📍 Shadrach, Meshack and Abednego refused to worship the idol.

📍 Satan who knows he has no power over God's children, sent his human slave (as usual) to fight for him; he sent King Nebuchadnezzar with the threat of death by fire. 


*[D&#93;.* Hezekiah versus the Idols of Israel.

📍Idolatry had brought the wrath of the Lord upon Judah and Israel. King Hezekiah wrote to all Judah and Israel that they should return to the Lord and keep a passover to Him. *2 Chronicles 30&#58;1.*

📍 The people responded and went out and destroyed all their idols, cutting down the groves, broke the images, and threw down the altars, in Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim amd Manasseh. * Chronicles 31&#58;1.*

📍 The response of the idols (as usual) was to move King Shenacherib to fight against King Hezekiah. *2 Chronicles 32&#58;1*

📍 The comments of King Shenacherib clearly showed the real reason for his invasion. He was angry that the idolatrous altars were destroyed and King Hezekiah has commanded all the people to offer sacrifices henceforth at the newly cleansed altar at the Lord's temple in Jerusalem. *2 Chronicles 32&#58; 11-12*

Take notice that in the _*first verse of each of the three chapters above,*_ critical decisions were made.

In the _*first verse of Chapter 30,*_ King Hezekiah appealed to the people to abandon idolatry and be joined unto the Lord;  in the _*first verse of Chapter 31,*_ the people obeyed the king and demonstrated it by destroying their idols and, in the _*first verse of Chapter 32,*_ Satan, unable to fight King Hezekiah or the people by himself, sent his servant, the King of Assyria to fight for him. 

The scenario is the same. From the old testament to the new testament, and up to the contemporary time, Satan and his demons have never been able to stand against true servants of the true God. 

Jesus never ran from any demon or Satan, neither did the apostles, nor the early Christians. And, as it was in the Bible time, so it is today, that Satan always requires unsaved people to always fight for their gods.

The day that men allow all the gods in the land to fight for themselves will be the day that men will know the God that is real. That will be the day when the world will know real peace because all the false gods will be extinguished.

Pray that Jerubbaal will happen, that men will allow Baal to fight for himself.
