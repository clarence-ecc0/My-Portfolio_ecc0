{{text:I had just rounded off my morning devotion this morning and was about to leave the spot where I did my study when I saw a booklet titled _*The Prominence, Properties and Permanence of Love*._

As I scanned through it, I came upon a story that touched me deeply. I have copied it verbatim below and shared with us hoping that it will speak to you as it spoke to me. Read and be blessed in Jesus name.

*The story of a Christian statesman*

It was the eve of a crucial political gathering where he had to convince the electorate to vote for him. 

He was preparing his address when a neighbor came  to ask for help. A child was dying in the neighborhood. 

An old woman who knew this statesman came with tears and sorrow to tell him of the situation and asked him to come and speak comforting words to the boy and lead him to the Lord so that he would die in peace. 

The decision was a difficult one, but this brother left for the bedside of the dying boy. The boy's death was prolonged. The statesman has to sit by the deathbed offering words of hope, faith and trust in the Lord to this boy. Finally, in the morning and with a smile, this boy closed his eyes in death.

There was no time to prepare the political speech. Unprepared, this statesman went for the rally. The people gathered. He delivered his speech and it was the greatest speech he ever delivered in his life. God seemed to say that the good turn of helping a dying boy to see His face deserved another.
