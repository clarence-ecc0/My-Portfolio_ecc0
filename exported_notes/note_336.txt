2 Timothy 2&#58;19-21, 22-26
God has a foundation to His word, will and it stands sure forever. The foundation also has a seal of approval. The standard of the work for the worker is firm and will not change. It is the worker that must change to attain the standard. To serve the Lord as worker, the seal/standard demands that we depart from iniquity. 
Yes, some vessels in the church are unto honor and some to dishonor but it is never the will of God that any vessel should be unto dishonor. Aaron became a vessel unto dishonor but it wasn't God's will, and so was Dathan and Abiram, Achan all who fell away. God expects that individuals should purge themselves to become vessels unto honor. Doing this requires us to "flee also youthful lusts".
