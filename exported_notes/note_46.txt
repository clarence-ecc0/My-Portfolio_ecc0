Point-1&#58; Christ compelling call
Luke 5&#58;27

a. His constant call to repentance and salvation
Luke 5&#58;31-32; Mark 1&#58;14; Luke 13&#58;3,5; 24&#58;47

Christ was never bothered about how strong His sermon was, weather people will leave Him or remain. He preached the truth as it ought to and only the truly converted stayed with Him. We ought to be like that too. Members that can be stolen by other assemblies are not truly members. We must earnestly contend for the truth. Never change your message because some people or even the leadership do not accept your message. Stand by the truth, preach it only and God is satisfied with you. 

b. The creative conversion to righteousness and sonship
Luke 10&#58;20; Acts 3&#58;19,26; James 5&#58;19

To preach repentance or conversion, we must have experienced the same thing. Once anyone has erred from the truth, no matter his title or head-knowledge, he needs conversion afresh. 

c. The compelling consecration to the responsibility of soul winning 
Acts 2&#58;37-40, 41-42


Point-2&#58; Christ confirmed choice for Simon Peter and all subsequent preachers
John 6&#58;63

a. The personal choice to faithfully follow Christ
John 6&#58;67-69, 70; Mark 10&#58;28-30; 

The word of God is always raw. There was no embellishments in it then and there should not be any today. Half gospel and half truth can never save anyone. Miracle- and healing-messages without the preaching of the nature of God (holiness) can never save anyone. Accept the word of God as raw as it comes and do not react to it negatively. 

The verse God gave to pastor Kumuyi when he started was Jude 1&#58;3. It has guided him up till now. 
I have the strength to withstand persecution over what I believe in. God is with me. Nothing will take my conviction away in Jesus name. 

b. The powerful choice of fruitful followers of Christ.
John 15&#58;16; Acts 1&#58;24

It is Christ that will make our fruits remain, it is not our compromising with those that threaten to scatter our membership if we remain resolute in our conviction. Pastor Kumuyi preached a powerful message in Ghana and the church leadership there then threatened to soften or he would loose all. He persisted and came back for another meeting only to meet a few. He continued with the few and it has grown to what we see today. Lesson&#58; Continue to do what is good even if people are deserting you. The house will be filled up again, with better people. 


c. The peculiar choice to fearlessly follow Christ.
Galatians 1&#58;15-16

When you have been called by God, you do not need the opinion of any authority or even the agreement of your own flesh. Whether the people or your flesh agree or not, move on. God will do His part of the bargain. 


Point-3&#58; Christ comprehensive commission to Simon and all saved and sanctified people
John 21&#58;15-17,19

a. The unchanging concern that we should follow Christ 
John 8&#58;12,31

What is on our inside cannot be different from what is seen of us outside if we are truly following Christ

b.  The undeniable command that we should totally follow Christ 
Mathew 28&#58;18-20

We are to reach people to do, practice, all that Christ has commanded us. We are to observe all the doctrines of Christ. Christ cannot abandon me if I am teaching people to do, practice all He has commanded. All I need to do is to be faithful to Him and I am preserved. Stress and distress from man to me because of the work of the Lord in my hand is incapable of cutting off my life. I have a covenant of life with Him.

c.  The unceasing Clarion call to teachable followers of Christ
2 Timothy 4&#58;1-3-5
