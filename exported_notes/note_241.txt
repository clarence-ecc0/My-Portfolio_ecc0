TOPIC&#58; CONQUERING YOUR MOUNTAINS THROUGH THE CHANGE MAKER

Roman 8&#58;37; Mathew 17&#58;20-21
Despite all that are in opposition before me, seen and unseen, I am more than a conqueror through my Change Maker (God)

Why can we not do the things we want for our nation? Unbelief is responsible.


Point-1&#58; Moving away the mountains by faith 
Our change begins with our language, our proclamation to the mountain. 
(1) A change maker must change his language, mind how he speaks to the mountain. I used to say, "Mountain, are you still there? Okay, I move away because of you." Now, my language has changed to, "Mountain, move away because of me."

(2) Next, there is a change of my heart about the mountain. 
Our mountain is not Everest, it may be sickness, poverty, etc. Nebuchadnezzar was a mountain before Shadrach and his brethren, Kilimanjaro was not their mountain, it stood as no obstacle to them. The Pharisees were the mountain before Christ and the apostles. Our mountains are personal and as changer makers, we proclaim and as we believe, the mountain shall be moved because of us. 

Christ says, some mountains will not move without prayer and fasting. We follow that divine counsel and it is done. Fasting is defined as below.

F = Faith. Faith without food

A= Abstinence.  From the smell, the look and the taste of food

S = Submission. We still have to say, "Thy will be done". If the mountain is there to strengthen me, we do not insist that our will be done rather than God's. A recruit can't pray away a mountain that his commander has allowed for his training. Apostle Paul shouldn't pray away the 'Thorn in his flesh' that is for his training in humility. King Saul was a mountain before David; Saul of Tarsus was a mountain before the early believers, etc. Remember, your thoughts about the mountain may not be God's thoughts about it

T = Temperance. If we are fasting, it will be with temperance. If I can't endure fasting for a reasonable length of time, that may be a suitable measure of my temperance. Am I temperate in all things, including food?

I = Iniquity. While we are fasting, we stay away from iniquity (fighting, cheating, thoughts of violence, etc.)

N = Name. You are not fasting in your name but in the name of Christ

G = Glory. Whatever I ask that will not give you glory, do not grant it, Lord. 


Point-2&#58;  Making advantage of mountains without fear
Instead of mourning over mountains, moping at mountains, moving around mountains, I can take advantage of every mountain in my life. I can use it to my advantage. The Commander (God) uses mountains to train His recruits.
Fear has been our hindrance to taking advantage of our mountains. A curse was placed on a family and all are suffering from it. But the Bible said, a curse cause-less cannot come to you, especially as a child of God. 
Jesus said, His Father has blessed me, and an enemy (through his master, Satan) has placed a curse on me, Who do I believe? Jesus, or Satan? God sees my fear of Him in a world controlled by Satan as my wisdom; Satan sees my fear of God in a world controlled by him as my weakness. Again, who do I believe, Jesus or Satan? Jesus of course. 
I move away from my enemy and allow the Holy Ghost to address him.
By grace, my hand laid the foundation of my software, by grace, my hand shall conclude it in Jesus name. Zechariah 4&#58;6

Point-3&#58;  Mounting above your mountains by flight
If only we can think, we can fly over our mountains. 

Isaiah 40&#58;28-31; Job 39&#58;27; 
There are many things that we know that we do not personalize, apply in our lives. Yet, they are the things that make us conquer our mountains. 
An old man strengthened by heaven will out-perform the youth with his natural strength.  I will be running and walking with the renewed strength (from the Lord) until I go above my mountain.

The bus that will move me above my mountain is coming. I won't be anxious even though I can't see any sign of the bus yet, but I will wait upon the Lord and my strength shall be renewed.
