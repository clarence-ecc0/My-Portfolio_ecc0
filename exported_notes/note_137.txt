*Theme&#58;* _Our experiences during our daily temporary sleeps and the final permanent sleep_ 

God's word teaches us that death is sleep. 

The word of God has not left us in the dark over the connection between death and sleep. In *John 11&#58;11,* Christ said,

"Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; but I go, that I may awake him out of sleep."
Afterward, Jesus said plainly in verse 14, "... Lazarus is dead"

*1 Thessalonians 4&#58; 14* says,
"For if we believe that Jesus *died* and *rose* again, even so them also which *sleep* in Jesus will God bring with him. 

I believe God has given us dreams during our daily temporary sleeps to epitomize (show us) what is ahead for us when we enter into our final permanent sleep (death). For instance, our liberty and rejoicing while we are awake may continue or be drastically changed in dreams to fear, horror or severe suffering when we fall asleep. The opposite may also be our case.

While what we experience in dreams during our temporary daily death may not be the result of our temporary daily deeds, God's word, the Holy Bible, has clearly prophesied that our experiences during our final permanent death will definitely be according to our deeds. Below Bible reference is taken from the book of Romans. (Please, take note that the words in italics are mine)

We can tell what our experiences will be in our final permanent sleep. If yours look gloomy, it can still be changed. Accept Christ and believe (obey) the gospel.

My prayer is that this message will ultimately minister peace to your soul in Jesus name.
