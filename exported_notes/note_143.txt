What does it mean to overcome? Who is an overcomer and what or who did he overcome? 
Let's x-ray these questions with the analogies below.

A young lady from an educationally backward family became a graduate after facing severe satan-inspired distractions and oppositions. She chose to use what she had to get what she needed, her body for the money and marks from the lectures. 

She gave her testimony with tears in her church, declaring how God helped her to overcome the forces of evil to become the first and only graduate  in her family tree. 
Is she truly an overcomer?

The poverty in Mr Samuel's family was great and it has its origin in the curse placed on his own father and his lineage by an enemy of the family. 

Mr Samuel's strategy to overcome the enemy was to work hard and make money by all means. So he sent his youngest son to the town, to become an apprentice and learn a trade.

Truly, the young David was industrious. He also attended services and worship at his master's church. He learnt at the service one day that those that are God's children have the power to overcome the enemy and every curse placed on them. 

Young David looked at the poverty in the family and resolved more fervently to fight Satan through the power of his mind and muscle. His strategy? Work hard and steal from 'Master'. He succeeded, and now he has a big shop with money rolling in. He now testifies, "praise the Lord, I have overcome the demon of poverty unleashed on my family through a generational curse. I am an overcomer. 
Is he an overcomer?

We are not overcomers because we won physical victory. There must be a spiritual victory of Satan and his team before we can say our physical victory is an evidence that shows we are overcomers. Conversely, we may not have won a physical victory and yet we are truly overcomers. Recognize that Paul the apostle, Peter the apostle, James the apostle, John the apostle all were prisoners and all but John was killed and yet, they were truly overcomers. It is not our physical successes that qualifies us as overcomers. It is our spiritual victory (successful resistance of Satan) despite all that Satan did to take us away from the way of God that is the evidence that we overcame him. When we accept his ungodly ways (through threat or enticement) and we have some physical victories over backwardness in our lives,we did not overcome him rather, he overcame us. 
May God give us understanding in Jesus name.
