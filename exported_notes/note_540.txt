{{text:THE BELIEVER'S AUTHORITY OVER DEMONS, DISEASES AND DEATH 

Luke 10&#58;19;  1 Corinthians 15&#58;53-57
The word of God tells us that believers have authority over demons, diseases and death.  
Christ retrieved all the powers that fallen man transferred to Adam at cavalry and has given it to those that believe upon His name. At resurrection when mortal man shall have put on immortality, the power of death over man will be vanquished forever.

1. The powerlessness of the sinful man over demons, diseases and death.

2 Peter 2&#58;19
When man sinned, he lost his dominion and he that overcame him (Satan) seized his dominion and held him in bondage. Genesis 1&#58;18; Luke 4&#58;5-7.
Since the fall of man, he has been in the bondage of Satan. Isaiah 14&#58;12,17

Anyone who attempts to exercise dominion over Satan while he is still living in sin will do so at his own peril (John 13&#58;16). A slave cannot exercise authority over his master. 
Acts 19&#58;13-16


2. The power of the sinless man over demons, diseases and death 
Satan is the head of the disobedient created beings. So long as he cannot find any fault in you, he cannot rule over you rather, you rule over him by the dominion God gave to you at the beginning of creation. The prince of this world cometh and hath nothing in me (John 14&#58;30)

Christ came to make us sinless so that we can have our dominion back Luke 10;19, 17-18. Demons and Satan are subject to the saints through the name of Christ.

Believers in Christ do not live their lives in fear of death. They know that death is not the end of life for them.  

Hebrews 2&#58;14-15
The fear of death has made the sinful man to live in fear of him that has the power of death (the devil). Christ came in the likeness of a sinful man, was put to death and rose again, demonstrating to the sinful man that if he turns over to Him in repentance and submission , even if Satan puts him to death, He will raise him up from the dead.  This is the provision that sets the once sinful man free from his bondage of fear of death
1 Corinthians 15&#58;53-57


3. Our call to power over demons, diseases and death
Mathew 11&#58;28-30
Christ has issued out the call for salvation to all men&#58; come into me, all that are under the grievous labor of Satan and receive rest. 

Those that answer the call of Christ are not only freed from their own heavy burden under Satan but are graciously given power by Christ to destroy the works of the devil in the lives of others, including healing the sick, delivering the oppressed and raising the dead (Mathew 10&#58;8)
