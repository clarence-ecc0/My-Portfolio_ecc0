[B&#93; MEDICATION MANAGEMENT TIPS

1. Drugs that should be discontinued or continued after we feel better.

2. Go to the hospital or pharmacy with all the drugs you are using and show the health workers there (doctor, pharmacist, nurse)

3. Keep unused drugs safe from children but, discard the liquid drug preparations that have been opened for up to two weeks. 

4a. Do not stop taking drugs for chronic ailments (HTN, DM, Anti-psychotic medications, etc.) by yourself. Seek counsel.

4b. Monitor the effects of the drugs you are taking. The doses may be changed depending on your body's response to it. 

5. Do not take drugs without the advice of a health professional (or with the advice of a lay person ). It can be dangerous  

6. Do not mix orthodox medicines with herbs. Everything we take in become chemical and they can interact

7. If you are taking your drugs and are not getting better, go back to the health care provider who gave you the drug. The drugs or the dosage can be changed if necessary
