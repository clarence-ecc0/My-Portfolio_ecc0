You cannot catch men in a state of fear because they will see that you lack the confidence. Why are you swayed by people's opinions? The Lion is not swayed by the opinion of thousands of sheep

a. His constant call to repentance and salvation

b. conversion to righteousness and sonship 
Do not tell people that if you give your life to the Lord, you will be prosperous, you will be saved.  Christ did not even say they should give their lives to him but should first repent. He can't receive them with their sins. When they repent and are converted, Christ will receive them. Our message will this not to tell people to give their lives to Christ but that they should repent and be converted.

c. The compelling consecration to the responsibility of soul winning.

a. The personal choice to faithfully follow Christ
