PART-1&#58; The practical purpose of glorifying God and edifying others

1 Corinthians 10&#58;23-24; 6&#58;12-14; Romans 14&#58;16-19
All things that are lawful (alright) for me are not all expedient (profitable). Some things that are proper in some situations may be improper before some audiences. 

If I give money to a young lady to help her, that is good but will people think I am doing this for a certain purpose? Will my good deed raise suspicion in people's mind? If yes, my good deed is not good enough. My actions must always come out to make others righteous, making peace in their homes or family. Our good deeds must create peace and not lead to chaos, suspicion, etc. Our good deeds must edify others. 

Mathew 5&#58;16; Philippians 2&#58;4-5; 1 Corinthians 14&#58;12
Even the good thing you want to do, does it paint another person in a bad light? If yes, think twice. Your good deeds (because of your passion) should not rob others of their salvation, provoke them to lose their salvation. Your Christian activities should not drive your family members or close associates from God. Your deeds should consider others unto edification. When you keep the family in poverty, children can't continue their education, etc. because you spend all your resources in the church, if it will drive your family away from God, rethink it. You are not edifying them  


PART-2&#58; The personal price of glorifying God and eating without offence

1 Corinthians 10;25-29 Romans 14&#58;20
If you are to buy meat in the market and your conscience tells you that this probably came from an idolatrous ceremony that just took place, do not buy it for conscience sake. Buying and eating it will not edify others. 
If you hear that people are remembering their dead father and the ideology behind the ceremony is that the dead will be favored where he is, and you know that is an idolatrous belief, you can't eat that meal. 
The Christian Life is a communal life. We consider how our affairs affect others. 
The things I say in my home at dinner table, is it able to destroy the conscience of my children towards God, Christianity? If yes, refrain from such discussions. 

All things are indeed pure but they are not longer pure if there is an offence. To know that people are offended are made weak in their Christian faith by a particular character and to continue in it does not edify others. It is unchristianly. 

Psalms 106&#58;13-15; John 6&#58;25-26
Our purpose for seeking God matters. If it is for the satisfaction of the body, to gain secular things and not spiritual, it is a waste of opportunity. 


PART-3&#58; The pleasing pattern for glorifying God and evangelizing others 

1 Corinthians 10&#58;31-33; 2 Corinthians 4&#58;6; Colossians 3&#58;17, 23-24; Mathew 7&#58;12
Whatever we do, we must ensure it pleases God. First, we say whatever we do, it must edify others but now, we are also learning that whatever we do, it must glorify God. 

1 Corinthians 10&#58;32; Philippians 1&#58;10-11
Do not be provoking the Jews or the gentiles. Do not allow your worship of God to make you an offence to others. For instance, blocking the road for service, or using loudspeakers at bedtime that disturbs the people that are sleeping is not good enough. 

Acts 25&#58;15-16; 1 Corinthians 10&#58;33
We must exercise ourselves to always have a conscience that is void of offence towards God and men. We must seek to have the habit that desires to please others, seeking their profits (particularly for their salvation) at all times. Backsliders or sinners should see our deeds and utterances and be driven to salvation or remain in the faith of Christ.

Romans 10&#58;1; 1 Corinthians 9&#58;19-23
Desire the salvation of your people (family, friends, colleagues, etc.). Whatever character that you have (my strong will against deceitful behavior) that will stand in the way of salvation of my people, I must mellow down so that I do not become a hindrance to them. Always consider the effect of your actions on the ministry and comport yourself accordingly. We have to endure all things so that others can be saved
