Point-1&#58; Definite facts about difficult times

Job 5&#58;6-8
Difficult times are part of life. Some facts about difficult times we need to know&#58;

a. God knows about the difficult times that we are in.  2 Timothy 3&#58;1; Revelations 2&#58;8-9
The Lord says that although we may be experiencing poverty, He says we are rich

b. Difficult times are part of human life. 
Abraham, Isaac, Jacob (with Rachael) all experienced childlessness and famine. Romans 8&#58;22-
We shouldn't allow difficult times to affect us too much

c. The devil is the reason behind the difficult times - insecurity, kidnapping, etc. During the millennium reign when Satan will be at the bottom of the pit, these will not be there

d. Some difficult times are unavoidable and even necessary. 
Israel left Egypt and must go to the promised land. Hunger and thirst were on the way and they needed to face this to achieve their great inheritance. Students suffer a lot to achieve their degrees. Difficult times are stepping stones to our destination 

e. Difficult times don't last forever. Abraham may have been childless for 25 years but he had his promised child (and more) before he died. Job suffered but his redemption eventually came. Psalm 30&#58;5; Job 14&#58;14
Joy always comes in the morning for the Lord's children. My change is coming therefore I will wait.


Point-2&#58; Diligence and fervency in difficult times 

Hebrews 11&#58;6-; 
If we diligently seek God , in times of trouble, He will come for us. 
Some things to take note of in difficult times.
1. Don't be complacent, do something in difficult times. Romans 12&#58;11
2. Learn to manage your resources 
3. Don't be cast down(discouraged). My soul, why art thou cast down, trust in the Lord. Psalms 42&#58;5
4. Don't murmur over anything. It displeases God and stop His intervention. Do not attempt to go back into the world (because of difficult times) as Israel tried to go back to Egypt. It displeased God.
5. Don't compromise on God's standards/instructions in difficult times (as Saul did when his army left him and the Philistines were gathered against him). It may be a test. God's REST is for those who come out BEST in His TEST. Endure and pass the TEST. 

Attitudes we should demonstrate in difficult times.
1. We are to be faithful (in service, tithing, giving, etc.). Revelations 2&#58;9-10
Our intervention will only come if we do not compromise.

2. Diligence and fervency in difficult times.
We need to put on our faith in difficult times like the faith of Abraham. Romans 4&#58;17-21

Fervency&#58; Romans 12&#58;11-
No matter the difficulties around him, a fervent believer is also hopeful, prayerful, tithing, etc.
