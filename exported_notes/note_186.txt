Follow up is the process of nurturing and conserving the fruits of evangelism.

PEOPLE

 counsellors irrespective of your department

iii. Purpose for FU
To collect data from the converts (fill the convert slip once GS asks the counsellors to step in)

PLAN

PARTICIPATION DURING THE CRUSADE 

What to do
Each group is assigned a portion on the crusade ground. Number the clusters of 25 chairs by 20 rows.
The crusade layout is arranged in clusters of 500 chairs

Each group should have a supervisor (a full time counsellor). He distributes the decision slips and collects them back.

The supervisor moves to his cluster (segment)

The supervisor gives out the crusade packages 

The supervisor wears a different jacket design 

The counsellors do not preach to converts again at the crusade after GS message

Each convert must be followed up within 24 hours of decision. You are leaving them for Satan to get over to them and take them back again if you delay. We must reach out to them as quickly as possible within 24 hours.

Initial FU (1st week)
Can be thru bulk SMS
Can be thru direct phone call possibly that very day or the following day.

Intensive FU (4th to 6th week)
This is when we invite them to the service
Physical contact to invite them to church programs
Visit them not only on the fellowship days but on other days to establish rapport. This increases the chance of bringing them to the service with you.

Integrating FU
Here, the convert is integrated into the church. Water baptism after the discipleship class has been conducted.
