{{text:FAITH OR OBEDIENCE TO GOD'S LAWS, WHICH IS HIS PLAN FOR OUR SALVATION?


God did not plan it that men should attain righteousness through observance of the law. His intention was to assign righteousness to us as He sees our faith in Him (exemplified by complete submission to His word, His will and His ways). 

The promise to Abraham was evidently based on his faith in Him, not on any laws that Abraham kept (Galatians 3&#58;6).

Righteousness for salvation of man after his fall was only to be gotten by trusting in God again, through faith in HIM alone, faith that is confirmed by our obedience to His will, way, word that is written in our hearts.


What then was the purpose of the law that God gave through Moses/? (Galatians 3&#58;19)

We see this clearly as we understand what the Holy Ghost is teaching us in Galatians 3&#58;23-25

Therefore, to assign righteousness to Abraham's children while waiting for the manifestation of the promised Christ (the Seed), the transgressions of the people had to be managed. The law was the instrument that God gave to manage the transgressions.

But about 2000 years ago, the Seed (Christ) came. We have no need for the law again. All that is required of us for righteousness to be assigned to us is to have faith in Him (Christ), the express image of the Father and this faith, again is exemplified by our submission to His word, His way and His will. We will be counted righteous by God if this faith is seen in us.]text_span:[]}}
