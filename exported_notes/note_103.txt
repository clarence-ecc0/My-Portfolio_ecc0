Problem statement&#58; It is evident that donations from funders to execute public health interventions have dwindled over the years. With the myriad of challenges in the health sector still requiring system strengthening, it is very clear that recipients of donor funds should begin to mobilize local resources (in cash &/or in kind) to complement the donor funds in achieving the desired project objectives.


What was done&#58;
Howard University has towed the line of local resource mobilizations (in cash and in kind) to complement donor funds in implementing funded projects. In the SIDHAS project through the PEPFAR supported Sustainable Financing Initiative (SFI), Howard University carried out the decentralization of .... ART refill services to private sector pharmacies. Using the AVENIR costing scale, the effort of decentralization of ART refill services saved ..... donor funds that would have been required to maintain the personnel in the supported ART facilities. HIV case finding has been mostly through the donated HIV kits procured using the donor fund. The kits were sold to targeted subpopulations (AYP, index clients partners of unknown status, etc.) for improved case finding. Howard University also mobilized free donation of drugs worth over 7 million naira from pharmaceutical distributors across several states, along with .... private sector pharmacists to support community -based PMTCT and ART case finding. Pharmaceutical care&#58;

Problem statement&#58; Just as rational medication therapy can improve the quality of life, irrational therapy is not capable of achieving the desired treatment outcome but can, in some situations become a threat to the quality of life. When multiple medications are employed as is the case in HIV chemotherapy, a good understanding of, and adherence to pharmaceutical care principles will go a long way in ensuring that the treatment objectives of safety and effectiveness are achieved 

What was done&#58; Howard University has spent over 20 years developing people and setting up structures and processes in hospitals and community settings for the provision and documentation of pharmaceutical care services to patients across the 36 states and the FCT in Nigeria. This involved the development of service providers capacity in pharmaceutical care service provision and documentation, development of tools for pharmaceutical care documentation, setting up structures and procedures for routine review of collated pharmaceutical care data and use in decision making for continuous improvement in quality of medication usage.
