When God chooses to humiliate Satan to prove He alone is God

There was a day Satan entered trouble. Prophet Elijah has presented a challenge, " the God that answereth by fire, let Him be God".

Satan had 450 prophets of Baal and 400 prophets of the groove in the kingdom of Israel and all were called to the contest. On ordinary days, Satan could have given his servants the power to bring down fire from heaven. The Bible testifies that he has the power. 
Of the Beast that came out of the earth with two horns which spoke like the dragon, the Bible says,

_"And he doeth great wonders, so that he maketh fire come down from heaven on the earth in the sight of men,"_.  *Revelation 13&#58;13*

The servant of Job also erroneously reported that fire came down from heaven and destroyed his sheep and servants, sparing only him. We know already that this and all the other afflictions of Job were caused by Satan and not God

_"The fire of God is fallen from heaven, and hath burned up the sheep, and the servants, and consumed them; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee."_.  *Job 1&#58;16*

These all show that Satan had power to call down fire from heaven on ordinary days. But the day God's glory will be tested against the glory of Satan cannot be said to be an ordinary day. The prophets of Satan could not carry out their evil enterprise on the day of the Lord.
