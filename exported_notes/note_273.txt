Phillipians 3&#58;13-16
Many things have been done and are behind me. But, I am not looking at those things again rather, I am looking forward to the things that I must now do.


Point-1&#58; The purpose and the vision of a pioneering change maker
Daniel came to Babylon as a slave, captive, without a voice or value. He was assigned to where they wanted him. But Daniel reached Babylon with one secret&#58; He had a purpose in his heart. Change making requires possession of a vision, a purpose. Daniel had this.

The corrupt people will be aware when your way has changed and they will want to corrupt you. You must stand your ground and reject all their overtures.

As you read the book of Daniel, you will observe that nothing was known about him in chapters 1 & 2 but as he gets to chapter 3, he became known.
But Daniel was not alone. There were also other change makers beside Daniel (Shadrach et al). Where you are making your change, there are other change makers that your boldness will bring out. Go ahead and bring them out through your endeavors. 

They put Shadrach et al in a furnace but they came out. Every change maker will come out of any furnace the corruption will want to consign them.

Point-2&#58;  The priority and virtue of a progressing change model

Acts 20&#58;24
When you are working for God as His change maker, nothing moves you not should anything should cause you to count your life for anything. Your life is already hid with Christ in God. God is able to protect you so that you can bring about the change He appointed you for. 

Point-3&#58;  The proficiency and visibility of a proof-producing change mentor
There are things that a change maker cannot do. He has boundaries. Joseph was a change maker and there were things he couldn't do. It is the same with me. 
The problem with people most time is that they are not looking at the future but at the present. Joseph and all such change makers look more into the future rather than the present. 
There may be lies against you, and you may end up in prison but not to worry, Joseph entered and came out. Yours might be like that as well. In the prison, Joseph kept doing good. As a change maker is misunderstood, he should keep on doing good but his change will come. He may be forgotten in the prison but keep on doing good. 

Pharaoh made Joseph the teacher of his senators, he became the change mentor in Egypt.
I will do my nation proud by the Spirit of the Lord.
