TOPIC&#58; AWAKE, LOOSE YOURSELF, LEST YOU ARE LOST IN CAPTIVITY 

Isaiah 52&#58;1-2
We have a responsibility, a role we ought to play for our freedom from captivity. Christ said, stretch out thy hand; Christ did not come to stretch out the hand of the paralysed.

Point-1&#58; Loose yourself from the bands of captivity 

a. Strengthen yourself  from the north of captivity 
Isaiah 52&#58;1-
Sleep time is a time of no activity. We need to awake ourselves and get into activities. Enough of idleness. There is strength in us, waiting for us. Put it on. Once we open the door of our heart unto God, the uncircumcised (e.g., Lucifer) are locked out and can't come in again, forever. Any dust (sin) that has gathered in our lives, we shake them off. 
There is no enemy that is greater than our inner man. When we say 'no' and mean it, heaven says 'no' with us. What has hindered you from making progress? Say 'no' today and be freed by your own faith.

b. Sold? unsell yourself from the demons of captivity 
People sell themselves to demons, people that will impoverish them because we got pittance from them. We just make friends without finding out if the person can add to us or remove from us. Do not be like King Jehoshaphat before Ahab and his son. 
Ahab sold himself to Jezebel and unfortunately, Ahab could not unsell himself. He sold himself for nought, same thing with Samson who sold himself to Delilah. We are redeemed without money. The blood of Christ is greater than money and it redeemed us.


c. Strengthen yourself against the dictatorship of captors 
Despite all the manoevering of the captors over my life I am free today in Jesus name. 

Ephesians 6&#58;10,12-18
We need to be strong in the Lord because anything and anyone that will come to attack us, the Lord dwelling inside me is stronger than them all. 
The Holy Spirit said, "Take unto you; the Holy Spirit did not say, "give unto them"; I take the armor unto me by myself. It is a personal decision. 
Learn the habit of short prayers. Prayers must not be long for it to be effective.


Point-2&#58; Learning beyond yourself, the beauty without contamination 
We need to learn beyond what we already are. We will never go beyond ourselves if we restrict ourselves that way. There is something more for me to learn. 

Isaiah 52&#58;7
The gospel that brings peace, in it lieth the beauty of heaven, of holiness and of happiness all the days of our lives. Look away from yourself and look at the cavalry and you will go beyond yourself. This is what apostle Paul meant when he said, "I have not yet attained but I press towards the mark of His high (higher) calling." He was already saved, Sanctified and baptized in the Holy Ghost but there was more and he needed to attain those as well.


a. The gospel of salvation through His name
Isaiah 52&#58;6-7
With the receipt of salvation, my God reigneth in my life. I go to the office and there is evil charcoal spread upon my seat, it will not reign but my Lord, the God of heaven reigneth in my life. 


b. The goodness of the Savior for all nations

Isaiah 52&#58;9
The goodness of the Savior reaches me in my nation. The Lord's holy arm of sustenance, deliverance has been laid bare in the eyes of all the nations including Nigeria. I am not seeing the corruption of the enemy anymore but the liberation, sustenance , deliverance of my God.

c. The godliness of the Son now in our nature 
Christ came to implant His godliness in our nature. I am a partaker of the divine nature now.

Isaiah 52&#58;11-12
In the vessel of the Lord are mercy, godliness, grace and other good things and I am a bearer of that vessel. Bearing the vessel of the Lord, I do not go out in haste. I need to tarry and fill the vessel with the good things of the Lord before I go out to preach or minister to people. The Lord goes before me to all the preaching ground.

Point-3&#58; Lifted above yourself by the bruises of Christ
This very day, I am lifted above myself. I am taking note of where I am today (list them). I am not going down but going up. I will see this when I check myself. 
Lying down horizontally like all other men, there is  man that can lift me up above myself. One higher than all men is needed and Christ is He.

a. The amazing blessedness and supremacy of Christ
Isaiah 52&#58;13
I will definitely forget the dungeon where I was before

b. The astonishing brushes and sufferings of Christ 
Isaiah 53&#58;3-4
The bruises of Christ is what has given us great benefits. 

c. The accomplished boundlessness of salvation in Christ
When I am asleep, awake, my salvation is there with me, closer to me than any perplexity in the world.

Revelations 7&#58;9-17
When you look at that great multitude which no man could number, if you know my face, look very well and you will find me there among the multitude.
