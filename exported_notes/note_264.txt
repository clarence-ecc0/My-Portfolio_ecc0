TOPIC&#58; THE NEW ANOINTING FOR SONS, DAUGHTERS AND SERVANTS IN THE NEW COVENANT

Point-1&#58; Protection and security from the angers of the Lord
Numbers 11&#58;1,4

Complaining after all that the Lord has done for us displeases the Lord. We ought to acknowledge His goodness unto us- He make us to be ministers in His ministry. That is a great thing.
The mixed multitude amongst us can lead us to great trouble as they did to the church in the wilderness. We need to be watchful always.
Why murmur when you can ask for your needs and they will be met? Many murmur because they can't ask (i.e., they can't pray or, they have no assurance of answers because they are living in sin)

Malachi 2&#58;13
To come and ask with weeping before the Lord is a bad habit. Even human parents do not cherish it when their children come for heritage (bread, shirt, etc.)  and they start with weeping. We ought to speak with expectations as children because we have rights as children. The Holy Scriptures says, "this is the heritage of the servants of the Lord and their righteousness is of me". We are not slaves or beggars in our Father's house. That which will cause us to weep should rather cause us to reflect on why our prayer is unanswered concerning the cause of our cry? 
Is it that we asked out of order, are we in a season of broken relationship with our Father and He has refused to answer us, is He telling us it is not the right time but we are unable to discern His feedback to us OR, our lust for what we want is so much that we are not prepared to accept 'no' for an answer or wait for the the appropriate time? REFLECT!


Point-2&#58; Putting the Spirit on the seventy assistants in labor
The Lord said Moses was the one to choose the 70 elders. He checked their qualifications and they were people that were not sold to covetousness, they love the truth, and were not joined to the complainants, etc. The Spirit rested upon the seventy because they were qualified.

As we are saved and are free from external and internal sins, and no murmuring, etc. the Spirit of God will come upon us. We need to pray with all our might and request for His Spirit. As we are united with our leaders and one another, the Lord will pour out His Spirit upon us. A critical (criticizing) spirit will not be given the Holy Spirit of God. 


Point-3&#58; Pouring out the Spirit on sons with anointing from the Lord
The Spirit does not come alone but with unction, power, anointing, to do the work required of us. 
Our leaders may see that although we are saved, sanctified and yet, they see that we are powerless against trials and temptations. Our utterances give us away, our helplessness during trials gives us away. With the presence of an indwelling Hoy Ghost is the obvious manifestation of power.
