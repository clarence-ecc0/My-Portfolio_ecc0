{{text:TRUE REPENTANCE BEFORE GOD 
Check&#58;
There are many people that may be laboring for the Lord in one way or the other who may miss heaven because their repentance for their sin was not accepted. This needs not to be so. Everyone that repent of his/her sins (appropriately) and asks for forgiveness of sins should receive it. 
It is critical that our repentance is accepted by God. How can we confirm this? How can we check?

1. CHECK&#58; Were you sincere in the acknowledgement of your sins; confessed them and asked for forgiveness through the name of Christ?

Psalms 51&#58;3
"For I acknowledge my transgressions&#58; and my sin is ever before me."


2. CHECK&#58; Is there contrition in your heart (sorrow for sin)? 
   # David sinned great sins of adultery and murder and was forgiven; Saul sinned the sin of disobedience to carry out instructions and, although he acknowledged his sin, it remained. 


3. CHECK&#58; As you are asking for forgiveness, have you resolved in your heart that you will not go back to the same sin again, and have you asked the Lord to make you strong against the temptations that led to that sin the first time?

Romans 3&#58;25
"Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;"


4. CHECK&#58; Christ said the heavenly Father will not forgive you your trespasses if you will not forgive others that trespassed against you. 
  # Have you from your heart, forgiven all that have trespassed against you? 
  # What is the evidence that you have forgiven them? If you can, have you reached out to them or, allowed them to have access to you, and does the thought of reconciling bring joy to your heart?


5. The show of religion without true repentance does not reconcile anyone to God. Do you have true repentance? Has God accepted your repentance?
