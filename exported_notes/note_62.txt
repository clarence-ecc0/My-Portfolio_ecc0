{{text:TOPIC&#58; THE PRIVILEGE OF BELONGING TO GOD'S EARTHLY AND HEAVENLY FAMILY 
Mathew 12&#58;46-50

Of course God created all human beings but He is not the father of all human beings. 

Point-1&#58; The faithful brothers and sisters in Christ 
Mathew 12&#58;46-48; John 14&#58;8
Christ asked the question (Who is my mother and who are my brethren) in His capacity as God. He has a universal family and His brethren are all that come unto Him in the world, that hear the word of God and do them. A mother teaches, mentor, mould, encourages, refreshes and monitor a child. A mother in Christ will do all these. A brother is a burden-bearer, a repairer, restorer. The best brothers we have, to whom we go to if we have challenges are the brothers in the faith. A brother hears for us what we may not hear, sees what we may not see, and refines us. A sister is a supporter, stays with you & motivating you to move on, cheers you when you do well. 
a. The conversion of repentant sinner and backsliders
To belong to God's family, repentance is key. 
Acts 3&#58;19,26; James 5&#58;19-20
When we hear the word of God and truly feel sorrowful for our sins, inwardly we are convinced it was bad and made the decision not to do it again (that is repentance). That inward decision is repentance. When this happens, it means we have been converted from our old ways, we have rejected the old way. As the Lord sees that inward decision (we have decided not to repeat that evil life again), He forgives us and wipes off (blot out) the record of our sins.

b. The character of recognized sisters and brothers 
Mathew 12&#58;49-59; 1 Thessalonians 4&#58;3-4; Romans 12&#58;1-2

The recognized brothers and sisters of Christ are those that do the will of God in heaven (they may be in Christ Embassy, Living Faith, etc.); They are His brothers and sisters and they should be ours as well. Conformers to the world standard is not a real brother or sister of Jesus. 

c. The commitment of renewed souls as believers 
As real believers, our commitment as renewed souls are;
i. Committed to know God's will like the disciples of Christ did.
ii. Committed to submit to the word of God (as soon as I know God's word in any area of my life, I submit to it immediately).
iii. Committed, to do the will of God.

Hebrews 10&#58;7; Romans 8&#58;29
Christ as our eldest brother will do the will of God to the point of death. I will follow His steps in Jesus name.

Point-2&#58; The faithless brethren not subject to Christ
Mathew 12&#58;46-48
The earthly family should not hold us from doing the work of our heavenly Father. 

a. The evidence reaction fo false brethren 
Mark 3&#58;20-21; Phillipians 1&#58;15-16; 

These so-called friends of Christ tried to take Him away from His heavenly tasks. They are false-brethrensand are drawing people away from salvation.

b. The evil report of fallen backsliders 
Nehemiah 6&#58;3-4; Numbers 13&#58; 

Do not allow the evil reports (comments) of envious people to make you abandon the work of God. Do not waste your time arguing with them.
The evil report of the 10 spies prevented all the adults above 20 years to miss Canaan land.
