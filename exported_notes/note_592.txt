{{text:BLESSINGS FROM THE CROSS FOR ALL NATIONS 
Galatians 3&#58;8+14; Psalms 68&#58;1-2

Point- 1&#58; Saving help by Christ for all nations
Mark 16&#58;15
The light does not drive the darkness away little by little. Once the light comes, it takes over 100% from darkness. It is the same with coming to Christ. 


Point- 2&#58; Salvation and holiness through the crucified in all nations

There is available salvation for everyone in all nations. 
There are people that tend to sew for themselves their own garments of righteousness and holiness. Adam tried that and it was not sufficient. They were still naked. God had to do that for him.

There are some of Adam's descendants whose garment is "I don't smoke", "I do not womanize", etc. Even though Adam sewed his garment, he was still driven out of the garden of Eden. Christ came with the right garment of righteousness from heaven and is available for everyone that is willing.

Isaiah 56&#58;5-11
The robe of righteousness from Christ to us will make us look glorified, unlike the garment that Adam sewed. 
A man wearing dirty clothes must put it off before he can put on the clean, new cloth. You can't join the old and the new cloth together. It is the same with salvation. We must put off the old life (robe of sin) before we put on the garment of righteousness. 
A believer must not be like the pig that will get back into the mud again. We must stay clean. The wicked must forsake his old way ( garment of sin).

No man can pay for the robe of righteousness. It is sewed in heaven and only Christ can pay for it, in the currency that is accepted in heaven, an unblemished blood.


Point-3&#58; Supernatural help from the cross for all nations
Isaiah 41&#58;10, 13
There is no sin that any man has committed that God cannot cleanse him out of. If there is contrite in my heart, He will save He will deliver. He is with me right here.
