{{text:TOPIC&#58; PROCLAIMING THE GOSPEL OF SALVATION TO EVERY CREATURE
If you are born again, you are already at the table of the Lord. Invite others to the table. 
Psalm 51&#58;12,13; Mark 16&#58;15-16;
As we are at the table of the Lord enjoying His blessings. We ought to bring others in to enjoy the same blessings as well. 

Point-1&#58; Preaching repentance towards God before salvation 

Mark 1&#58;14-15; Luke 3&#58;6-8; Luke 24&#58;27; 2 Peter 3&#58;9; Acts 3&#58;19, 26; Acts 5&#58;30-3; 17&#58;30-31; 20&#58;20
To come to the banquet table of the Lord requires first, repentance. Sinners feel they are robbed of enjoyment when they repent. This is the lie of Satan that keeps many in their sins. On the contrary, repentance brings great blessings of rejoicing. Forgiveness  goes along with repentance There can't be forgiveness without repentance.

Acts 26&#58;19-20; 2 Corinthians 7&#58;10; 2 Timothy 2&#58;24-25; Revelations 3&#58;15, 16, 19-22


Point-2&#58; Possessing the righteousness of God with salvation

Romans 10&#58;9-10; Isaiah 45&#58;8; 61&#58;10; Romans 10&#58;13; Psalms 71&#58;15; 24&#58; 3-5; Romans 6&#58;13-18; 8&#58;1-4; 2 Corinthians 5&#58;17-21; Ephesians 4&#58;21-24; Philippians 3&#58;9; 1 John 3&#58;4-10
Salvation is always connected to righteousness. Garments of salvation and robe of righteousness go together .
Obedience gives unto us righteousness. There is no righteousness without obedience to God. (Romans 6&#58;16)
They that live in disobedience are sinners. They cannot see God.

Point-3&#58; Promoting the reign of God through salvation 

Isaiah 52&#58;7; 26&#58;13; Judges 8&#58;22-23; 1 Samuel 8&#58;7
I must be promoting the reign of God in my own life. Then, I can promote it in the lives of others. When I tell people how they can be saved, I am promoting salvation.
If God has used someone to bless/deliver you, you must not allow the person to rule over you. Gideon delivered Israel from the Midianites and he refused to rule over them. If you are the one that is a blessing to people, do not become a God to the person/people. 
Man must be ruled over. If someone rejects God from ruling over him, then Satan must rule over him. 

Psalms 19;7-13; 119&#58;133; Romans 6&#58;11-14; James 4&#58;7; Luke 4&#58;8; Mathew 6&#58;10
Two powerful authorities by your side asking you to submit - God and Satan. We are to submit ourselves to God and to resist the devil. Note that rejection of God automatically means submission to the devil. You are a servant of whomsoever you obey. You can't command devil to move away from you when you are not obeying Jesus. You must serve one.

As we submit ourselves to the authority of God, the kingdom of God is established in our lives, family, business. Then, the will of God (not the will of witchcraft, enemies, occultist) will be done in our lives. You will not be afraid of the enemy if you have already submitted to God.
