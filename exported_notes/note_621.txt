{{text:TOPIC&#58; THE PERMANENT CONQUEST AND OUR PRESERVED INHERITANCE 

God was waiting for the cup of iniquity of the amorite to be filled before He would bring judgement upon them and give their land to Israel. The sinner must be careful and repent before his cup of iniquity gets filled up.

God could have wiped out the Amorites as easily as He used fire to wipe out the land of Sodom and Gomorrah; He could have used water as He did in the time of Noah but He chose to use man here. Known unto the God are all His works from the beginning of the world.

We are in a different dispensation. Israel had enemies they must destroy but we have humans (engineered to become our enemies) that we must love. We do not conquer our human enemies. We have common enemies and these are Satan, sin and self (flesh) and the Lord has given us the power to conquer them. We do not even conquer our persecutors, we are to win them unto the Lord.

The husband is not to conquer the wife and vice versa. 
Should we command the sun to "Stand still" today? No. We are not fighting the war that requires the sun to stand still for us in order to finish our victory. The enemy we are fighting today is Satan; he fights us in the light and in the dark and we overcome him always, in light or darkness through Christ . We do not need the sun to stand still for us today to contend with our enemy.
