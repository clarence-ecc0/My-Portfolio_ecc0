✓ In all the teachings of Christ during His earthly walk, He never admonished His disciples to beware/afraid of devils/ demons; He never ran away from them but they rather ran away from Him and trembled before Him. 
✓ As we look at the lives of all the great men in the Bible as well and take notice that lived or warred in idolatrous nations, from Abraham, Isaac, Moses, Joshua, David, the prophets, they were never afraid of demons. They could destroy their altars (Gideon did). But they were to be conscious (beware) of men who could harm them (Abraham and Isaac out of fear of men denied their wives).  We are not to be afraid of devils but to be mindful of their manipulating us (to sin) and men around us (to persecute us)
✓ Christ went and stayed in the desert, slipped out of the hands of the Pharisees when they wanted to cast Him down the cliff and He did all these when His time was not up. We know that Christ instructed His disciples that if they persecute them in one city, they should run to another city (Mathew 10&#58;23)
✓ When men of God speak against demons or Satan, because they are powerless against God's servants, they mobilize men (their slaves), to take up the fight against God's servants, therefore Christ enjoined us to beware of men. 

In all these, Christ enjoined us also Not to live our lives in fear but to do His work fearlessly. The command to run to another city during persecution is not a command of cowardice. It is for two reasons&#58; (a) Believers are peacemakers and cannot fight back. There is no record in the New Testament of believers fighting back the persecutors (remember 1 Peter 2&#58;23); (b) men that chose to fight believers are just opposing God's word and work. We are enjoined to take the word to another city (When Christ healed the mad man at Gardarene, the people sent Him away and and He went. When He was coming back (after the preaching of the healed mad man), they welcomed Him into their city.

But, should everyone then run because of persecution? No. We know that the believers were scattered abroad (and they went out, preaching the word) but the apostles remained in Jerusalem, preaching the word and supporting & guiding the field workers. 

God actually wants us to be fearless. He instructed Jeremiah to be fearless though a youth (Jeremiah 1&#58;8, 17). Also, Revelations 21&#58;8 counted the fearful amongst those that are to perish in the lake of fire. We will beware of men but will not be afraid to speak the truth before men. 
✓ Moreover, what are we afraid of when the Lord has promised that He will "fulfill the number of [our&#93; days?" A child of God will only die at his appointed time. Many attempts were made on Christ but He died at the appointed time, at the place and in the manner prescribed. The two witnesses in Revelations 11&#58;3-7 finished their ministry before they could be killed, Stephen also and with all the idols in the land of Canaan, none could cut short the lives of Moses, Joshua, Gideon, David, the apostles, etc. None can cut short our lives in Jesus name.
