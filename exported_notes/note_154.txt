TOPIC&#58; DIVINE PRESCRIPTION FOR SERVING GOD ACCEPTABLY 

1 Chronicles 13&#58;1-4, 7-10; Hebrews 9&#58;27
In verse 8, David and all Israel played (praise) before God with all their might but in verse 10, the Lord was wrath with Uzzah and struck him dead. Having a good mind/intentions to serve and worship God is not enough. Our approach must be right before God. 
We cannot serve God acceptably outside of His prescription. That we are serving God is only good if we are serving Him according to His prescription. Uzzah lost his position, he died in disobedience, he missed heaven by trying to do a good thing in an unapproved way. 




Point-1&#58; The danger of unacceptable consultation without scriptures 

As true followers of Christ, we should not consult our head knowledge when we are to carry out a divine assignment but should seek the prescription of the Lord on how to go about the matter. Go back to the scriptures, seek the face of the Holy Spirit or, talk to Holy Spirit inspired leaders in the church. By all means, seek counsel and get it before you take the step.
