Point-2&#58; The purifying experience of Sanctification in kingdom citizens 
In dealing with other people, exhibit meaknes. 
Mathew 5&#58; 6
Do I have poverty of spirit? In Christian life, we are never stagnant or self-satisfied. We are to continue to hunger for a higher level of righteousness.

Luke 1&#58;72-75; Mathew 5&#58;8
The Lord will deliver me from all my enemies (man and circumstances) so that I can serve Him without fear of them.
The components of our salvation are beyond salvation (I no longer steal, fight, cheat people, etc). Our salvation is more than these and Christ wants us to reach higher levels such as possession of inner purity of purpose, intentions, etc.

Point-3&#58; The pleasant endurance while suffering for the King's cause
There is necessity for endurance for anyone that chooses to serve the King of kings. That is only we can defend and propagate the King's cause irrespective of obstacles on the way. We do not endure with murmuring or bitterness but pleasantly.

Mathew 5&#58;10; 2 Timothy 3&#58;12-13
The persecution is because of our determination to remain righteous for the King's cause. There are many that are not ready to pay the price for righteousness. Anyone that dares to live a righteous (godly) life in a world controlled by Satan will be targeted and persecuted.

Acts 14&#58;22; Mathew 5&#58;11; Luke 6&#58;22-23,26; Mathew 5&#58;12
To be despised and lied against because of our righteous stand and we continue to live in the manner for which we are reviled, is a mark of our true righteousness. If however we compromise and people now love us, it is utter failure and Christ says, "woe unto us". If we refuse to compromise however, our rewards will be great on this earth and also in heaven.
