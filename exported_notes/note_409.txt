Deuteronomy 20&#58;4; 2&#58;4; 20&#58;3
The commandments that Israelites that have built a house should not go to war does not apply to new testament children of God. The children of Israel were fighting physical enemies but we fight spiritual enemies of God and ourselves and we are commanded, whether we built new houses or not, to fight all our enemy and the enemies of God. We need to understand when to fight, where to fight and what to fight.
For Israel, God gave the concession that the Israelites that were afraid should not go to battle but in the new testament, all of God's children are required to fight our enemy and the enemy of God. 
"Fear Not" is commanded in the new testament to the saints.
