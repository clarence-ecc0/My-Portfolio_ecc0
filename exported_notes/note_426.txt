Christ was obviously inconvenienced being visited at night and yet, Christ attended to him. As ministers, we should not consider it inconvenient when we are approached particularly for spirit matters at odd times. We should learn from the self-sacrifice of Christ and do likewise.

For the great escape from the coming great tribulation, we must watch and pray ALWAYS

We should lay down our life (time, money, gift, etc.) for our brethren so that they have relief and salvation. This is the practical loving nature of God, the evidence of our regeneration. 

Point -1&#58; The present escape and transformation at regeneration 

John 5&#58;24; 1 John 3&#58;14-16; 2 Peter 1&#58;3-4; Hebrews 7&#58;3-4
If we have passed from death unto life, we should know because the Spirit of God should be us witness that we are forgiven our sins. 
Our manifestations of righteousness at work place and everywhere shows that we have received all things that pertains to life and godliness. Are the things we do unto glory and virtue? If not, we do not have the born-again experience yet. Have you escaped from the corruption in the world, have you abandoned them? If yes, you are born-again already otherwise, you need to visit Calvary again. 

1 Corinthians 10&#58;12-14
If temptation comes to a man and he is unable to escape, it is not that there is no way to escape but because the man was looking for the way of escape but was busy looking at the tempter air temptress. 
What stops men from escaping from temptations is because the temptation has become an idol (e.g., money, beautiful woman, position in office, knowledge, etc.). We are enjoined to flee from every form of idolatry so that it does not cut us from our way of escape from the tempter when he comes tomorrow. I will flee from every solicitation to evil.
