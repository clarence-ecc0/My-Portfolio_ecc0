Repentance is the foundation to entering heaven. This comes before all things. It is the key that opens the strait gate for us so that we can enter and then walk on the narrow way. 


TOPIC&#58; THE SUPERNATURAL IMPARTATION OF VISION FOR FRUITFULNESS 

Proverb 29&#58;18; Lamentations 2&#58;9,14; Habakkuk 2&#58;2-4; Genesis 15&#58;1-6; Mark 4&#58;20.
Where there is no vision, the people perish. When we receive vision from the Lord, we should write it down. Paul had a direction as a Pharisees, Moses had a direction as a herdsman, the 12 disciples had directions for their lives (fishermen, tax collector, etc). They encountered God

Point-1&#58; Internalizing the vision and the promise of 30-fold fruitfulness 

Daniel 7&#58;1,28; Acts 26&#58; 16-19; Mark 4&#58;14,20.
Daniel did 4 things with the vision&#58; wrote it down, shared it, meditated on it (cogitation) and, kept it in his heart.
When you receive a heavenly vision, do not be disobedient to it. 

Seven things we must do when we receive visions.
i. Internalize the vision. Understand it and let it get to your heart

ii. Investigate the vision. Moses investigated the vision of the burning bush. 

iii. Interpret the vision- try to understand it so that you can respond appropriately. Paul saw the man of Macedonia and understood what it was for.

iv. Inform others about the vision. Write it down that 'THEY' may run that hear it 

v. Intercede (pray) about it because it may be beyond your personal strength. 

vi. Initiate action on the basis of the vision. Do something about the vision.

vii. Inculcate the vision in the hearts of others. The GS inculcated the vision for holiness in others and today, we have the DLBC. Cornelius gathered his family and friends to share in the vision he received. 

Point-2&#58; Investing the valuables in the process of sixty-fold fruitfulness 

Mathew 13&#58;8; Acts 18&#58;9-11; Proverbs 29&#58;18
Fruitfulness in life and ministry takes time for the fruits to start coming in. The resources God has given us should be invested in the work so that we can achieve 60-fold fruitfulness. What you did not invest cannot bear fruits, produce profits.
The apostles did not bear multiple fruits at the beginning but as they progressed, they could pray and many will be saved

The 7 things we must do for fruitfulness 
i. Travail with fervency. Christianity is not for laziness. Isaiah 54&#58;11

ii. Team up with focus. Ecclesiastes 4&#58;9-
     Work with others for the work of God

iii. Treat the fruitless. As we are here in the church, some hearts may be on the roadside, seeing people that got up from their seats, looking at people's dress. Some hearts have rejected what they are hearing (stony hearts). 

iv. Trim the fruitful. Help others to eliminate the distractions of life so that they can bear more fruits.

v. Transform the feeble. 1 Samuel 22&#58;1; 2 Samuel 23&#58;8-12. David transformed ordinary people that were in debt, sons of belial and they became his mighty men. 

vi. Train the faithful. They will then be equipped so that they can lead others.

vii. Treasure and thirst for personal fruitfulness. Genesis 30&#58;1, 22


Point-3&#58; Implementing with virtue and perseverance in the 100-fold fruitfulness 

Mark 4&#58;14, 20; Genesis 26&#58;12
Fruit-bearing is progressive both in life and ministry. As we continue to work on ourselves, persevering in all circumstances, we will end up earning more for our labor.
The disciples started by preaching to a few them they got 3000 people at once, then 5000 men (not counting women, and then uncountable.
Guide on vision settings in order to achieve the goal 

i. Envision it and you will experience it

ii. Conceive it (vision) before you carry it

iii. Pay the price before you can possess it (John 4&#58;36)

iv. Persevere before we produce it (Ecclesiastes 11&#58;4,6)

v. Sweat in the harvest field before we can see the 100-fold
Psalms 126&#58;5-6
