Point-2&#58; The condemnation of faithless sign-seekers.

Psalm145&#58;8;Mark 8&#58;10-14; Acts 2&#58;22; John &#58;__
The Pharisees have lots of signs from Christ already to convince them that He came from God. To ask Him for more signs is mere hypocrisy. The Pharisees and even Herod requests that Christ should do some miracles for them was mere hypocrisy and pride, things that satisfy the carnal mind. 

Christ commanded His disciples not to satisfy sinners' carnal craving for signs & miracles and, not to satisfy their own desire also to do signs & wonders to win admiration. Such desires will generate pride in the disciples and thus, a trap from Satan). Signs and wonders are supposed to lead people to Christ in whose name they were done, not to glorify the disciples, the vehicle that Christ used to do them. It will be a trap for the disciples the same way the Pharisees used it as a trap for Christ (the Bible says the Pharisees made the request to tempt Christ, not to believe on him). The same Pharisees that wanted a sign before they could believe Christ saw the sign of raising Lazarus from the dead and they decided to kill him.
