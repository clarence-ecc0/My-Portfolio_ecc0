Point-2&#58; Conversion and cleansing for God's glory
After seeing the glory of God, there will be cleansing that prepares you for His task.

Malachi 3&#58;3; Mathew 3&#58;11-12
Many times, we think we are honoring Jesus and praising Him, but we never think or talk about repentance. Christ came for repentance, He said if we do not repent, we will likewise perish; even after going to heaven, He told the church at Ephesus to repent. There can't be rejoicing without repentance. 

If you are baptized by the Holy Ghost, there will be evidence (the fire/passion/zeal) for the Lord's work. When the baptism is there, coldness will be driven away. 
A man who has the fire of the Holy Ghost doesn't need anyone to drive him to evangelize.

The fire of the Holy Ghost will burn away every chaff in my life (sickness, laziness, procrastination, etc.)

Isaiah 6&#58;8 is a personal question for me. The old Isaiah is gone and I am now the Isaiah of today.
A new chapter of my life is being written, and I am moving to a new level.
