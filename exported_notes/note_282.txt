Deuteronomy chapters 5 and 6
Exodus 19&#58;161
Israel entered into agreement with the Lord, to keep them and prosper them but they renege on the agreement. We also are in a covenant with the Lord through the sacrifice of Christ. Will we behave like Israel, the church in the wilderness? God forbid!

The ten commandments were written on two tables of stone. The first four commandments were written on the first table and they speak to man's duty to God. The remaining six commandments were written on the second stone and they speak to the duty of man to his neighbor, parents, etc. See  Deuteronomy 5&#58;16-21
The whole ten commandments were summarized into two laws (Mathew 22&#58; ) by our Lord Jesus namely, thou shalt love the Lord thy God and, thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.


Sunday message

TOPIC&#58; THE POSSIBILITIES OF FAITH , GREAT FAITH AND GROWING FAITH 

Ephesians 2&#58;8-10; Mathew 15&#58;28; 2 Thessalonians 1&#58;3
There is faith, great faith and growing faith. If God has promised, we do not cast doubt on His promise. We cannot say, even if God does not fulfill His word, He is still God. If I Should say that, then I am not sure it is God's promise. 

Point-1&#58; Preventing hardness of heart with faith at the foundation 

Jeremiah 32&#58;17, 17; Genesis 18&#58;14; Zechariah 7&#58;12
The Lord commanded that men that call upon His name should depart from iniquity. That is the foundation.

Mark 10&#58;5; 
When you carry the standard (God's word) and measure it with a faulty scale (philosophy of the world), the outcome will not be correct because the scale is a misfit. God's Word is forever established. 

Revelations 22&#58;18-19; Ezekiel 11&#58;19
There are people that have heard the word of God and have decided not to change. They have heard that repentance is required to enter God's kingdom. The pastor, because of the hardness of their heart, he no longer mentions repentance, restitutions, etc. 
That was exactly what Moses did for Israel but Christ came to condemn that act. Consider Mark 10&#58;5 & verse 9. A man that is desirous of seeing God cannot change the scriptures for anyone. Whether you are teaching the STS or preaching the main message. If you bend the scriptures, you are transacting away your name from God's book of life. 

Point-2&#58; Preserving holiness for heaven through faith in His faithfulness 

If it is possible for anyone to enter heaven without holiness, then God the Father wasted the life of Christ. Why? Because Christ came and died to show us how to be holy, in order to enter heaven, a task that has become unnecessary seeing unholy people can enter heaven. 

God is faithful. I must trust in His faithfulness to be able to reach heaven. 
The word of holiness is the major thing in God's church. Healing and deliverance and such likes are minor things. We must major in major things and minor in minor things. 

Acts 15&#58;9; Psalms 24&#58;3-4; Leviticus 20&#58;7; 19;2; Luke 1&#58;74-75
When we have faith in God, He purifies our heart because of that faith. 
We should not allow anyone (coming as a helper or an open antagonist) to take the gospel of holiness from us. You will speak the truth, live by it and depart in it. Leave any organization that wants you to compromise the standard of the Lord and this have your name out of God's book of life. That organization is your greatest enemy.

Hebrews 12&#58;10,14-17,28.
Christ is always with and in us and always cancels all fears out of us, out of our personality. 
God's kingdom cannot be moved (the standard cannot be altered for anyone). All we can do is to receive grace that will help us to serve Him acceptably because the standard can't be changed for us.. 

Point-3&#58; Possessing humility and honesty in the fellowship of His family 

James 4&#58;6-10; 1 Peter 2&#58;11-12; Micah 6&#58;8
In all things that we do, there must be humility and honesty in our heart.
