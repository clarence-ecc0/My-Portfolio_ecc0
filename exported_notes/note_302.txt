Point-1&#58; The persistent widow and God's promise to answer prayers 
The Lord wants us to learn from the persistence of the widow by continuing in prayer, waiting for answers to come. We must be persistent and ask in faith. 

Delays in answer to prayers is not to punish us but they are for our good. If the Lord through His omniscience knows what will happen in the land in 5 years time and how it will affect my request and thus delays granting my request until the affliction is passed, the delay is for my good. 
The Lord did not give the promised land all at once to Israel because He did not want the wild beasts to multiply against them. So thoughtful of our God.
Our prayers may not be answered the way we expected it. God is all knowing and will modify our request and grant unto us that which is best for us. Paul prayed for his infirmity to be removed but God knew that through it, he would be kept humble in order to remain in the kingdom. He gave him grace rather than remove the infirmity. It was for the good of Paul.
