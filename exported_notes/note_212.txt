Memory verse&#58; 

When a request comes to you that there is no precedent, do not just use your age or experience to answer it. As Moses did, take it to the Lord. Once God has given a verdict, the man of God has no choice but to release the portion. 

To preserve the inheritance given to the daughters of Zelophehad, the Lord commanded that they should marry within their tribe so that their inheritance does not go to another tribe. In a similar manner, beloved Christian sisters should marry within their Christian tribe so that their inheritance (their husband, their children) will not be lost to Satan but preserved in Christ. Our children are the heritage of the Lord, the fruit of the womb is His reward (our inheritance from God)

TOPIC&#58; 

Colossians 1&#58;12-14;
There is an abundance of inheritance for the saints and the #1 inheritance is redemption. Believers believe the entire scriptures including the promises of God for His children, the warnings of God, and the punishment for those that persist in their transgressions.

Sinners in church (no matter their material blessings) cannot be said to be fully enjoying the inheritance of the blessings of God that are reserved for the saints in Christ. For instance, they do not have access to God, to make requests and have an assurance that their prayers will be answered. 



Point-1&#58; The promise of a new inheritance 

Ezekiel 36&#58;11,25-27; Jeremiah 31&#58;31,33; Luke 1&#58;68-75; Acts 20&#58;28,32
We have here, promises of material blessings (verse 11) and spiritual blessings (verse 25). The spiritual promise of a new heart will help us to wage battle successfully against the attacks of lust, revenge to those that offended us, and the spirit of selfishness.
The new heart the Lord promises to give believers will come pre-loaded with God's laws written upon it and the power to obey the laws. 
The great enemies that hate us include Satan (and his demons), sin and the flesh.


Point-2&#58; The provision of a new inheritance 

Ephesians 2&#58;12-18
The scriptures here tells us that Christ abolished the ordinances of laws (in the scriptures) that put a separation between Jews and gentiles (us). If Christ said He did not come to abolish the law, how does that claim stand in the presence of what is spoken here?
The statement of Christ is still true. True, He didn't come to abolish the law, He came to fulfill the laws and those that have been fulfilled by him will be observed no longer (i.e., abolished).

Ephesians 3&#58;20-21; 5&#58;25-27
There is a power given to us when we accept Christ that move God to answer our prayers to Him. 

Philippians 2&#58;5; Colossians 3&#58;1-4; Hebrews 9&#58;15
We receive this when we come to Christ in humility, coming without any reputation of our own that we can claim ( our labors and our fruits, our personal ness, etc.). 


Point-3&#58; The partakers of the new inheritance 

Ultimately, the new inheritance is heaven although there are blessings here on earth for us 

The partakers of new inheritance are believers who have separated themselves from the evil in the world. Those that are living in sin, openly or secretly will be named among the partakers. Those that are partakers of other men's sins will not be partakers of the inheritance of the saints.
